## Conflicts

**CONFLICT 1: Romanization (not factual).** "Lee You Jin / Cha Gyeong Eun" (Soompi, Medcom, detik) vs
"Lee Yu-jin / Cha Kyung-eun" (StarNews English) vs "Lee Yoo-jin / Cha Kyung-eun" (Sports Kyunghyang English).
House form: Lee You Jin, Cha Gyeong Eun (see Names).

**CONFLICT 2: Lee's age.** 34 per Newsis [S10] and SBS [S6]; 35 per Tenasia [S15]. Born 1992 [S11][S16], so 34
in international age in October 2026 (Korean outlets now use international age since the June 2023 law);
Tenasia's 35 is likely the older counting or a slip. Print **34**.

**CONFLICT 3: StarNews English AI translation errors.** StarNews' English edition (machine translated)
misgenders both: it calls Lee "she/actress" and Cha "he" [S2][S3][S4][S5], and in S3 makes Kim Seung-yoon
"Lee Yu-jin's older sister". Korean outlets are clear that Lee is the son of Lee Hyo-jung [S6][S7][S11] and
that Kim Seung-yoon is **Cha's** sister [S9][S16]. Never use StarNews English for gender, family or titles.

**CONFLICT 4: Weekday in detikPop.** detikPop body says StarNews reported on "Rabu (6/10/2026)"; 6 Oct 2026 is a
**Tuesday**, and detik's own byline says "Selasa, 06 Okt 2026" [S19]. Use Tuesday.

**CONFLICT 5: "Early November".** A StarNews related-link headline says "early November" [S5]; every statement
and Lee's own letter say 14 November / mid-November [S2][S6][S20]. Use **14 November**.

**Trap (do not mix):** Song Ji-ho & Kim So-ri announced their October wedding on the same morning, and several
roundups (Sports Kyunghyang [S17], OSEN, StarNews English) cover both couples together. Their agency (Inyeon
Entertainment), the AALIA Studio pictorial, the "long relationship" wording and the handwritten letters on paper
belong to **Song & Kim**, not to Lee & Cha. Conversely, "private, family and close acquaintances", "14 November,
somewhere in Seoul", "met on his directorial debut", "dating since June / five months" and the pregnancy belong
to **Lee & Cha** only. Kim Jong-min's baby news in the same roundups is a third, unrelated story. Also keep Jang
Dong-yoon & Kim Seung-yoon's own wedding facts (church-style, small, October) out of this couple's wedding
sections.
