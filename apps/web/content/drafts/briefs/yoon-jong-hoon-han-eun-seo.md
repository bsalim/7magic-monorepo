Compiled 2026-10-08 (Thursday) for a background-only draft; to be completed on the wedding day.
Source markers [S#] refer to the Sources list at the end. Every printed fact carries at least two
independent markers. Independence notes:
- The Korea Times [S15] is an AI-assisted translation of a Hankook Ilbo report (its sister paper, which had
  the exclusive [S16]). S15 and S16 count as **one** source.
- Okezone [S21] and RCTI+ carry the same article (same reporter). Count once.
- Popbela [S23] says it relays OSEN. The OSEN article itself was not found, so Popbela is used only for leads.
- Sports Kyunghyang English [S17] says it builds on an OSEN report, but it adds its own career details; it
  is counted as independent of Ilgan, Xports, StarNews and Hankook, not of OSEN.

## The announcement

**Date:** **Monday, 7 September 2026** (KST), evening. See CONFLICT 3 for the "8 September" variant.

**Format** (two parts, plus the bride's post the next day):
1. A **handwritten letter** from Yoon on his fan cafe [S1][S4][S13][S15]. Full text reproduced in Xports [S4];
   key lines also quoted by Pickcon [S14].
2. Confirmation from **YK Media Plus**. Wording as quoted: "윤종훈이 한은서와 오는 11월 결혼한다" (to Ilgan
   Sports) [S1]; "윤종훈과 한은서가 오는 11월 1일 서울 강남 모처에서 결혼식을 올린다" [S10][S11].
3. Han's own post, Tuesday 8 September [S10][S18].

**Yoon's letter, short paraphrase** (all lines in [S4]; second source as marked):
- Opens "안녕하세요. 윤종훈입니다." and asks how fans got through a very hot August [S4][S14].
- He took up the pen after thinking hard about how to share this news; he writes to fans at the end of
  every month, and this time it took longer [S4][S14].
- "저에게 귀중한 인연이 생겼고, 이제 그 사람과 평생을 함께 걸어가려 합니다." After caring for and understanding
  each other and putting in effort, they begin anew with marriage [S4][S11][S14].
- He wanted fans to hear first [S4][S13].
- He will live more responsibly as a husband and as the actor Yoon Jong-hoon [S4][S14][S15]; will keep
  striving to be a good actor and a good person [S1][S4].
- Asks fans to laugh with them and bless them; feels excitement and weight [S4][S14].
- Will keep showing up at the end of every month to share life stories [S4][S14].

**Han's post, short paraphrase** [S10][S18]:
- Yoon is someone who has stood by her for a long time, consistently, with warm love.
- As partners for life they will cherish and love each other more and walk together.
- Thanks everyone for blessing their new beginning; they will keep trying to be better people to each
  other and to those around them.

## Conflicts

**CONFLICT 1: Yoon's debut (SETTLED by framing).** Hankook/Korea Times and Ilgan: debut 2006, stage play
*Moonlight Trunk* [S1][S3][S15]. Sports Kyunghyang, TV Report, Herald, Xports, Soompi: debut 2013, tvN
*Monstar* [S4][S9][S12][S17][S20]. Both are true of different media. Print "stage 2006, TV drama debut 2013".

**CONFLICT 2: Venue wording (SETTLED: Gangnam).** "서울 강남 모처" [S4][S10][S11][S22] vs "서울 모처" [S1][S3][S8][S19].
Compatible; the narrower form is quoted from the agency by two Korean outlets [S10][S11].

**CONFLICT 3: Agency statement date (SETTLED: 7 September).** Herald writes that the agency "8일 밝혔다" [S12];
Viu says the agency confirmed on 8 September and the letter came on the 7th [S22]. Ilgan [S1], StarNews [S6],
TV Report [S10] and Insight [S11] carry the agency statement on 7 September, and Ilgan's [공식] is timestamped
2026-09-07 21:39. Herald was reporting the next morning.

**CONFLICT 4: Agency name.** "YK Media Plus" / "YK미디어플러스" everywhere [S1][S15][S17]; "YK Mediaplus"
[S20][S21] (spacing only); Popbela "YK Media Keyeast Entertainment" [S23] (error, Keyeast is a different company).

**CONFLICT 5: English title of 기쁜 우리 좋은 날.** *Our Happy Days* [S15][S20][S22]; *Our Joyful, Fine Day*
[S17]; *Glad Our Good Day* (Ilgan machine text) [S3]; *Happy Our Home* [S13]. Use *Our Happy Days* with the
Hangul. Start month also conflicts (March per [S17], February per [S24]); not printed.

**CONFLICT 6: Han's doctorate.** "Completed doctoral coursework" (박사 과정 수료) [S7][S11][S15] vs Xports
"completed bachelor's, master's and doctoral courses" [S5]. Print the coursework wording; never "Dr." or "PhD".

**CONFLICT 7: Romanization of the Return role.** "Seo Jun-hee" [S11] vs "Seo Joon-hee" [S25]. Same character
(서준희). Use "Seo Jun-hee".

## Trap (not a conflict, easy to get wrong)

Several weddings were announced in the same days and are often bundled in one article [S19]:
- **Baek Jin-hee** announced on 7 Sep 2026, the same evening, a marriage to a non-celebrity in Canada, with a
  **private ceremony in Seoul in January 2027**. The "private ceremony" language belongs to her.
- **Kwak Si-yang & Yoo Ji-ae** (28 Nov) and **Ji Ye-eun & Vata** (12 Dec) appear in the same round-ups.
- **Lee You-jin & Cha Gyeong-eun** (14 Nov, private, with a pregnancy announcement) were announced on 6 Oct.
None of these details apply to Yoon & Han.
