# Research brief: jadwal pernikahan artis, Oktober 2026 sampai Januari 2027

Compiled 2026-10-08 (Thursday) for the roundup article published 2026-10-09:
`apps/web/content/articles/jadwal-pernikahan-artis-2026-2027.md` (ID) and
`apps/web/content/articles/en/jadwal-pernikahan-artis-2026-2027.md` (EN).
Started from `docs/marketing/celebrity-wedding-watchlist.md` (researched 2026-10-07);
every entry was re-checked on 2026-10-08 for postponements, changes and new dates.

Rules used:

- A fact is printed only with two independent sources. Outlets in the same media group
  count as one (detik network = detikHOT, detikPop, Wolipop; KLY = KapanLagi, Liputan6;
  MNC = iNews, Okezone, RCTI+; IDN = IDN Times, Popbela). An Indonesian outlet that only
  translates Soompi is not independent of Soompi, so Korean-wedding facts are paired as
  one Korean/English outlet plus one Indonesian outlet, or two Korean outlets.
- "Schedule" = date or month announced by the couple, family or agency (CONFIRMED in the
  watch list scale). "Belum ada tanggal resmi" = engaged, wedding expected, no official day.
- Private partners are never named, here or in the article (the repo is public).
- Weekdays checked with Python `datetime`: 23 Oct 2026 Fri, 30 Oct Fri, 1 Nov Sun,
  3 Nov Tue, 7 Nov Sat, 14 Nov Sat, 28 Nov Sat, 12 Dec Sat, 24 Dec Thu, 25 Dec Fri,
  23 Jan 2027 Sat, 26 Sep 2026 Sat.
