Compiled 2026-10-08 (Thursday). Status: **wedding not yet held.** No date, venue,
city or form of ceremony has been announced as of this brief. This is background
only; the article is completed on the wedding day.

Rule used throughout: a fact is listed in the main sections only if at least two
independent outlets (or one outlet plus the couple's own post as quoted) carry it.

## Conflicts

1. **Post date of the pre-wedding photos: 2 Oct vs 3 Oct.**
   - Liputan6 and KapanLagi say Gege posted on **Friday 2 Oct 2026** ("Jumat (2/10/2026)"), and both published that day (Liputan6 14:34 WIB, KapanLagi 15:16 WIB). [S4][S6] The weekday is correct.
   - detik says "dilihat di Instagram, Sabtu (3/10/2026)" and published 3 Oct 20:06 WIB. [S9] That is the day detik viewed it, not necessarily the post day.
   - The watch list (2026-10-07) says "posted 3 Oct 2026". KLY's publish timestamps show the post existed by 14:34 WIB on 2 Oct, so **3 Oct is wrong** as a post date.
   - The exact day rests on one group (KLY). The article says "awal Oktober 2026".
2. **KapanLagi gallery resurfaced with a new date.** The proposal gallery [S11] was first published 24 Mar 2026 but now shows "Diperbarui 02 Oktober 2026". Don't cite it for anything dated October. It is not in the article's references.
3. **Type of *Bestie*.** Wolipop calls it an FTV (2022) [S8]; Liputan6 a web series 2022 to 2023 [S4]; Wikipedia a web series (2022). Printed as "serial web *Bestie* (2022)".
4. **Proposal location: Tuileries vs Eiffel Tower.** A low-quality aggregator (viv.co.id, not the VIVA group) headlines "di depan Menara Eiffel". Every mainstream group that names the place says Tuileries Garden. [S3][S4][S10] Use Tuileries.
