## Conflicts

**CONFLICT 1: Announcement date (SETTLED: Tuesday 6 October 2026).**
- Version A: 6 Oct 2026. RRI ("Selasa, 6 Oktober 2026") [S3]; Chosun/Sports Chosun ("on the 6th",
  published 2026-10-06) [S1]; Soompi ("On October 6", published 2026-10-06) [S2]; every Korean outlet ("6일",
  articles dated 2026-10-06) [S5][S6][S8][S26].
- Version B: SINDOnews: "Agensi Song Ji Ho, Inyeon Entertainment, mengumumkan kabar tersebut pada
  Selasa, 10 Juni 2026" [S4].
- Why B is wrong: (a) 10 June 2026 was a **Wednesday**, so "Selasa, 10 Juni 2026" contradicts itself;
  6 Oct 2026 **is** a Tuesday. (b) SINDOnews' own article is dated "Selasa, 06 Oktober 2026 – 16:01 WIB"
  and cites Soompi, which says October 6. (c) The wedding itself is in October, so a June announcement
  would not say "this month". The likely cause is "10/6" (month/day, US style) read as day/month.

**CONFLICT 2: Romanization.** Agency forms are "SONG JI HO" and "Kim So Ri" [S21][S22]; media vary
between "Song Ji-ho"/"Song Jiho"/"Song Ji Ho" and "Kim So-ri"/"Kim So Ri" [S1][S2][S15]. Not a factual
conflict; choose one house form.

**CONFLICT 3: *Made in Korea* release year.** Korean outlets: March **this year** (2026)
[S1][S6][S7][S8][S27]. StarNews English: "In March **last year**" [S16]. Go with **March 2026**; StarNews
English is a translation slip.

**CONFLICT 4: Pictorial credit.** Almost all outlets credit AALIA Studio [S1][S5][S6][S9][S15][S26].
Variants: Cineplay "Alia Studio" [S17]; StarNews English "Song Ji-ho's agency" [S16]; NewDaily "ⓒ연합뉴스" [S8];
SBS Star image alt text "Getty Images Korea" against its own body credit "AALIA Studio" [S15]. Use **AALIA Studio**.

**CONFLICT 5: Kim So-ri's debut year (SETTLED: 2016).** Daily Economy's March 2026 article, built on a
b.able company release, says "2009년 데뷔한 김소리" [S27]. Every announcement outlet says 2016, most naming
CoCoSoRi [S1][S5][S6][S8][S26][S35][S38]. The namesake Kim So-ri (born 1985) debuted in July 2009 [S40], so
"2009" is almost certainly a mix-up with her. Print **2016**; never 2009.

**CONFLICT 6: *Mix Nine* broadcaster (SETTLED: JTBC).** Sports Seoul calls it "Mnet '믹스나인'" [S7]. Cineplay
("JTBC's 'Mix Nine'") [S17], Money Today ("JTBC 서바이벌 '믹스나인'") [S34] and Wikipedia ("broadcast on JTBC") [S39] say **JTBC**. Sports Seoul confused it with *Somebody 2*, which *is* Mnet.

**Trap (not a conflict, easy to get wrong):** the "private ceremony with family and close friends" line
and the "14 November, somewhere in Seoul" date belong to **Lee Yu-jin & Cha Kyung-eun**, announced about
two hours later the same day [S5][S11][S16]. So do "met on his directorial debut", "dating since June /
five months", and the pregnancy news [S11][S16]. None of it applies to Song & Kim. StarNews' headline
"same-day weddings" means same-day **announcements**. Likewise the Newsen/Tenasia "선업튀 부부" angle is about
Song's *on-screen* partner Seo Hye-won, who is married to someone else and expecting [S9][S10].
