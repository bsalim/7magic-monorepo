said the akad and reception will both be in **December 2026**, date to follow. No
date, venue or adat has been announced as of this brief. This is background only;
the article is completed on the wedding day.

Rule used throughout: a fact is listed in the main sections only if at least two
independent outlets (or one outlet plus the couple's or family's own post or
statement as quoted) carry it. Outlets from the same media group are counted as ONE
source:
