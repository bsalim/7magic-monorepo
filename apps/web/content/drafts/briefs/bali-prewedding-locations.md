# Research brief: Unique pre-wedding photoshoot locations in Bali

Compiled 2026-10-07 for an Indonesian article and its English version. Every page cited below was read on
**2026-10-07** unless a line gives a different date. Source markers [S#] refer to the Sources list at the end.
Numbers are grouped by area (S1–S29 general rules, S100–S141 Tabanan/Badung/Jembrana/Buleleng highlands,
S200–S231 Gianyar/Bangli/Buleleng, S300–S337 Karangasem/Klungkung), so there are gaps.

**How to read this brief**

- Ordinary facts carry at least two independent markers, and Wikipedia counts as at most one of them.
- **Fees, permit rules, hours and levies** are business data. They are quoted only from an official page, marked
  **(official)**, or from at least two independent outlets citing the site manager, marked **(two outlets citing
  the manager)**. Quote them exactly as printed, with the date. **Do not round, convert or estimate.**
- "Not confirmed" means no qualifying source answered the question. Write it that way in the article ("tanyakan
  ke pengelola" / "check with the site manager") and never fill the gap with a guess.
- **Big picture for the article:** only **four** locations have a sourced prewedding fee (Kelingking, Kertha
  Gosa, Taman Soekasada Ujung, Danau Tamblingan). Most Bali sites publish an entrance ticket but no prewedding
  tariff; photographers negotiate on the spot. The honest framing is "confirm the shoot fee with the pengelola
  before the day".
- **Drone fees:** the only one confirmed is Jatiluwih's IDR 300.000 (official) [S100]. Penglipuran bans drones
  [S206][S207]. Everything else is not confirmed.

## Overview: general rules

### Bali tourist levy (Pungutan bagi Wisatawan Asing)

- **Amount:** Rp150.000 per foreign tourist, paid electronically. Set by Perda Provinsi Bali No. 6 Tahun 2023
  Pasal 5(3) and kept by Perda No. 2 Tahun 2025 Pasal 5(2), which requires non-cash payment (official) [S4][S5].
  The Love Bali site still shows IDR 150,000 (official) [S1].
- **Who pays:** "Wisatawan Asing", meaning anyone from outside Indonesia who is not an Indonesian citizen and is
  touring Bali (official, Perda 6/2023 Pasal 1 angka 9) [S4].
  - **Indonesian couples do not pay.** This follows from the definition [S4].
- **Legal basis now:**
  - Perda 6/2023, signed 8 Aug 2023, in force 14 Feb 2024 [S4].
  - Amended by Perda 2/2025, signed 20 Jun 2025, in force 1 Jul 2025 [S5].
  - Payment procedure: Pergub 2/2024, as amended by Pergub 25/2025 (official) [S1][S6].
- **When to pay:** "sebelum atau selama berwisata di Bali" (official, Perda 2/2025 Pasal 5(3)) [S5].
  - Love Bali encourages paying before arrival, via lovebali.baliprov.go.id or the Love Bali app [S1][S3].
  - Payment is non-cash and non-refundable, and a digital voucher is issued (official, Love Bali Terms) [S3].
- **Exempt (official, Perda 2/2025 Pasal 4A(2)) [S5]; the same list is in Pergub 2/2024 Pasal 6 [S6]:**
  - crew on a means of transport
  - KITAS or KITAP holders
  - diplomatic and service visa holders
  - family-unification visa holders
  - student visa holders
  - golden visa holders
  - "pemegang jenis visa lainnya" (other visa types)
  - Antara reported the same seven categories on 8 Feb 2024, citing Dinas Pariwisata Bali [S7].
- **Exemption procedure:** golden-visa and "other visa" holders apply through Love Bali at least 5 days before
  arrival; a later application means the visitor pays (official, Pergub 2/2024 Pasal 7 and Love Bali Terms)
  [S6][S2][S3]. Print "apply via Love Bali at least 5 days before arrival".
- **Sanctions:** foreign tourists who do not pay face an oral warning, a written warning and/or refusal of
  service at tourist attractions (official, Perda 2/2025 Pasal 16A) [S5][S9].
- **Second levy in Nusa Penida (Klungkung) from 1 Sep 2026, on top of the provincial levy (official, Perda Kab.
  Klungkung No. 4 Tahun 2026):**
  - Area levy for foreign visitors to Nusa Penida or Lembongan–Ceningan: Rp25.000 per adult, Rp15.000 per child
    aged 4–13 [S323b][S324][S331].
  - **Plus** a levy at each attraction (DTW) of Rp25.000 per adult and Rp15.000 per child, paid by foreign
    **and** domestic visitors [S323b][S324][S331].
- **Kintamani area levy (Pemkab Bangli)** is collected at posts around Penelokan and the Batur caldera. Whether it
  applies to a Bukit Pinggan visit is **not confirmed**; see that location [S200][S201].

### Code of conduct: Surat Edaran Gubernur Bali No. 07 Tahun 2025 (current successor to SE 4/2023)

- **This is the current circular.** SE 07/2025, "Tatanan Baru bagi Wisatawan Asing selama berada di Bali", was
  signed by Gubernur Wayan Koster on 21 Mar 2025 and took effect on issuance (official) [S9][S10].
  - It refines and replaces SE 4/2023 [S10][S11].
  - No newer circular or Perda was found as of 2026-10-07.
  - It addresses foreign tourists. Indonesian couples should still follow the same temple rules (see Temple
    etiquette).
- **Obligations that matter for a shoot (official) [S9]; corroborated by [S10][S11]:**
  - respect the sanctity of temples, pratima and religious symbols, and customs during ceremonies
  - dress modestly at sacred areas, tourist attractions and public places
  - behave respectfully
  - pay the levy
  - be accompanied by a licensed tour guide at tourist attractions
  - follow each attraction's own rules
- **Prohibitions (official) [S9]; corroborated by [S10][S11]:**
  - **Inner courts.** No entering the Utamaning Mandala and Madyaning Mandala (inner and middle courts), except to
    pray in Balinese or prayer dress, and only when not menstruating.
  - **Sacred trees.** No climbing trees regarded as sacred.
  - **Sacred buildings and photos.** No climbing sacred buildings. No "indecent/inappropriate photos, including
    nudity" ("berfoto dengan pakaian tidak sopan/tanpa pakaian" [S10]). For prewedding wardrobes, this is the
    load-bearing line.
  - **Pollution.** No polluting lakes, springs, rivers or seas, and no single-use plastics.
  - **Work without papers.** No working or doing business without official documents. This is relevant to foreign
    photographers flown in, but the circular says nothing specific about photography; do not extrapolate.
- **Reporting:** violations can be reported to the "Siaga" WhatsApp line +62 812-8759-0999. Satpol PP Bali
  enforces (official) [S9].
- **SE 7/2025 does not mention drones** (checked in the official text) [S9].

### Drones

- **National basis:** Permenhub PM 37/2020, signed 2 Jun 2020, promulgated 8 Jun 2020, status "Berlaku"
  (official text read on JDIH Kemenhub) [S12a][S12][S13].
- **Airports:** flying in controlled airspace or inside an airport's KKOP (airport safety zone) needs approval from
  the Direktur Jenderal Perhubungan Udara (official) [S12a][S13].
  - Ngurah Rai's KKOP covers much of the Bukit and Kuta area. This is general knowledge, not sourced; do not
    print a radius.
- **Height (settled, official):** in uncontrolled airspace, up to "400 feet (120 m) tanpa persetujuan Direktur
  Jenderal"; above that needs approval (PM 37/2020 Lampiran 2.1.1.2) [S12a][S13]. RRI's 150 m [S12] is wrong.
- **Commercial and photography use (official, PM 37/2020 Lampiran) [S12a]:**
  - A drone under 55 lbs used "untuk kepentingan komersial harus mendapatkan penilaian safety assessment dari
    Direktur Jenderal" (3.2.a.3); non-hobby use must meet CASR Part 107 (3.2.a.1).
  - For "pemotretan atau pemfilman, operator harus memiliki surat persetujuan dari institusi/pihak yang
    berwenang" (4.13b); the application attaches a letter from the competent institution and/or the owner of the
    object being filmed.
  - Camera drones may not operate within 500 m of the outer edge of a prohibited or restricted area (3.13).
  - Whether a paid prewedding shoot counts as "komersial" is not stated; do not assert it.
- **Temples:** no province-wide ban on drones over temples was found. Rules are site by site:
  - Penglipuran: banned since March 2024 [S206][S207].
  - Jatiluwih: allowed with prior authorisation, IDR 300.000 (official) [S100].
- **Kites near Ngurah Rai:** Perda Bali 9/2000 limits kites near the airport [S14]. It covers kites, not drones;
  do not apply it to drones.
- **Nyepi:** whether drones are explicitly banned on Nyepi is not confirmed. Under the Catur Brata (see Nyepi)
  nobody should be outdoors shooting anyway.

### Weather (BMKG)

- **Rainy season 2026/27 onset:** part of Bali in November 2026, most of Bali in December 2026 (official, BMKG
  press release 22 Sep 2026) [S15].
- **Rainy season 2026/27 peak:** January 2027 in central Bali, February 2027 in most of Bali (official) [S15].
- **National picture:** onset is later than normal across 61.08% of Indonesia's area. A "very strong" El Niño is
  under way, and El Niño conditions are expected to persist into early 2027 (official) [S15][S16].
- **Dry season 2026:** BMKG forecast (10 Jun 2026) that Bali's dry-season peak would be August 2026 (official)
  [S17].
- No BMKG wording for a generic Bali "dry season April–October" was found. Do not print that line. Use the
  2026/27 forecast above.
- **Site-specific seasons:**
  - Savana Tianyar is yellow-brown in the dry season and green in the rains [S312b][S309].
  - Jatiluwih's crop stages follow its own calendar (official) [S102].

### Nyepi 2027

- **Date:** Senin, 8 Maret 2027, Hari Suci Nyepi Tahun Baru Saka 1949 (official, SKB 3 Menteri on national
  holidays 2027, published 15 Sep 2026) [S18].
- **Duration:** 24 hours, 06:00 to 06:00 WITA the next day [S21][S22].
- **Tourists** must stay at their accommodation, and the Catur Brata Penyepian applies [S21][S22]:
  - amati geni: no fire or lights
  - amati karya: no work
  - amati lelungan: no travel
  - amati lelanguan: no entertainment
- **Airport precedent:** in 2026 Ngurah Rai stopped regular commercial flights from 19 Mar 06:00 to 20 Mar 06:00
  WITA under NOTAM A0096 [S19][S20]. Ferries such as Ketapang–Gilimanuk and Padangbai–Lembar stopped too [S20].
- **The 2027 airport closure is not announced yet.** Write "expected to follow the usual 24-hour closure; check
  closer to the date".
- **For shoot planning:** do not schedule shoots on 8 Mar 2027. The nature parks and Penglipuran close (see
  single-source and location notes).

### Temple and sacred-site etiquette (applies to every temple-adjacent location below)

- **Dress:**
  - Wear a kamen/sarong.
    - At Besakih, kamen and udeng are "pakaian wajib bagi wisatawan" [S24].
    - At Pura Ulun Danu Beratan, a kamen is required; a selendang may also be worn [S25].
    - Generally, a sarong below the knee with shoulders covered [S26].
  - SE 7/2025 requires modest dress at sacred areas (official) [S9].
- **Menstruation:** menstruating women do not enter the temple. The official rule is SE 7/2025 for the inner and
  middle courts [S9]. Also reported for Ulun Danu Beratan [S25] and for temples generally [S26]. The Karangasem
  official portal says menstruating women may not climb (naik) to Lempuyang [S336].
- **No climbing** shrines, sacred buildings or sacred trees (official) [S9][S10][S11]. MDA Tabanan asked desa adat
  to post prohibition signs in three languages after a tourist climbed a sacred banyan (2022) [S29].
- **Ceremonies close sites:**
  - Pura Luhur Lempuyang closes to tourists for every pujawali (piodalan), for example 23–27 Apr 2025, per the
    Bendesa Adat [S27].
  - Gunung Agung climbing closed 1 Oct–30 Nov 2024 for ceremonies [S28].
  - Advise couples to check the temple's ceremony calendar with the pengelola when booking.
- **No guide-less entry into sacred areas:** Jatiluwih's own rules say "Forbidden to enter the sacred area or
  temple without a guide" (official) [S100].

## Locations

Fifteen locations across eight regencies. Types:

- **Rice terraces:** Jatiluwih.
- **Waterfalls:** Sekumpul, Tukad Cepung.
- **Beaches and cliffs:** Melasti, Kelingking.
- **Lakes and highlands:** Tamblingan, Bukit Pinggan.
- **Forest and gardens:** Kebun Raya Bali, Penglipuran bamboo forest.
- **Ridge:** Campuhan.
- **Heritage and palaces:** Taman Soekasada Ujung, Tirta Gangga, Kertha Gosa, Palasari.
- **Savanna:** Savana Tianyar.
- **Well-known spots with a fresh angle:** Jatiluwih (time the shoot to the rice calendar), Kelingking (top only,
  frame out the lift structure), Penglipuran (bamboo forest rather than the main street).

### 1. Jatiluwih (DTW Jatiluwih). Desa Jatiluwih, Kecamatan Penebel, Kabupaten Tabanan. Known spot, fresh angle

- **Manager:** Badan Pengelola Daya Tarik Wisata Jatiluwih (official) [S100].
- **Distance:** 26 km north of Tabanan city, about 45 min by car; about 40 km from Denpasar, a 1.5–2 hour drive;
  700 m above sea level (official) [S101].
- **The shot:** terraces that follow the contours of the land, with Gunung Batukaru behind (official) [S100][S101].
- **Distinctive:**
  - Part of the UNESCO World Heritage "Cultural Landscape of Bali Province: the Subak System…", inscribed 2012
    [S103][S105].
  - UN Tourism named it one of the Best Tourism Villages in 2024 [S104].
- **Fresh angle: time the shoot to the rice stage.** The manager publishes a crop calendar (official) [S102]:
  - **Green ("sea of lush greenery"):** April and October.
  - **Golden ("golden paddies"):** June and November.
  - **Harvest:** July, and harvest is completed in December.
  - **Ploughing and planting:** August, which is also "the highest guest visits" (manager's statement).
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.** The manager's site lists none [S100].
- **Drones (official):** "Flying drones within the Jatiluwih area is permitted only with prior authorization and
  will incur an additional charge of IDR 300,000" (homepage modified 2026-09-27) [S100].
- **Entrance tickets (official):** foreign adult IDR 75.000, foreign child IDR 50.000; domestic adult IDR 25.000,
  domestic child IDR 15.000 [S100].
  - Effective 1 Apr 2025, per operational manager I Ketut Purna [S106][S107].
- **Booking (official):** "bookings must be made at least 2 days in advance to ensure the availability of
  activities" [S100].
- **Rules (official) [S100]:**
  - Every visitor needs a ticket.
  - No vehicles on the trekking routes.
  - No picking rice, fruit or plants.
  - Walk only on the paths provided.
  - No entering sacred areas or temples without a guide.
  - Temples inside the area: Pura Luhur Sri Rambut Sedana and Pura Luhur Bhujangga Waisnawa Gunung Sari.
- **Context:** in Dec 2025 farmers covered fields with metal sheets and black plastic to protest the sealing of
  businesses by DPRD Bali's Pansus TRAP; the Bupati promised a PBB (land tax) exemption [S108]. Whether the covers
  are gone is not confirmed. Ask the pengelola before booking.

### 2. Kebun Raya "Eka Karya" Bali (Bali Botanic Garden). Desa Candikuning, Kecamatan Baturiti, Kabupaten Tabanan

- **Manager:** BRIN runs conservation; PT Mitra Natura Raya runs educational tourism. Area 157.5 ha [S129][S130].
- **Name:** "Kebun Raya 'Eka Karya' Bali" is the historical name; the operator site now uses "Kebun Raya Bali"
  [S127][S129]. Print "Kebun Raya Bali (Eka Karya)".
- **The shot:** cool highland garden. The official visitor guide (Jul–Sep 2026) lists these as "Interesting Spot"
  (not as photo spots) [S128]:
  - Giant Ficus Tree
  - Kumbakarna Laga Statue
  - Cactus Greenhouse
  - Begonia Greenhouse
  - Cyathea (tree fern) Garden
  - Orchid and Rhododendron gardens
  - Lake View
- **Distinctive:** a forest-and-glasshouse look you cannot get on the coast. DJKN notes the garden is already used
  for prewedding shoots and music videos [S129].
  - The official site sells a "Wedding Package" and venue rental (official) [S127], but prices load by script and
    could not be read.
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.** Contact via kebunraya.id/r/bali [S127].
- **Drones:** not confirmed.
- **Elevation:** CONFLICT (see Conflicts).
- **Hours and ticket:** single-source (see that section).

### 3. Danau Tamblingan (TWA Danau Buyan–Danau Tamblingan). Desa Munduk, Kecamatan Banjar, Kabupaten Buleleng

- **Location and status:** Desa Munduk, Kec. Banjar, about 1,000 m above sea level [S119][S307].
  - Inside the TWA (nature tourism park), 1,847.38 ha, one of five conservation units run by BKSDA Bali [S114].
- **The shot:**
  - Lakeside temples Pura Ulun Danu Tamblingan and Pura Dalem Tamblingan [S119][S120].
  - Traditional *pedau*/*pedahu* boats [S119][S120]. Do not call them wooden; newer ones are fibreglass [S120].
  - The Alas Mertajati forest around the lake [S120][S121].
- **Distinctive:** quiet, forested, no beach clubs. The opposite of south Bali.
- **Sacred status:** the forest and lake are sacred to the four villages of Adat Dalem Tamblingan (Catur Desa:
  Munduk, Gobleg, Gesing, Umajero) [S120][S121b].
  - The head of BKSDA said the area "in principle… is not for mass tourism" [S121].
  - **No prewedding ban was found** (checked again 2026-10-07).
  - Tell couples to shoot only with permission from BKSDA and the pengempon (temple custodians), and to keep away
    from the temple courts.
- **Prewedding fee (national PNBP under PP 36/2024; signed and promulgated 30 Sep 2024, in force 30 days later)
  [S118]:**
  - BKSDA Bali's rates for its TWAs, as reported by Antara on 21 Feb 2026 after quoting Kepala BKSDA Bali Ratna
    Hendratmoko: "Video dan foto prewedding sebesar Rp1 juta" [S114]. Rinjani national park reports the same
    "Rp 1 juta per paket per lokasi" (Nov 2024) [S117b].
  - **The Rp3 juta foreign rate is NOT confirmed for Bali.** It was reported only for national parks (Komodo,
    Lore Lindu) [S116][S117]; S114 gives no foreigner figure.
  - **Caveat:** the PP's tariff annex was not readable, and S114 is the reporter's paraphrase. Printable wording:
    "Rp1 juta for prewedding photo/video (BKSDA Bali rates as reported Feb 2026); confirm the package terms and
    the rate for foreign couples with BKSDA Bali."
  - Couples need a Simaksi (entry permit for a conservation area) [S117]. BKSDA Bali is moving Simaksi online and
    to field posts [S114].
- **Drones:** fee single-source (see that section).
- **Entrance fee:** CONFLICT (see Conflicts).
- **Nyepi:** BKSDA closed this TWA 18–20 Mar 2026 for Nyepi [S23]. Single-source.

### 4. Air Terjun Sekumpul. Desa Sekumpul, Kecamatan Sawan, Kabupaten Buleleng

- **The shot:** a cluster of several falls side by side in a green gorge with sheer walls [S219][S223][S224]. The
  name "Sekumpul" means a group or cluster [S219][S224].
- **Distinctive:**
  - The scale: a curtain of falls rather than one drop.
  - "Jika beruntung" (with luck) you may see a rainbow in the spray [S218]. detik only, so if used, keep the
    hedge: "with luck"; never "rainbows often form".
- **Manager:** Unit Pengelola BUMDes Sekar Bank Sekumpul runs Sekumpul and the hidden fall; neighbouring Air
  Terjun Fiji is run by Desa Lemukih [S218][S222].
- **Access:** hundreds of steps down [S218][S219]. The step count is a CONFLICT; handrails and the river crossing
  are single-source.
- **Scam warning (print it):** in Nov 2023 Dispar Buleleng permanently closed four illegal ticket posts in the
  Desa Lemukih area after a tourist complained of being asked for Rp300.000. Buy only at the official post
  [S221][S222][S220].
- **Entrance fee:** CONFLICT. Do not print a figure.
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.**
- **Drones:** not confirmed.

### 5. Pantai Melasti. Desa Ungasan, Kecamatan Kuta Selatan, Kabupaten Badung

- **Manager:** BUPDA Desa Adat Ungasan, through its Unit Usaha DTW Pantai Melasti; open since August 2018 [S125].
  The location is confirmed by [S122][S123][S124] (S123 and S124 are both detik and count as one).
- **The shot:** the access road is cut through a limestone hill, giving tall, carved white cliff walls that frame
  white sand and clear water [S122][S123][S124].
- **Distinctive:** a cliff-and-road composition on the Bukit coast, without hiking.
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.** One readable source from 2022 only (see that section). A NusaBali article
  (~2023, quoting the manager) could not be read [S126].
- **Entrance ticket:** CONFLICT. Do not print.
- **Drones:** not confirmed. Melasti is on the Bukit, near Ngurah Rai; flag the general KKOP rule.

### 6. Bendungan Palasari and Gereja Hati Kudus Yesus Palasari. Desa Ekasari, Kecamatan Melaya, Kabupaten Jembrana

- **The shot, dam:** a reservoir in West Bali [S132][S134]. Its surroundings (forest north, rice fields south)
  are single-source.
- **The shot, church:** a Catholic parish church whose community uses Balinese elements such as language, dress,
  *penjor* and *gebogan* [S133][S134]. The architectural description ("Balinese spires reminiscent of merus") is
  single-source.
- **Distinctive:** West Bali, far from the crowds. The only location here that pairs a Catholic church with
  Balinese architecture, which suits Catholic couples.
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.**
- **Etiquette:** it is an active parish church. Couples should ask the parish for permission first. This is
  common-sense advice, not a sourced rule; word it that way.
- **Drive time:** not confirmed (single-source, unread).
- **Weakest-sourced location in the list.** Keep it for Jembrana coverage and keep the copy short.

### 7. Bukit Campuhan (Campuhan Ridge Walk). Gianyar; starts at Pura Gunung Lebah, Ubud

- **Sourcing note:** S226 (2022) copies S227 (2016); both are Kompas and count as one. Mongabay [S227b] is the
  independent second source.
- **The shot:** a paved ridge path between two river valleys, with open green slopes on both sides
  [S225][S226][S227b].
- **Distinctive:** a long leading line through grass, minutes from Ubud.
- **Best time:** morning and late afternoon; joggers use it in the morning [S226][S227b].
- **Fee:** free entry [S225][S227].
- **Access:**
  - Starts by Pura Gunung Lebah; entry is through the Ibah hotel gate [S226][S227b].
  - About 2 km [S226][S227b].
  - Path width, walking time and "no food stalls" are single-source (and Mongabay 2019 reported restaurants
    being built along the path).
- **Etiquette:** the trailhead passes a temple. Apply the general temple etiquette.
- **Prewedding fee and rules:** **not confirmed.**
- **Village and kecamatan:** CONFLICT. Safe wording: "starts at Pura Gunung Lebah, Ubud, Gianyar".

### 8. Desa Penglipuran. Desa Adat Penglipuran, Kelurahan Kubu, Kecamatan Bangli, Kabupaten Bangli. Known spot, fresh angle

- **Location:** Kelurahan Kubu, Kecamatan Bangli, per Pemkab Bangli data cited by Antara [S230][S231].
  - **Do not write "Kecamatan Kubu".** That is a kecamatan in Karangasem.
  - About 45 km from Denpasar [S205][S230]. Older figure: CONFLICT.
- **The shot:**
  - A tidy main street of uniform traditional compounds [S204][S206].
  - **Fresh angle: the bamboo forest** of about 45 ha north of the village, with a walking path, where light
    breaks through the canopy [S205][S206][S204]. Mongabay saw tourists scouting that light for "pre-wedding"
    shoots [S205].
  - The forest is much quieter than the village centre [S205].
- **Crowds:** visitor figures are single-source (see that section). "Go on a weekday morning" is advice, not a
  sourced fact.
- **Manager:** Pengelola Desa Wisata Penglipuran, head I Wayan Sumiarsa [S203][S204].
- **Entrance (Paket Pelipur Lara 1: main street, houses and bamboo forest) [S203][S208]:**
  - Indonesian adult Rp25.000, child Rp15.000.
  - Foreign adult Rp50.000, child Rp30.000.
  - Free safety insurance is included. This is two outlets citing the manager (Antara 20 May 2026; Tribun Travel
    14 Sep 2026).
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.**
- **Drones: banned** [S207][S206]. The March 2024 date and the earlier Rp150.000 drone ticket are single-source.
- **Etiquette:**
  - No motor vehicles in the village; dress modestly [S206][S208].
  - Temple courts: apply SE 7/2025 (official) [S9]. Site-specific rules (statue heads, photographing inside
    houses) are single-source.
- **Hours:** CONFLICT. Nyepi closure is single-source.

### 9. Air Terjun Tukad Cepung. Banjar Penida Kelod, Desa Tembuku, Kecamatan Tembuku, Kabupaten Bangli

- **The shot:** the fall drops inside a narrow, cave-like gorge open at the top, and shafts of sunlight come down
  through the gap [S210][S211][S215].
- **Distinctive:** the light beams and the enclosed rock walls. Nothing else in this list looks like it.
- **Sacred:** long held sacred by locals and used for melukat (ritual cleansing) [S210][S212]. Apply temple
  etiquette.
- **Sourcing note:** S210 and S211 are both Kompas and count as one.
- **Distance:** about 53 km and about 75 min from Ngurah Rai [S209][S210].
- **Access:** stairs down from parking, about 15–20 min on foot [S209][S211].
- **Entrance:** Rp30.000 per person (detik 2024, Kompas 2024) [S209][S211], against Kompas 2023's "donation only"
  [S210]. Neither cites a reachable official page. Print "confirm with the pengelola". "Same price for locals
  and foreigners" is Kompas only.
- **Prewedding fee:** **not confirmed.**
- **Drones:** not confirmed. The gorge is narrow, so a drone is impractical anyway; do not print that as a rule.
- **Hours and light-beam timing:** CONFLICT.
- **History:** closed about two years in the pandemic; the Bupati reopened it in Sep 2021 [S213].

### 10. Bukit Desa Pinggan (sunrise viewpoint). Desa Pinggan, Kecamatan Kintamani, Kabupaten Bangli

### 11. Taman Soekasada Ujung (Taman Ujung Sukasada). Banjar Ujung, Desa Tumbu, Kecamatan Karangasem, Kabupaten Karangasem

- **Name:** the legal name is "Taman Soekasada Ujung" (official, Perbup Karangasem 38/2017, run by the "Badan
  Pengelola Taman Soekasada Ujung") [S338]; detik uses it too [S300]. Kemenpar writes "Taman Ujung Sukasada"
  [S305]; NusaBali and the Karangasem tourism portal write "Taman Sukasada Ujung" [S306][S312c]. Use **Taman
  Soekasada Ujung**, with "Taman Ujung" as the short form.
- **Owner:** a joint asset of Pemkab Karangasem and Puri Agung Karangasem (official, Perbup 38/2017, Menimbang b)
  [S338]. Do not write "owned by the Puri".
- **Pricing basis:** since 1 Jan 2024 ticket prices follow Pemkab Karangasem's Perda 8/2023 (PDRD) [S300][S304].
  The "adds a 10% tax" detail is single-source.
- **Distance:** about 2 hours / 64–70 km from Denpasar [S301][S312][S312c]. The 80 km airport figure is
  single-source; for the airport drive time, see CONFLICT.
- **The shot:**
  - Large pools, bridges and a roofless pavilion of tall pillars on the hill, looking out to the sea at Pantai
    Ujung [S301][S304][S307][S312].
  - detik names the pools, the bridge and the pillared roofless building as the usual prewedding spots [S307].
- **Distinctive:** built from 1909 by the Raja of Karangasem with a Dutch and a Chinese architect, mixing European
  and Balinese styles [S301][S312]. A palace look with the sea behind.
- **Prewedding fee:**
  - **Rp1.000.000 domestic, Rp2.000.000 foreign, for all crew.** NusaBali, 23 Jul 2017 [S306]; detikTravel,
    9 Sep 2023 [S307]. NusaBali names the manager (Ida Made Alit) but the fee line is the reporter's own text,

    izin lokasi pemotretan" [S305].

  - **Printable wording:** "from Rp1 juta (domestic); confirm with the pengelola". Do not print the Rp2 juta
    foreign figure as current.
- **Entrance (since 1 Jan 2024):** domestic adult Rp35.000, child (5–12) Rp25.000; foreign adult Rp100.000, child
  Rp75.000 [S300][S304].
- **Hours:** 07.00–19.00 WITA daily [S301][S304][S307]. Best in the morning or late afternoon, when it is not too
  hot [S301][S304][S307].
- **Drones:** not confirmed (fee single-source).

### 13. Savana Tianyar. Desa Tianyar, Kecamatan Kubu, Kabupaten Karangasem
