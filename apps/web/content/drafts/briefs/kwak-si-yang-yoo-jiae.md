# Research brief: Kwak Si-yang & Yoo Jiae wedding

Compiled 2026-10-08 (Thursday) for a background-only draft; to be completed on the wedding day.
Source markers [S#] refer to the Sources list at the end. Every printed fact carries at least two
independent markers. Indonesian outlets (S31 to S34) and Malay Mail (S35) relay Soompi (S3) or MyDaily (S1),
so they do **not** count as independent of those two; they are listed for conflicts, photo credits and
Instagram handles only. Where several articles come from one outlet (Ilgan S5 to S8, Xportsnews S9 to S13,
Sports Kyunghyang S19 to S21, TV Report S22 to S25, MyDaily S1 and S2), they count as **one** source.
All dates have been day-of-week checked with Python `datetime`.

## The announcement

**Date:** **Thursday, 3 September 2026** (KST) [S1][S3][S4][S15][S16]. Earliest timestamps found: MyDaily
exclusive 14:55 KST [S1]; Sports Kyunghyang 15:04 [S19]; TV Report 15:16 [S22]; Xportsnews and Hankyung
15:18 [S9][S28]; Supple "[공식]" 15:20 [S30]; Edaily 15:32 [S17]; Soompi 06:43 UTC = 15:43 KST [S3].
Draft says "menjelang pukul tiga sore" for the scoop (14:55).

**Sequence:** the news broke as a press exclusive, **not** as an announcement by the couple. The
agencies confirmed it afterwards, and the letters followed the same day [S1][S2][S11][S22].

**9Ato Entertainment statement (Kwak), paraphrase:** Kwak will marry singer and actress Yoo Jiae this
coming **November**; they promised a future together based on deep trust and love [S2][S3][S8][S17][S28][S30].
The ceremony will be private, with both families, relatives and close acquaintances [S2][S3][S11][S23][S24].
Specific details are hard to disclose out of respect for both families' wishes [S3][S17]. Please look on
them warmly and send blessings and support [S3][S5]. Korean original (core): "곽시양 배우가 오는 11월 가수 겸 배우
유지애와 결혼식을 올릴 예정입니다" [S30].

**Note on the date in the statement:** the written statement says only "November". The day, 28 November,
was given by a 9Ato representative to News1 [S4], Money Today [S14] and Sports Kyunghyang [S19], and by
MyDaily's scoop [S1]. Soompi writes that the agency "confirmed that the two are getting married on November
28" [S3]. Edaily notes the date "has been reported as November 28" next to the agency's no-details line [S17].
Four independent outlets carry the agency's own confirmation, so the date is printed.

**KPLUS statement (Yoo), paraphrase:** "Yoo Jiae is marrying Kwak Si-yang" [S4][S17]; over about a year of
seeing each other they grew their love and promised a shared future [S2][S4][S10][S18]; please watch warmly
over the two at their new beginning and give many blessings and support [S2][S18]. JoongAng quotes a shorter
line, "The two are preparing to get married" [S15].

**Yoo Jiae's letter, paraphrase** (posted 3 Sep to her own social media):
- Opens: she thinks fans were very surprised by today's news [S6][S11][S12][S21].
- She has someone with whom she cares for and relies on each other, with whom she wants to share the time
  ahead, and with a good heart they promised a new beginning [S6][S11][S16][S21].
- Apologises that fans who watched over her a long time learned the news through articles [S6][S24][S26].
- As the news is precious to her she wanted to share it more carefully, and her heart is heavy that she
  could not [S6][S24].
- Thanks fans for always being by her side, in joyful and in hard, tiring moments [S6][S21].
- Asks fans to warmly celebrate her new beginning [S6][S11][S16][S21].
- Will keep showing herself working hard in her place, unchanged [S12][S24].

**Kwak Si Yang's letter, paraphrase** (posted 3 Sep to his own social media):
- He thought a lot about how to share the news, and his heart flutters [S7][S11].
- He has met a precious person he wants to spend his life with [S7][S11][S16][S24][S26].
- Based on trust and love, they promised to walk through life together [S7][S16][S24][S26].
- In hard times be each other's support, in happy times laugh together, build a warm family
  [S16][S24][S26].
- Thanks fans; will hold that gratitude close [S7][S11].
- After forming a family, will show a more mature side and good work and acting as an actor
  [S11][S16][S24][S26].
