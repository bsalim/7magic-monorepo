Yoon Jong Hoon's letter opens like one of his usual monthly letters to his fans: "안녕하세요. 윤종훈입니다", or "Hello, this is Yoon Jong Hoon." After asking how they had come through a very hot August, he wrote that he picked up the pen only after thinking long and hard about how best to share this news.

The handwritten letter went up on his fan cafe on Monday, 7 September 2026. Its news: he will marry the actress Han Eun Seo. On the same day his agency, YK Media Plus, confirmed that the wedding will take place on 1 November 2026 at a venue in Gangnam, Seoul. There had never been any report that the two were dating.

This article gathers what is already known about the couple and the way they shared their news. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **Monday, 7 September 2026:** the wedding announcement, through Yoon's handwritten letter on his fan cafe and a confirmation from his agency, YK Media Plus.
- **Tuesday, 8 September 2026:** Han posted a photo of herself with Yoon, along with a message for everyone who had sent their blessings.
- **Sunday, 1 November 2026:** the wedding.
- **Venue:** a location in Gangnam, Seoul. The name of the venue has not been announced.

## Who Yoon Jong Hoon and Han Eun Seo are

Yoon Jong Hoon is an actor born in 1984, aged 42 at the time of the announcement. He started out on stage in the play *Moonlight Trunk* (달빛트렁크) in 2006, then made his television drama debut in *Monstar* on tvN in 2013. He went on to appear in *Reply 1994* and *Misaeng*. K-drama viewers know him best as Ha Yoon-cheol in all three seasons of *The Penthouse* (SBS), the role that won him an Excellence Award. His most recent drama is the KBS1 daily drama *Our Happy Days* (기쁜 우리 좋은 날), in which he played Go Gyeol opposite Uhm Hyun-kyung.

Han Eun Seo was born in 1994, ten years after Yoon, and was 32 at the time of the announcement. She began acting as a teenager and debuted in 2008 in the KBS2 drama *The Great King, Sejong*. She went on to appear in *Will It Snow for Christmas?*, *High Kick! Revenge of the Short Legged*, *Phantom*, *Golden Tower* and *Hymn of Death*. Her most recent television role was in the JTBC drama *Esquire* (English title *Beyond the Bar*). Away from acting, she has completed the coursework of a doctoral programme at Sungkyunkwan University and is reported to be studying at the Royal Central School of Speech and Drama in London.

Yoon is signed to YK Media Plus. Coverage so far does not name Han's agency, and every official confirmation has come from Yoon's.

At the time of the announcement, Han was reported to be studying in London, while Yoon was still starring in a daily drama in Korea. ## Meeting on the set of Return

The two first worked together on the SBS drama *Return*, which aired in 2018. On screen they were on opposite sides. Yoon played Seo Jun-hee, one of the group of perpetrators in the story, while Han played one of that group's victims.

According to reports, they began dating after the drama and decided to marry after several years together. Exactly how many years has never been stated. Nor had the relationship ever been reported before the wedding announcement.

## A letter to his fans, before anyone else

Yoon has a habit of writing to his fans on his fan cafe at the end of every month. In this letter he admitted that this time it had taken him longer to write. He wrote that a precious person had come into his life, and that he now wants to walk beside that person for the rest of his life. After caring for each other, understanding each other and both putting in the effort, they decided to begin a new life through marriage.

He wrote that he wanted his fans to be the first to hear about an important moment in his life. From now on, he wants to live more responsibly as a husband and as the actor Yoon Jong Hoon, and he will keep working to be both a good actor and a good person. He admitted to feeling excited and also feeling the weight of this new chapter. At the end of the letter he promised to keep turning up at the end of every month as usual to share stories.

On the same day his agency, YK Media Plus, confirmed that Yoon and Han will marry on 1 November at a venue in Gangnam, Seoul.

Write it in your own words, as Yoon did for his fans. The people closest to you will feel valued for hearing it straight from you.

## Han Eun Seo's message a day later

On Tuesday, 8 September 2026, Han posted a photo of the two of them on her social media account, along with a message. She described Yoon as someone who has stood by her for a long time, steadily, with warm affection. As partners who will spend their lives together, she wrote, they will cherish each other even more and move forward together. She thanked everyone who had sent their blessings and promised that they will keep trying to become better people, for each other and for those around them.

Yoon and Han each shared the news on their own channel, in their own voice, a day apart. Indonesian couples can use the same pattern with the extended family. Agree first on who tells which side of the family and when, so that no relative on either side hears it from someone else first.
