Ji Ye Eun admitted she had struggled to find the right time and the right way to share the news. "I'm getting married," she wrote on Instagram on Wednesday, 2 September 2026. On the same day, Vata posted the same news from his own account, and both their agencies confirmed it to the Korean press.

They will marry on Saturday, 12 December 2026, in Seoul. That date is Vata's birthday. Their relationship only went public in April 2026, and Korean media wrote that the two had been dating for about a year at the time of the announcement. The venue has not been announced.

This article gathers what is already known about the couple, from the way they met to the story of the proposal. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **Monday, 13 April 2026:** Ji Ye Eun's agency, CP Entertainment, confirmed that the two were dating.
- **Wednesday, 2 September 2026, morning in Korea:** both agencies confirm the wedding.
- **Wednesday, 2 September 2026, evening:** the two announce the wedding in an Instagram post each.
- **Saturday, 12 December 2026:** the wedding, falling on Vata's birthday.
- **Venue:** an undisclosed location in Seoul.

## Who Ji Ye Eun and Vata are

Ji Ye Eun is an actress born in 1994. She started her career in the web drama *How To* in 2017, then became widely known for her comedy roles on *SNL Korea* on Coupang Play. She is now a regular cast member of the SBS variety show *Running Man*, alongside Yoo Jae-suk, Ji Suk-jin and the other members. She is signed to CP Entertainment.

Vata was also born in 1994, so the two are the same age. He is a dancer and choreographer, and the leader of the dance crew We Dem Boyz. The crew finished second on the Mnet dance competition show *Street Man Fighter* in 2022. As a choreographer, he created the moves for "like JENNIE" by BLACKPINK's Jennie and for Zico's "New Thing". He is signed to the agency ARA.

Vata's line of work suggests an idea you can borrow. There is no word yet on whether there will be dancing at their wedding, but a couple's first dance is turning up more and more often at Indonesian receptions. If you would like to try one, start practising a few months ahead and choose simple moves that still feel comfortable in a wedding gown and wedding shoes. A dance coach can help you trim the choreography down to just two or three minutes.

## Friends from church

Korean media wrote that Ji Ye Eun and Vata met because they worshipped at the same church. They were friends first before they started dating. In 2025 they also worked together: Vata choreographed the song "Milkshake", which Ji Ye Eun performed with Ji Suk-jin on the Waterbomb Seoul 2025 stage as the project group Chungju Ji-ssi.

Word of their relationship came out on 13 April 2026, and Ji Ye Eun's agency confirmed it that same day. According to the agency, the two had been colleagues and were now dating because they had come to like each other. After that, Ji Ye Eun talked about Vata quite openly, both on *Running Man* and on the YouTube talk show *Pinggyego*. On *Running Man* she shared that even after they officially started dating, it was three weeks before they held hands.

Many Indonesian couples also meet through a faith community, whether a church or a pengajian (Quran study) group. If you and your partner come from the same community, make use of it as you prepare for the wedding. Ask the people who run it whether there is a premarital class or counselling, and sign up early, because sessions are usually limited.

## An announcement in two posts

On 2 September 2026, Ji Ye Eun's agency, CP Entertainment, said that the two had promised to share their lives on the basis of deep trust, and asked for warm congratulations and support. Vata's agency, ARA, said that the two would begin a new journey as husband and wife.

The posts from the two of them were more personal. Ji Ye Eun wrote that marriage had always felt like a distant story in her life, until she met someone she wants to spend her whole life with. She described Vata as someone who is always on her side, who gives her strength, and who taught her the happiness of enjoying things together. She promised that they would be each other's support and live healthy, happy lives.

Vata wrote that he had met someone who is like a gift, who sees him and loves him as he is. He described himself as someone who had only ever known work, and said Ji Ye Eun had taught him what love is. He promised that they would lean on each other and live happily together.

You can use this pattern when telling the extended family. Instead of one joint message, each of you writes a short paragraph about the other: what makes you sure of them. Send the two one after the other to the family group chat. Both families then get to know their future in-law through their own child's words.

## A proposal along a path of candles

Ji Ye Eun told the story of Vata's proposal on the *Running Man* episode that aired on 13 September 2026. That day she had a packed filming schedule and was exhausted. As soon as she opened the door and walked in, she saw balloons and a path of candles. She thought Vata had hired an events company. Vata then read her a letter and gave her a ring. They hugged and cried, then tidied up the balloons together.

On the same show she explained why she chose Vata. In her words he is a very warm person, and he still speaks kindly even when they argue.

Two things in this story are easy to borrow. A proposal doesn't have to happen in a restaurant or a tourist spot: a room that feels familiar to you both, at the end of a tiring day, can be just as memorable if there is a letter you wrote yourself. And before the wedding, it is worth the two of you talking about how you argue, for example which words you will never say and when to take a break before carrying on.

## Marrying on Vata's birthday

12 December 2026 falls on a Saturday, and it is Vata's birthday. Korean media said only that the wedding will be held at a location in Seoul. The venue's name and the order of events have not been announced.

Indonesian couples often choose a date by weton or shio reckoning, or simply pick one that is easy to remember. One partner's birthday is a personal choice. If you go for it, agree early on how you will celebrate both occasions every year, so that the birthday doesn't get lost behind the anniversary, or the other way round.

## What you can borrow

- If you want a first dance, start practising a few months ahead with moves that feel comfortable in a wedding gown and shoes.
- Ask about premarital classes in your faith community, and sign up early.
- When telling the extended family, have each of you write one paragraph about the other.
- A proposal in a familiar place, with a letter you wrote yourself, can be as memorable as one somewhere grand.
- Talk about how the two of you argue before the wedding day.
- If you choose a birthday as your wedding date, agree on how to celebrate both every year.
