The news of Kwak Si Yang and Yoo Jiae's wedding did not come from the couple themselves. On Thursday, 3 September 2026, shortly before three in the afternoon Korean time, the Korean outlet MyDaily reported as an exclusive that the actor and the former Lovelyz member would marry on 28 November. Until then there had never been any report that the two were dating.

That same afternoon, each of their agencies confirmed the news. Later the same day, Kwak and Yoo each posted a handwritten letter to their fans. In hers, Yoo apologised that fans had learned the news from the press first.

This article gathers what is already known about the couple and their wedding plans. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **Thursday, 3 September 2026:** the wedding news was first reported by MyDaily, then confirmed by Kwak's agency, 9Ato Entertainment, and Yoo's agency, KPLUS. On the same day, each of them posted a handwritten letter on social media.
- **Saturday, 28 November 2026:** the wedding, held privately, for both families, relatives and close acquaintances only.
- **Venue:** Seoul. The agencies have not announced a venue. Two Korean outlets name a hotel in the Yongsan-gu district.

## Who Kwak Si Yang and Yoo Jiae are

Kwak Si Yang is an actor born in 1987, aged 39 at the time of the announcement. His real name is Kwak Myung-jin. He started out as a model, then made his film debut in *Night Flight* (2014). K-drama viewers know him from *Oh My Ghost* (2015), *Alice* (2020), *Lovers of the Red Sky* (2021), *Flex X Cop* (2024) and *Good Partner*. On the big screen, his films include *The Witness*. He also appears in the KBS2 weekend drama *Neo Malgo Dareun Yeonae* (너 말고 다른 연애), which began airing on 12 September 2026. Outlets translate its title into English in several different ways, so we use the Korean title.

Yoo Jiae was born in 1993, six years younger than Kwak, and was 33 at the time of the announcement. She debuted as a member of the girl group Lovelyz in 2014 under Woollim Entertainment, and sang on songs such as "Ah-Choo" and "Destiny". Lovelyz's contract with Woollim ended in November 2021. Since then she has focused more on acting, including the MBC daily drama *The Third Marriage* and, in early 2026, the short-form drama *Doctor K*.

Kwak is signed to 9Ato Entertainment, while Yoo is with KPLUS. Both are known for work in front of the camera, but they came to it by different routes: Kwak through dramas and film, Yoo through the music stage before moving into acting.

In his letter, Kwak wrote that after starting a family he wants to show a more mature side of himself and keep delivering good work and good acting. Yoo promised her fans that she will keep working hard in her place, unchanged. Lines like these are worth borrowing. Many brides and grooms in Indonesia get asked "will you still be working?" by relatives, colleagues or clients. When you hand out invitations at the office, mention that you will keep working and when your wedding leave falls, so people know what to expect.

## News that arrived through the press first

After the MyDaily report, 9Ato Entertainment issued an official statement. It said that Kwak will marry singer and actress Yoo Jiae in November, and that the two have promised to build a future together on deep mutual trust and love. The agency asked for understanding that details are hard to share out of respect for the wishes of both families, then asked the public to greet the couple warmly. A representative of the same agency confirmed the date of 28 November to several Korean outlets.

KPLUS released a statement of its own. According to Yoo's agency, the two grew their affection over about a year of seeing each other, then promised to share their future. KPLUS also asked the public for their blessings and support for the couple's new beginning.

Wedding news can get out before you are ready, for example through a relative who is a little too eager to post engagement photos. Prepare two things early on. First, a list of the people who should hear the news from you directly, such as parents, grandparents, close friends and your boss. Second, a single paragraph of announcement, ready to post. If the news leaks, you can respond the same day, as Kwak and Yoo did.

## Handwritten letters to fans

Yoo's letter opens by saying she imagines fans were very surprised by that day's news. She wrote that she now has someone with whom she can care for and lean on each other, and whom she wants beside her in the time ahead. With good hearts, they have promised a new beginning. She apologised to the fans who have followed her for a long time that they learned the news from the press. Because the news was precious to her, she wrote, she had wanted to share it more carefully, and her heart was heavy that this did not happen. She thanked the fans who have always been by her side, in happy moments and in tired ones, then asked them to warmly welcome her new beginning.

Kwak wrote that he thought a great deal about how to share the news, and that his heart was fluttering. He has met someone precious whom he wants to be with for the rest of his life. Built on trust and love, they have promised to walk through life together. In hard times they want to be each other's support, in happy moments to laugh together, and to build a warm family. He also thanked the fans who have always supported him, and wrote that he will hold that gratitude close.

Both letters thank the fans who have supported them for a long time (Kwak traces that support back to his first steps as an actor). You can do the same in your reception speech or on your thank-you cards. Set aside one part for the people who were with you before you met your partner, for example a teacher, a coach, an old housemate or your first boss, and name them one by one.

## They met at the same salon

The agencies have not said how the two met. Ilgan Sports and Money Today report that Kwak and Yoo got to know each other as customers of the same hair and makeup salon, and that the friendship grew into a relationship. According to KPLUS, they were together for about a year before deciding to marry. In all that time, no report ever circulated that they were dating.

Your regular hairstylist and makeup artist usually know your face and hair by heart. If you have a salon or MUA who has looked after you for a long time, ask early on whether they take on bridal work. Book a makeup trial well before the wedding day, too, so there is still time to change the look if the result isn't quite right.

## A private ceremony for family

The wedding will take place on Saturday, 28 November 2026, in Seoul. According to 9Ato Entertainment, only both families, relatives and close acquaintances will attend. The agencies have not announced a venue. Sports Kyunghyang and Money Today name a hotel in the Yongsan-gu district, without naming the hotel itself. As of writing, no pre-wedding photos have been made public.

The agency said the details are being kept private out of respect for the wishes of both families. This pattern is useful if your own wedding is small. Agree with both families early on what may be shared publicly, for example one official photo after the event, and who will post it. That way no relative posts before the two of you do.
