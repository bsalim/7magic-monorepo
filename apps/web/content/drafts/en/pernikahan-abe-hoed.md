"Rasanya saya tidak pernah membayangkan bahwa pertemuan kami di Australia pada akhirnya membawa saya pada pagi hari ini," roughly "I never imagined that our meeting in Australia would one day bring me to this morning." Abe Hoed said those words in front of two extended families on Saturday morning, 26 September 2026, in Jakarta, as he set out his wish to marry his girlfriend. The moment was captured in a video shared by Raffi Ahmad, one of the guests.

Abe is the younger son of the musician couple Melly Goeslaw and Anto Hoed. In a video the media received on Sunday, 27 September 2026, Melly announced that her son's akad, the Islamic marriage contract, and the reception would be held in December 2026. As of writing on 8 October 2026, the date and venue have not been announced.

This article gathers their story so far, from their meeting in Australia to the formal lamaran in Jakarta. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **2024**: Abe left for Brisbane, Australia, to continue his studies.
- **February 2026**: Abe graduated from Universitas Indonesia.
- **August 2026**: Abe posted the moment he proposed to his girlfriend on his personal Instagram.
- **Saturday, 26 September 2026**: the formal lamaran, or engagement, in Jakarta, bringing both families together.
- **Sunday, 27 September 2026**: in a video the media received that day, Melly Goeslaw said the akad and reception would be held in December 2026.
- **The wedding**: December 2026. The date and venue have not been announced.
- **Early 2027**: the couple plan to start master's degrees.

## Who Abe Hoed is

His full name is Pria Bernama Hoed, which reads in Indonesian as "A Man Named Hoed". He was born on 9 May 2003, which makes him 23. His older brother is Anakku Lelaki Hoed ("My Son Hoed"), known as Ale. In 2016 the brothers formed the band Goodbye Felicia, with Abe on drums. After that he made music as a solo artist, releasing a single in 2018 and then the album *Carnation* in 2019. He also produces his own work. Outside music, Abe is a graduate of Universitas Indonesia.

His fiancée is not a public figure, so we don't give her name here. She is the daughter of the politician Bambang Soesatyo, and the engagement means Melly Goeslaw and Anto Hoed will become in-laws of Bambang Soesatyo's family.

Abe is only 23, and according to Melly the couple already plan to start master's degrees soon after the wedding. Young couples often face something similar: the wedding arrives at the same time as plans for study or a first job. Before you set a date, sit down together and write out each of your plans for the next two or three years. A date chosen after that is less likely to need moving.

## A meeting in Australia

In 2024 Abe left for Brisbane, Australia, to continue his studies. It was in Australia that he met his girlfriend, as he said himself at the engagement. When and where exactly they first met has not been reported.

His studies in Indonesia wrapped up in February 2026, when he graduated from Universitas Indonesia, seven months before the engagement.

Abe's opening line at the engagement took the guests straight back to the start of their story. If you or your family are asked to give a speech at your lamaran, slip in a sentence or two about where the two of you first met. It doesn't need to be long, because a short line like that is what guests remember.

## Two proposals

The proposal in front of the families in September was not the first. About four weeks earlier, in August 2026, Abe had posted the moment he proposed to his girlfriend on his personal Instagram. Only after that did Abe's family come formally to ask for her hand.

This order is common in Indonesia: a proposal between the two of you first, then the family lamaran. Leave a few weeks between them. That time is useful for agreeing who will speak for each family and for preparing the seserahan, the gifts carried to the engagement, without rushing.

## The family lamaran in Jakarta

The lamaran took place on Saturday morning, 26 September 2026, in Jakarta, with the extended families and close relatives of both sides. Abe and his fiancée matched in pastel yellow, also called butter yellow, while his fiancée's family wore blue. The room was decorated with arrangements of flowers in many colours. Melly Goeslaw herself handed one of the seserahan to the family of her future daughter-in-law.

Among the guests were Raffi Ahmad and Nagita Slavina, who shared moments from the engagement on Instagram, and the singer Rossa.

A colour split like this is easy to copy. Give each family one outfit colour for the lamaran, then let everyone know through the family group chat a few weeks ahead. In the photos, guests can tell the groom's side from the bride's side at a glance, and the couple still stand out in a colour of their own.

## December plans and a master's degree

In a video the media received on Sunday, 27 September 2026, Melly thanked people for their prayers and support for her son's lamaran. She said that "akad dan resepsi akan diadakan di bulan Desember", meaning the akad and reception would be held in December, with the date to follow. She also said Abe and his fiancée plan to start master's degrees early next year.

If the two of you also plan to go back to school or move city after the wedding, count back from the departure date. Leave a few weeks after the reception for paperwork and packing, so your first days as a married couple aren't swallowed by admin.

## What you can borrow

- Write out both your study and work plans before you set the wedding date.
- Slip a sentence or two about how you met into the lamaran speech.
- Leave a few weeks between the private proposal and the family lamaran.
- Give each family one outfit colour for the lamaran.
- If you will study or move after the wedding, count back from the departure date.
