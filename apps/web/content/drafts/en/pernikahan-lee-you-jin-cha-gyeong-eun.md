Lee You Jin's letter opens with a simple greeting: "안녕하세요. 이유진입니다", or "Hello, this is Lee You Jin." He wrote that this time he was sharing happy news from his life as a person, not as an actor. The letter was posted to Instagram on Tuesday, 6 October 2026. On the same day, Cha Gyeong Eun posted a letter of her own.

They will marry on Saturday, 14 November 2026, at a venue in Seoul. The wedding is private, for both families and close acquaintances only. A few hours after the wedding announcement, representatives for both of them also shared that Cha is expecting a baby.

This article gathers what is already known about the couple, from how they met on set to the way they shared their news. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **Tuesday, 6 October 2026:** the wedding announcement, through a statement from Lee's side and a letter from each of them on Instagram. On the same day, representatives for both announced Cha's pregnancy.
- **Saturday, 14 November 2026:** the wedding, at a venue in Seoul.
- **Venue:** not announced. The wedding is private, for both families and close acquaintances only.

## Who Lee You Jin and Cha Gyeong Eun are

Lee You Jin is an actor and director born in 1992, aged 34 at the time of the announcement. He is the son of veteran actor Lee Hyo-jung and the nephew of actor Lee Ki-young. He made his debut in 2013 in the MBC drama *Goddess of Fire* (불의 여신 정이). K-drama fans may know him from *Age of Youth 2*, *Familiar Wife*, *Be Melodramatic* and *Do You Like Brahms?*. More recently he has tried a new role behind the camera. The first short drama he directed, with the Korean title 길이 보이면 캐스팅해! (Soompi translates it as *Cast Me if You Can!*), entered *Directors Arena*, a short-drama directing competition on the ENA channel, and in May 2026 placed second.

Cha Gyeong Eun is an actress born in 1998, aged 28 at the time of the announcement. She began her career in 2021 with the short film *Peach* (복숭아), then played the lead in the web drama *PICKGO* (픽고). She has also appeared in *Future of the Future* (미래의 미래) on U+ Mobile TV and in the sketch comedy *Short Box* (숏박스), and is a regular cast member of *SNL Korea Reboot* season seven on Coupang Play. Her older sister is the actress Kim Seung-yoon, who is due to marry the actor Jang Dong-yoon in October 2026. That means the two sisters marry about a month apart.

After the wedding, Lee will keep working as an actor and director, and Cha will carry on with her acting career. If a sibling is marrying close to your own date, talk the dates through with both families early. Your parents will go through two sets of celebrations in a short time, so leave a sensible gap between them and divide the family's tasks clearly.

## Meeting on set

The two met through the first short drama Lee directed, in which Cha was one of the cast. According to Korean media, Lee fell for her at first sight while preparing the shoot, and Cha felt the same pull. After filming wrapped, Lee was the first to say how he felt. They have been dating with marriage in mind since June 2026, and both families have given their blessing. The wedding comes about five months after they started dating.

On 15 July 2026, Cha posted a video to her YouTube channel that follows the drama's shoot from the first day to the last. In it, Lee calls Cha the actor with the best working attitude he has come across, and predicts she will win a rookie award within two years. On the final day of filming, Lee handed flowers to the cast, and Cha cried.

If you and your partner met through work or a project, look again for photos and videos from that time. Footage from the early days of knowing each other makes material for a reception opening video that feels far more personal than a slideshow of pre-wedding photos alone.

## An announcement in two letters

In his letter, Lee wrote that in mid-November he will marry the person he loves. In his twenties he became an actor, and this year, in his thirties, he fulfilled a long-held dream of becoming a director. His last dream, he wrote, was to meet someone who made him want to devote his whole life to them. He described Gyeong Eun as the first person he wants to talk to at the end of a busy day. When he could not believe in himself, a single sentence from her made the impossible possible more than once. He closed with a promise to keep working faithfully, as an actor and as a director, and asked for warm congratulations. Lee also posted a short video from their wedding photo shoot captioned "이유진&차경은 결혼합니다", meaning "Lee You Jin and Cha Gyeong Eun are getting married."

Cha's letter was shorter. She wrote that some people might be surprised, but that for a long time, building a happy family had been her biggest goal, and that she has now met someone precious and is making a new start. She wanted to tell the people who have cared for and supported her directly, and promised to work at being a good actress as well as someone building a family. She closed by asking for their blessing.

What makes Lee's letter feel close is one everyday detail: the first person he wants to talk to at the end of the day. When you write your announcement or your vows, name one small habit like that about your partner. One real detail stays with guests longer than general praise.

## News of a pregnancy

On the same day, representatives for Lee and Cha issued a joint statement. The two had decided to marry and received the blessing of both families, and while preparing for the wedding they recently learned that Cha is pregnant. According to the statement, both of them and their families are joyfully getting ready to welcome a new life. Details such as the due date were not shared because they are a private matter, and they asked the public for understanding.

For couples who marry while the bride is pregnant, her comfort comes first. Tell your wedding organiser what she needs from the start: a chair always available near the stage, a running order that is not too long, a gown that does not press on the stomach, and a menu that is safe in pregnancy. You also have every right to choose which news you share and which stays within the family.

## A private wedding in Seoul

Lee's side said the wedding will be held on 14 November at a venue in Seoul, with invitations limited to both families and close acquaintances. The venue and other details have deliberately not been announced. In its statement, Lee's side asked for understanding of that decision, which was made out of consideration for the families and the guests.

A private wedding still needs the guests' cooperation. If you want a quiet wedding, put a short request on the invitation, for example asking guests not to post the location or tag the venue until the event is over. Guests are usually glad to help as long as the request is made clearly from the start.
