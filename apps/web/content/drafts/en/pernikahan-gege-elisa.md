"A little glimpse of forever, before the 'I do.'" That English line was the caption on the pre-wedding photos Gege Elisa posted to Instagram in early October 2026, together with the hashtag #prewedding. In them she wears a white wedding gown and poses with her fiancé in a garden, on a boat on a lake and on a riverbank as the sun goes down.

The post drew attention straight away. Many netizens asked who her fiancé was, because Gege has almost never shown her relationship on social media. As of writing on 8 October 2026, the date and venue of the wedding have not been announced.

This article gathers Gege's story so far, from the proposal in Paris to her pre-wedding photos. We will fill in the wedding-day sections once the wedding has taken place.

## What we know so far

- **Saturday, 21 March 2026**: Gege announced on Instagram that her partner had proposed to her in the Tuileries Garden in Paris.
- **Early October 2026**: Gege posted pre-wedding photos with her fiancé.
- **The wedding**: the date and venue have not been announced.

## Who Gege Elisa is

Gege Elisa was born in Jakarta on 10 January 1994, which makes her 32. She started acting at 16 in the film *Gaby dan Lagunya* (2010), then appeared in *Virgin 3: Satu Malam Mengubah Segalanya* (2011). Since then she has been in the film *Ada Cinta di SMA* (2016), a run of FTVs, the made-for-TV films that fill Indonesian evening schedules, and the web series *Bestie* (2022). Besides acting, she also works as a model.

Her fiancé is not a public figure. Gege has never given his name, neither when she announced the proposal nor when she posted the pre-wedding photos, and we don't give it here either. Their relationship barely appeared on Gege's account before the news of the proposal.

Not every couple wants their love story shared with lots of people, and that's fine. If one of you is more private, agree together first on what can be posted, for example whether your partner's face may appear in the proposal photos. A small agreement like this saves you from awkwardness once a post has already been seen by many people.

## A proposal in the Tuileries Garden, Paris

On Saturday, 21 March 2026, Gege shared the news that she was engaged. The proposal took place in the Tuileries Garden, a park in the centre of Paris, France. In the photo she posted, her partner is down on one knee holding out a ring. The ring box visible in the photo shows that the ring came from Harry Winston, the American jewellery house.

Her caption was just four words: "A lifetime to go." There was no long story about when they met or how long they had been together, and that has still not been reported.

Announcing an engagement doesn't need a long piece of writing. If you want the happy news to still feel like it belongs to the two of you, the one photo you like best and a single short line are enough. You can save the full story for your speech on the wedding day.

## A pre-wedding shoot in a garden, on a lake and by a river

Gege's pre-wedding photos show several locations. There is a European-style garden lined with tall cypress trees, there are shots on a boat with a lake and green mountains behind, and there is a session on a riverbank as the sun goes down, with a bridge in the background. The exact locations have not been confirmed.

In the main session Gege wears a white wedding gown, so the photos already feel like wedding pictures. The comments filled up with congratulations.

A sunset shot like the one by the river takes some timing. If your pre-wedding shoot uses more than one location, plan the route around the direction of the light, and keep the time just before sunset for the spot that suits a dusk mood best. Ask your photographer to check the sunset time for the day, then put the location you love most last.
