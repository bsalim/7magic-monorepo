The reception is over, guests from Jakarta, Surabaya or Singapore still have two or three days in Bali, and the two of you want a little quiet before flying home. Ubud and Kintamani work for both. Ubud has the more intimate options for a couple, while Kintamani, with its cafés looking over the Mount Batur caldera, suits a group of guests of mixed ages and fitness.

This article splits the ideas in two: what suits the two of you best, and what is easiest to arrange for guests. Every price below comes from a source listed at the end of the article, along with its date. Ticket prices in Bali change often, so check again before you go.

## Before you go: Ubud's one-way streets

The biggest change in Ubud this year is the traffic. Since 8 September 2026, the Gianyar transport department has run the third phase of a one-way scheme in central Ubud. Jl. Raya Pengosekan is now one-way northbound towards Monkey Forest, and Jl. Raya Ubud is one-way eastbound from Monkey Forest to the Arjuna statue. Jl. Sukma Kesuma and Jl. Sugriwa run one-way north, while Jl. Peliatan 1 and Jl. Jatayu run one-way west.

Tour buses are no longer allowed into the town centre. The main bus parking and drop-off point is Dalem Puri. On the first day, gridlock lasted more than four hours and ride-hailing fares reportedly doubled.

What this means for a group of guests: if you hire a bus, guests get off at Dalem Puri and walk or switch to small cars. Follow the signs and the officers on the ground rather than relying on a navigation app. Travel times into central Ubud from before 2026 no longer hold, so leave generous gaps in the itinerary.

## For the two of you in Ubud

### Melukat at Tirta Empul

Tirta Empul in Tampaksiring is a holy-spring temple where visitors can take part in melukat, a purification ritual. For many couples it is a calm way to begin married life after a busy wedding day. Tickets are IDR 75,000 for adults and IDR 50,000 for children, and it opens from 8am to 6pm. Some older sources, including the indonesia.travel page, still list IDR 50,000, but two 2026 sources agree on IDR 75,000.

A sarong (kamen) and sash must be worn. One source says they are provided and another says rental costs about IDR 10,000 to 15,000, so carry small notes. Bring a change of clothes if you join the melukat. Lockers cost extra. If Tirta Empul is too crowded, Pura Mengening, only five minutes away, is usually quieter.

### A couples spa in Tegallalang

One of the 2026 openings is Hammana Spa at Equipoise Resort in Tegallalang. It sits among the rice fields, has couples suites and a Turkish hammam, and opens from 10am to 9pm.

### Campuhan Ridge Walk at sunrise

This 2 km trail is free and open 24 hours. An easy walk takes about an hour. The best times are 6am to 7am or 4pm to 6pm. It starts near Ibah, about a 15-minute walk from Ubud Palace.

### Waterfalls

Tegenungan is the easiest to reach, with a ticket of IDR 30,000 and more than 150 steps. Go at 6:30am to 7am so you are not queuing for photos. Kanto Lampo and Tibumana are both listed at IDR 25,000 as of November 2026, although some guides give IDR 20,000 for Tibumana. Tukad Cepung is IDR 35,000.

Waterfalls are at their best in the dry season, April to October.

### Cooking classes and dinner

A cooking class for two is a memory you can take home to your own kitchen. Sokasi Cooking School at Four Seasons Sayan teaches six Balinese recipes over five hours, with vegetarian and vegan options.

For dinner, Locavore NXT, Room4Dessert and Kubu regularly appear on lists of romantic restaurants in Ubud. Nibble.id also lists Riserva in Kenderan, Tegallalang, a 16-seat charcoal steakhouse open Tuesday to Sunday from 5pm to 10pm. If you are putting together a budget for the days after the reception, our guide to [a honeymoon after the wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/bulan-madu-setelah-menikah-panduan-anggaran) can help.

## For your guests in Ubud

### Legong and Barong at Ubud Palace

The main courtyard of Ubud Palace (Puri Saren) is free and open from 7am to 6pm. Every night from 7:30pm there is a Legong and Barong dance performance, with tickets at about IDR 100,000. Guests of every age only need to sit and watch. Because of the one-way streets, guests are best off having dinner near the palace and walking to the show.

### Tegallalang rice terraces

The Tegallalang terraces are part of the UNESCO-listed subak landscape. Entry is IDR 50,000 according to Honeycombers, while another source gives a range of IDR 25,000 to 50,000. Parking, swings and cycling are charged separately, and we could not confirm swing prices. It opens from 8am to 6pm, with some entrances open from 6am. Go at 6:30am to 7am or 2pm to 4pm. Congestion from Ubud now reaches as far as Tegallalang.

### Monkey Forest at its new prices

Since 1 April 2026, the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary has charged new rates. Foreign adults pay IDR 130,000 and children aged 3 to 12 pay IDR 100,000. Indonesian citizens and KTP holders pay IDR 90,000 for adults and IDR 60,000 for children. Under-threes are free. You can pay by QRIS, card or cash, and one ticket is valid all day. It opens from 9am to 6pm, with last entry at 5pm.

So Indonesian guests should bring their KTP to get the local rate. If you see IDR 100,000 for adults in another article, that is the price before the April increase.

## Kintamani: Mount Batur and the caldera cafés

Kintamani sits at about 1,500 m, a 1.5 to 2 hour drive from Ubud. At dawn it is around 14 to 16°C, so bring a jacket or sweater, especially for guests used to Jakarta's heat. The roads are steep, winding and can be slippery, so a private driver who knows the route is safer than driving yourself.

Visitors pay a Geopark area entry fee of IDR 50,000 for adults and IDR 30,000 for children. The sources we found do not say whether domestic visitors pay a different rate.

### Mount Batur sunrise trek

The climb to the summit takes about two hours, and the trail is busy, with around 600 hikers a day. Shared packages usually include hotel pickup, a guide, the permit and breakfast at the top. Prices vary widely between operators and we could not confirm any official rate, so compare a few offers.

On guides, the sources disagree. Some say a licensed local guide is mandatory and that hikers without one may be turned away at the trailhead. Another says hiking alone is technically possible but strongly discouraged. Our advice is simple: use a local guide. The volcano's status was listed as normal at its last update on 26 August 2026.

For guests who would rather not hike, a sunrise jeep tour is the alternative. Many jeep tours and hikers stop at Black Lava Café, on the lava field from the 1963 eruption.

### Brunch at the caldera cafés

For a mixed group, the keen hikers can do the trek while everyone else waits at the cafés along Jl. Raya Penelokan. The best time is 9am to 11:30am. At dawn, 5:30am to 8am, it is cold and the cafés are at their busiest, and after 1pm clouds often roll in.

For older guests or anyone with a stroller, Pahdi Specialty Coffee has four glass-walled floors and a lift. Montana del Cafe has net seats hanging over the caldera, and Blue Mountains, glassed in all round, stays comfortable on misty days. Telemba at The Amora has a buffet at IDR 115,000 to 160,000 per person, plus 20% tax and service. Akasa opens from 5:30am. Himalila Café was under renovation as of April 2026. As a rough guide, drinks at these cafés run about IDR 50,000 to 150,000 and meals about IDR 80,000 to 200,000. For something cheaper, detikBali reviewed Kopi Danu in Desa Buahan, which opens from 7am to 8pm and deliberately has no Wi-Fi.

Nibble.id once published a list of 10 cafés in Kintamani, but that article dates from 6 January 2022, so its details need re-checking. From that list, we like its note on mujair nyat-nyat, tilapia from Lake Batur. The dish is served at Kava and at Batur 1926 Coffee, two places that no longer appear on the 2026 list, so make sure both are still open before you take a group there.

### Hot springs

After a trek, a soak in a hot spring is a good way to finish. Batur Natural Hot Spring is listed at IDR 180,000 for adults, but that figure comes from only one source. We found no current price for Toya Devasya.

## Clothing, food and the tourist levy

**Temple clothing.** Shoulders and knees must be covered, and beachwear or very tight clothes are not appropriate. A sarong and sash are required at Tirta Empul. In Kintamani, add a warm layer for the morning. For guests travelling in the wet season, our article on [what to wear to a Bali wedding in the rainy season](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/beauty-fashion/baju-kondangan-ke-bali-musim-hujan) applies to the sightseeing afterwards too.

**Food.** We have not found a restaurant in Ubud or Kintamani whose halal certification can be confirmed through BPJPH or SiHalal. Some of Ubud's famous dishes contain pork, such as the babi guling at Warung Ibu Oka. Plant-based cafés such as Zest Ubud use no pork, but they are not halal-certified. Muslim guests should check certification with each venue directly.

**The foreign tourist levy.** Foreign guests must pay a levy of IDR 150,000 per person, once per visit, ideally through the official Love Bali portal before they fly. Indonesian citizens do not pay it. Send the Love Bali link to your overseas guests along with the invitation.

## A sample day for a group

Ubud and Kintamani can be combined in one long day. Leave early, head up to Kintamani first for brunch at a caldera café, come back down via Tirta Empul and Tegallalang, and finish with the dance at Ubud Palace at 7:30pm. If guests are staying in Uluwatu, the drive back that night can take two to three hours at busy times, so consider a night in Ubud. If your group leans more towards beaches and rice fields in the west, see also our guide to [Nuanu and Tabanan after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/wisata-nuanu-tabanan-setelah-menikah-di-bali).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Indonesian citizens pay different ticket prices in Ubud?

At Monkey Forest, yes. Since 1 April 2026, KTP holders pay IDR 90,000 per adult, against IDR 130,000 for foreign visitors. For the other places in this article, we found no separate domestic rate.

### Is a guide mandatory for the Mount Batur trek?

The sources disagree. Some say it is mandatory and that hikers without a guide may be turned away, while another calls it strongly advised. The safe choice is to use a local guide.

### When is the best time to visit the Kintamani cafés?

From 9am to 11:30am. The sunrise crowds have gone and the lake view is at its clearest, before clouds usually arrive after 1pm.

### What about the one-way streets in Ubud?

Since 8 September 2026, several main streets in central Ubud have been one-way and tour buses must stop at Dalem Puri. Allow extra time, follow the signs and officers, and use a driver who knows the new layout.
