Behind Mario Caesar and Givina Lukita Dewi stood a large green woven installation, flanked by red, pink and yellow flowers, with strands of deep-red blooms hanging down. In the photo Mario posted on Sunday evening, 20 September 2026, at 20:06 WIB, the two of them look overjoyed. Givina wears a white mermaid gown and a long sheer veil; Mario wears a dark suit and a black peci.

The caption is short: "Alhamdulillah, bahagia sekali hari ini. Sabar, besok kami cerita ya 🤍" ("Alhamdulillah, we are so happy today. Be patient, we'll tell you tomorrow 🤍"). They kept that promise. On Monday afternoon, 21 September, Givina posted the full story on Instagram: why they chose that date, in front of whom the ijab kabul was spoken, and who worked on their day, from the stylist to the photographers.

The wedding was held in a restaurant, with a very small guest list. If you are planning a small wedding of your own, those two posts read like a tidy set of planning notes.

## What we know

- **Sunday, 20 September 2026:** an Islamic marriage contract (ijab kabul), followed by a reception in the evening.
- **Venue:** KAUM Restaurant, Jakarta (@kaumrestaurant).
- **Guests:** limited to family and close friends.
- **Givina's attire:** a white mermaid gown designed by Rama Dauhan.
- **Mario's attire:** a dark, collarless, beskap-cut suit, a white shirt and a black peci.
- **Decor:** Sekala Mekar.
- **Photography:** Roll Moments and Roll Portraits.
- **Mahar (dowry):** not disclosed by the couple.

## Who are Mario Caesar and Givina Lukita Dewi

Mario Caesar (@caesarmario) is a member of NPD (New Pusat Dunia), a pop-comedy trio with Niky Putra and Kak Kev (Kev Luqman). He is the son of actor Fendy Pradana, and his brother is Marco Ivanos. The singer Raisa Andriana is his cousin.

Givina Lukita Dewi (@givina__) was born on 7 September 1996. In 2017 she was a Miss Indonesia finalist representing Banten and reached the top 15. Givina is the younger sister of the comedian Uus, whose full name is Rizky Firdaus Wijaksana (@uusbiasaaja).

The two had been together for about four years before they married. Givina put it in everyday language: "Setelah 4 taun berusaha mengenal satu sama lain" ("After 4 years of trying to get to know each other").

## A date chosen four years to the day

Givina explained the choice of 20 September on behalf of both of them: "Kami memilih tanggal itu sebagai hari pernikahan kami karena bertepatan 4 tahun setelah Givina bilang 'Tembak aku dong biar official'." ("We chose that date as our wedding day because it falls exactly 4 years after Givina said, 'Ask me out already, so it's official.'") Their wedding day fell exactly four years after Givina said those words.

They also kept their wedding plans quiet on purpose. In the same post, Givina cited advice from Habib Isa Alkaf (@isalkaf) as the reason they told few people before the day. The couple shared the news themselves through Mario's post on the evening of the wedding.

## An akad in a restaurant, a reception in the evening

According to Givina, the ijab kabul was spoken "didepan @uusbiasaaja & @marcoivanos12" ("in front of @uusbiasaaja & @marcoivanos12"), that is, her brother and Mario's brother. Mario, in a post two days later, described the ijab kabul as marking "dimulainya ibadah panjang gua & Givina" ("the beginning of the long act of worship for me & Givina"). No source names a regional adat tradition in the ceremony.

Choosing a restaurant rather than a hotel ballroom changes how the decor works. With a limited guest list, the decor does not have to fill a large hall. Sekala Mekar made a single backdrop the focal point of the akad area: the large green woven installation with colourful flowers on both sides.

In the evening the day moved on to the reception, and Givina changed her look.

## Two looks for the akad and the reception

For the akad, Givina wore a white mermaid gown by Rama Dauhan covered in sequins and crystals, with sheer lace sleeves in a floral motif. Her hair was in a low, sleek bun with a white flower, under a long sheer veil.

At the reception, Givina wore a lighter look: a white sleeveless gown with a simpler silhouette and a satin skirt. If you also want two looks but would rather not order two gowns, ask your designer at the first fitting whether the sleeves or a skirt layer can be made detachable.

Mario wore a dark, collarless, beskap-cut suit, a white shirt and a black peci, with a small flower brooch on his chest.

On Tuesday morning, 22 September, Mario posted something more personal. He wrote that he had visited his mother's grave "2 hari sebelum menikah" ("2 days before getting married"), and then: "Dalam kondisi undangan pernikahan kami yang sangat terbatas, Givina dengan lapang hati mengabulkan permintaan gua untuk menyisakan 2 kursi kosong untuk 2 wanita kesayangan gua; Mama & Ibu Ria." ## What you can borrow

- **Choose a restaurant for a small guest list.** For an akad and reception attended only by family and close friends, a restaurant can feel warmer than a half-empty ballroom. Ask early whether the space can be closed to the public, whether outside decor vendors are allowed in, and how much time is available to set up the decor.
- **Use a date that means something to you both.** An anniversary, the day you first met, or the day someone said something important can become your wedding date. Check which day of the week it falls on, then register with the KUA and book the venue well ahead, because that date cannot be moved.
- **Make room for loved ones who have passed away.** An empty chair, a small photo, or the late person's favourite flowers can be a way to remember them on a happy day. Talk it through with your partner and family first, then tell your WO or whoever handles seating so that the chair is not taken by a guest.
- **Plan two looks for the akad and the reception.** If the budget does not stretch to a second gown, detachable sleeves or an overskirt can give one gown a new look for the reception.
- **Name your vendors in your wedding post.** Prepare a list of roles and Instagram accounts before the day, and ask each vendor to confirm the spelling of their handle. One underscore too few or too many can point to someone else's account.
