Amanda Rigby only came to the akad table once the ijab kabul was done. Andre Taulany had already spoken his kabul without a stumble, and when Amanda arrived she took her husband's hand and kissed it. This was on the morning of Sunday, 20 September 2026, at Hotel The Dharmawangsa in Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta.

The same day, Andre posted photos of the akad on Instagram with the caption: "Alhamdulillah pada hari ini, Minggu, 20 September 2026, kami telah melaksanakan akad nikah dan mengikat janji suci dengan restu dari kedua orang tua, anak anak dan keluarga, serta orang-orang tercinta." (Roughly: today we held our akad nikah and made our sacred vow, with the blessing of our parents, the children, our families and our loved ones.) That evening, at the same hotel, they held their reception.

Nearly four weeks on, what makes this wedding worth studying for couples planning their own is the planning: a mahar whose amount comes from the date, the akad and the reception on one day at one venue, both akad outfits from one designer, and a vendor list written by the groom himself.

## What we know

- **Date:** Sunday, 20 September 2026. The akad in the morning, the reception that evening.
- **Venue:** Hotel The Dharmawangsa, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta, for both the akad and the reception.
- **Ceremony:** an Islamic akad nikah with Javanese adat styling.
- **Mahar:** Rp20,092,026 in cash and a seperangkat alat salat (a set of prayer garments and a prayer mat).
- **Akad attire:** beskap and kebaya designed by Didiet Maulana for Svarna by IKAT Indonesia.
- **Reception gown:** by Hian Tjen.
- **Guests:** about 500, as reported by Liputan6 and Akurat. The event was closed, with tight security.
- **Groomsmen:** members of the motorbike gang The Prediksi, which Andre leads.

## Who Andre Taulany and Amanda Rigby are

Andre Taulany, registered at the KUA (the religious affairs office that records Muslim marriages) as Andreas Taulany, is best known as the former vocalist of Stinky. These days he is more often seen in the cast of *Lapor Pak!*, and he is also the leader of the motorbike gang The Prediksi. His Instagram account is @andreastaulany.

Amanda Lintang Larasati Rigby was born in Jakarta. She is an actress (*Kejar Tayang*), a presenter, including sports programmes, as well as a model and a businesswoman. She is of British and Javanese descent, and holds British citizenship. Her Instagram account is @amandarigby6.

They met as colleagues in television, and social media users took to pairing them up.

## A letter from the KUA, and an earlier date

The first sign came from the KUA. On Friday, 14 August 2026, a marriage recommendation letter (surat rekomendasi nikah) issued by KUA Ciputat Timur was logged at KUA Kebayoran Baru through SIMKAH, the Ministry of Religious Affairs' marriage registration system. The news only spread a month later, when the head of KUA Kebayoran Baru, Riswanto, confirmed it to Okezone on Monday, 14 September.

Andre himself had said the wedding would be "antara Oktober atau pertengahan Oktober", sometime in October or mid-October. So the 20 September date came as a surprise to many.

There is a practical lesson here. If your akad is held in a KUA district other than the one where one of you is registered, you will usually need a recommendation letter from your home KUA. For Andre and Amanda, that letter was logged five weeks before the wedding. Ask your KUA how long the process takes, and sort it out well before the invitations are printed.

## A Javanese akad and a Rp20,092,026 mahar

The akad was an Islamic ceremony with Javanese adat styling. The order is worth noting: the ijab kabul was spoken first, without the bride at the akad table, and only then did Amanda come in to meet her husband. The photos in Andre's post show what followed: the wedding rings, the buku nikah (marriage book), and the couple asking their parents for their blessing.

The mahar was Rp20,092,026 in cash plus a seperangkat alat salat. The number is the wedding date itself: 20, 09, 2026, or 20 September 2026. The mahar amount doubles as a record of the akad day, read out again every time the buku nikah is opened. [Luna Maya and Maxime Bouttier](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-luna-maya-maxime-bouttier-bali-jakarta) also worked the year of their wedding into their mahar.

The idea is easy to fit to any budget. A wedding on 7 June 2027, for example, could use Rp7,062,027. If that is more than you want to give, use only part of the date, and agree on the form with your partner and both families early on.

Through their management, the couple released a written statement: "Kami sangat bersyukur atas doa dan dukungan yang telah diberikan oleh keluarga, sahabat, serta masyarakat. Hari ini menjadi salah satu momen paling berharga dalam hidup kami." (We are deeply grateful for the prayers and support of our families, friends and the public. Today is one of the most precious moments of our lives.) They also asked for privacy.

## Both akad outfits from one designer

Didiet Maulana designed both akad outfits under his label Svarna by IKAT Indonesia. Andre credited it himself in his caption, and Didiet also shared Andre's akad look on his own account.

Andre wore a white beskap, kain batik, a blangkon and a melati garland, along with his glasses. Amanda wore a fitted white kebaya with embroidery and payet (sequin) work, a silver brooch at the centre of the chest, a diamond necklace, kain batik and a long sheer veil. Her hair was in a sanggul with ronce melati (jasmine strands), cunduk mentul hairpins and red roses at the side. Her make-up was soft glam by make-up artist Leo Afandi, paired with black Javanese paes on her forehead.

Because one designer handled both outfits, the white tones and embroidery match in every photo. For a couple the gain is practical too: one fitting schedule, one person to contact, and no matching fabrics across two boutiques. In the background of the colour photo in Andre's post you can see blue hydrangeas, white orchids and hanging strands of melati.

## The reception that same evening

In the evening, Amanda changed into an ivory off-shoulder gown by Hian Tjen, in lace with pearl and payet accents. Her hair was styled in a sleek low bun, with more glamorous make-up.

Members of The Prediksi served as groomsmen, in matching brown suits. Sule came as a guest, not as a groomsman. He later shrugged it off: in his view, Andre probably did not want to put him to any trouble.

Other guests reported by more than one outlet include Raffi Ahmad and Nagita Slavina, Ariel NOAH, Irwansyah and Zaskia Sungkar, Denny Cagur, Ayu Ting Ting, Hesti Purwadinata, Parto Patrio, and [El Rumi and Syifa Hadju](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-el-rumi-syifa-hadju-raffles-jakarta).

The moment people retold most came from Andre's children. They were at the reception and hugged the couple at the pelaminan (the bridal dais). In his speech, Andre said nothing made him happier that night than having his children there, then asked them to welcome Amanda as "Mama Amanda". After the formal programme there was an after party, and The Prediksi took over the stage.

Holding the akad and the reception at one venue on the same day has real advantages. Your families do not have to move between locations, the make-up and photo teams stay put, and guests invited only to the reception do not need to come in the morning. The trade-off is a long day. Leave a gap of a few hours between the two events to eat, rest, and change outfits and make-up.

## The vendors named in Andre's post

In the caption of his akad post, Andre listed his vendors himself: "Wedding organizer : @amoretorganizer / Photo & video : @derai.studio / Make up : @leoafandi_ / Kebaya & beskap : @svarna_byikatindonesia". That is Amoret Organizer as wedding organizer, Derai Studio for photo and video, Leo Afandi for make-up, and Svarna by IKAT Indonesia for the akad attire. Leo Afandi later shared the details of the products he used on Amanda on his own account.

A list like this answers the question that always comes up in the comments, and it lets the media quote the credits correctly. For vendors, a credit from the couple is the promotion people trust most.

## What you can borrow

- **Turn your wedding date into the mahar amount.** Write the day, month and year in sequence, as in Rp20,092,026, or use only part of it to suit your budget, and add a seperangkat alat salat.
- **Hold the akad and the reception on the same day at the same venue.** A morning akad for family, an evening reception for the wider guest list, with enough of a gap to rest and change.
- **Choose one designer for both akad outfits.** The beskap and kebaya will match in photos, and you only have one fitting schedule to manage.
- **Name your vendors in your announcement post.** One line per vendor with their Instagram handle is enough, and it helps friends who are looking for vendors of their own.
