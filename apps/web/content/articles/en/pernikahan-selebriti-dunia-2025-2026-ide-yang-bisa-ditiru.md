Between mid-2025 and mid-2026, five famous couples married in places far apart: Venice, Santa Barbara, Seoul, London and Sicily, then New York. The scale and budgets are a world away from most weddings. Look closer, though, and the most interesting decisions turn out to be cheap, and some are already familiar from Indonesian wedding customs.

This article doesn't go through the couples one by one. We've grouped them by idea, so you can take whatever fits. For the full story of each wedding, follow the link to its own article.

## The weddings

- **Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez:** Friday, 27 June 2025, San Giorgio Maggiore, Venice.
- **Selena Gomez and Benny Blanco:** Saturday, 27 September 2025, Sea Crest Nursery, Santa Barbara.
- **Kim Woo-bin and Shin Min-a:** Saturday, 20 December 2025, The Shilla Seoul.
- **Dua Lipa and Callum Turner:** Sunday, 31 May 2026 at Old Marylebone Town Hall, London, then Saturday, 6 June 2026 at Villa Valguarnera, Bagheria, Sicily.
- **Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce:** Friday, 3 July 2026, Madison Square Garden, New York.

## Gowns that carry a story

Lauren Sánchez's Dolce & Gabbana gown was high-necked, long-sleeved lace, with 180 buttons covered in silk chiffon. Its reference was the dress Sophia Loren wore in the film "Houseboat" (1958). Stefano Gabbana described it as a classic, elegant wedding and said Lauren didn't want anything flashy.

The story in [Selena Gomez](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-selena-gomez-benny-blanco-santa-barbara)'s dress was more hidden. Inside the corset of her Ralph Lauren ceremony dress was an embroidered heart with "S + B" and the wedding date. No guest could see it.

You can use both approaches at once. When you meet your designer or kebaya tailor, bring one reference that means something, such as a photo of your mother's or grandmother's kebaya on their wedding day. Then ask for one small detail only you know about, like your initials and date stitched on the inside.

## One day, several looks

Indonesian brides are used to changing outfits between the akad and the reception. Brides abroad, it turns out, do much the same, for equally practical reasons.

Selena wore three Ralph Lauren looks: one for the pre-ceremony portraits, one for the ceremony and a tulle dress for the reception. Lauren Sánchez changed into an Oscar de la Renta dress covered in 175,000 crystals for the after-party. [Dua Lipa](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-dua-lipa-callum-turner-london-sicilia) wore a Schiaparelli suit in London, Bottega Veneta for the welcome cocktails in Palermo, and a Chanel Haute Couture gown with about 480,000 beads for the ceremony in Sicily.

The lesson is in the order. The most elaborate outfit goes to the most sacred and most photographed moment. The party outfit is made so the bride can move. If your budget only stretches to one special outfit, put it at the moment that matters most to your family, then choose something simpler and more comfortable for the rest of the day.

## The two-part wedding

Dua Lipa and Callum Turner married in a civil ceremony at Old Marylebone Town Hall on 31 May 2026. Almost a week later they celebrated in Sicily for three days, from 5 to 7 June, with about 200 guests.

The pattern is almost the same as an Indonesian couple who marry at the KUA (the religious affairs office) or register at the civil registry with close family, then hold the reception in another city. The advantages are real. The paperwork is done first, and the reception can be planned without rushing.

The Bezos and Sánchez wedding also ran over several days, from 26 to 28 June 2025. Thursday brought a welcome reception in the cloister of Madonna dell'Orto, and Saturday closed with a pyjama party. In Indonesia, a sequence like this could be a family dinner before the akad and a relaxed gathering for close friends after the reception.

## Siblings beside the couple

[Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-taylor-swift-travis-kelce-madison-square-garden) had no bridesmaids or groomsmen. Austin Swift was man of honor and Jason Kelce was best man. Both are the couple's siblings.

Lauren Sánchez also gave her children the main roles. Her two sons, Nikko and Evan, walked her down the aisle, and her daughter, Ella, was maid of honour.

Indonesian families usually involve brothers, sisters and cousins in a wedding already. What you can copy is the confidence to stop there. Without a line of bridesmaids you don't have to choose between friends, and the important roles stay with family.

## Donations instead of gifts

Bezos and Sánchez asked guests not to bring gifts. Instead, they donated to the UNESCO Venice Office, CORILA and Venice International University. Kim Woo-bin and Shin Min-a also marked their marriage with donations, to the Hallym Burn Foundation, Asan Medical Center and Good Friends.

For Indonesian couples this can work in a simple way. Note on the invitation that you won't accept gifts or flower boards, then name one organisation you'd like to support, such as an orphanage near your parents' home or a foundation linked to your family's experience. Talk it through with your parents first, because in many families the envelopes from guests are still seen as part of the tradition.

## The hand-made invitation

Kim Woo-bin and Shin Min-a made their wedding invitation themselves. Shin Min-a drew the illustration and Kim Woo-bin did the lettering. They had been together for about ten years before marrying, and the invitation feels like the work of two people who have known each other a long time.

You don't need to be good at drawing to copy this. A simple sketch of your first home, your two names in your own handwriting, or a small drawing of the place you first met can be scanned and printed by an invitation printer. It can also go on a digital invitation, which many Indonesian couples now send to guests who live far away.

## Lily of the valley at two weddings

Selena Gomez and Shin Min-a both carried bouquets of lily of the valley, a small white bell-shaped flower. Shin Min-a's dress came from Elie Saab's Spring 2026 bridal collection, with a strapless sweetheart bodice decorated with three-dimensional tulle and organza flowers.

A bouquet of one small flower looks neat and calm, especially in photos. If that exact flower is hard to find or wilts quickly in tropical weather, ask your florist for a small white flower that's available locally. What you're copying is the effect, and the flower itself can change.

## A few guests or a thousand

Guest numbers across the five weddings varied widely. Selena and Benny invited about 170 people. Bezos and Sánchez had about 200, around 70 of them family. Dua Lipa and Callum Turner also had about 200 in Sicily. Kim Woo-bin and Shin Min-a married privately at 7 p.m. Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce, on the other hand, invited about 1,000 people to Madison Square Garden, while the rehearsal dinner the day before had only about 100.

That figure of 1,000 is close to the size of a ballroom reception in Indonesia. The way they entertained that many guests is familiar too: food from the couple's favourite restaurants and a raffle, or in our terms, gubukan (food stalls) and a doorprize draw.

The more useful pattern is how events are divided. Many couples abroad separate a small event for their nearest people from a large celebration. In Indonesia that division already exists naturally between the akad and the reception. You can use it on purpose: the akad, pengajian or church blessing for family and close friends, the reception for everyone who wants to share the happiness.

## What you can borrow

- Bring one personal reference when designing your outfit, and ask for one hidden detail such as embroidered initials and a date.
- Decide what each outfit is for before ordering, then save the most special one for the most important moment.
- Consider a two-part format: an akad or civil registration near home, a reception in another city.
- Give the main roles to siblings or children, and skip the line of bridesmaids if you don't need it.
- Replace gifts with a donation to an organisation linked to your story, once your parents agree.
- Make part of the invitation yourselves, even if it's only handwriting or a simple sketch.
- Choose a single small white flower for the bouquet.
