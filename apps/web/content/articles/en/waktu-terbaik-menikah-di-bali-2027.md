Almost every couple planning a Bali wedding starts with the same question: which month is best? The short answer is May to September, the dry season. The more useful answer depends on what matters most to you, whether that is the weather for a clifftop ceremony, venue prices and availability, guests flying in from Australia with children, or your own religious calendar.

This article sets it all out month by month for 2027: average rainfall, Balinese holy days, Indonesian public holidays and Australian school holidays. To check a particular date, use our [Bali Wedding Date Planner](https://7magicwedding.com/en/wedding-tools/bali-wedding-date-planner), which draws on the same sources.

## Two seasons, not four

Bali has two seasons. Climate averages for the Ngurah Rai station, near the airport, record under 70 mm of rain a month from June to October, and over 200 mm a month from December to March. The average temperature barely changes through the year, at around 26 to 28°C.

For the 2026/2027 wet season, BMKG, Indonesia's weather agency, expects the rain in Bali to start in late November or December 2026 and last until about April 2027, peaking in February. Ubud and the uplands are wetter than the south coast.

The wet season in Bali usually means heavy afternoon downpours, not rain all day. Plenty of weddings go smoothly in these months, provided the venue has a real plan B, not just an emergency marquee.

## Dates to avoid

**Nyepi, Monday 8 March 2027.** For 24 hours from 6am the whole of Bali stops and Ngurah Rai Airport closes. No wedding can be held, and guests cannot arrive or leave that day. Nyepi 2027 also runs straight into the Eid al-Fitr holiday, so 6 to 15 March is one of the busiest travel weeks of the year in Indonesia. Some travel sites give Nyepi 2027 as 9 March. The correct date, per the government decree, is 8 March.

**Galungan and Kuningan.** These are the biggest holy days for Balinese Hindus. Schools and offices usually close from the day before to the day after, and many local staff and vendors are with their families. You can still marry around these dates, but ask about your team's availability early. Galungan on 13 January 2027 already appears in a calendar published by an institution in Bali. Galungan on 11 August 2027 is calculated from the 210-day cycle, so check it against a Balinese calendar before you lock in a date.

**Your own religious calendar.** Catholic couples cannot marry during Lent (10 February to 27 March 2027) or Advent (from 28 November 2027) under the Diocese of Denpasar's policy, as we explain in our article on [Christian and Catholic weddings in Bali](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/pemberkatan-nikah-kristen-katolik-di-bali). Muslim couples usually avoid Ramadan, and the window before Ramadan 2027 is narrow, as we cover in our article on [an akad nikah in Bali](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-islami/akad-nikah-di-bali-kua-wali-venue). Balinese Hindu families choose the day by [dewasa ayu](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/dewasa-ayu-pawiwahan-hari-baik-menikah-bali), the Balinese good-day calendar.

## Choosing by priority

**If the weather for an outdoor ceremony comes first.** June to September. July and August are the driest, but also the busiest, so book your venue and vendors well ahead.

**If you want it dry but quieter.** May, September or October. Rainfall is still low and you avoid the July peak.

**If your Australian guests are bringing children.** Australian school holidays in 2027 fall from late March to late April, from late June to mid-July, and from mid-September to early October. The exact dates differ between states, and our date planner shows NSW, Victoria, Queensland and Western Australia.

**If budget comes first.** The wet season, November to March outside Christmas and New Year, usually offers more venue availability. Choose a venue with an indoor or covered space that can genuinely hold all your guests. ## If you choose the wet season anyway

- Schedule the ceremony for the morning or midday. Rain in Bali tends to come in the afternoon.
- Make sure the venue's plan B can hold your guest count, fully decorated, not an empty room.
- Have umbrellas for guests and set a realistic dress code. Our guest guide is [what to wear to a Bali wedding in the wet season](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/beauty-fashion/baju-kondangan-ke-bali-musim-hujan).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month to get married in Bali?

For weather, June to September, with July and August the driest. May and October are often a good compromise between weather and crowds.

### When is Nyepi in 2027?

Monday 8 March 2027, per the government's 2027 public holiday decree. There are no weddings and no flights that day.

### Is a wet-season wedding risky?

The risk is not rain all day but heavy afternoon downpours. With the right timing and a venue with a real plan B, many wet-season weddings go well.

### When is Galungan in 2027?

13 January and, by the 210-day cycle, 11 August 2027. Kuningan falls ten days after each.
