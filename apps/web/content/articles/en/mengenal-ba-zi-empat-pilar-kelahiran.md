As soon as a lamaran date comes up, someone in many Chinese Indonesian families will ask, "What time were they born?" The question often puzzles the couple. The zodiac sign only needs the year of birth, so why the hour?

Because what the family wants to see is not just the sign but the Ba Zi. The zodiac sign we all know is only one character out of eight. This article explains what those eight characters are, how they are worked out, and why the result sometimes differs from the sign you have always believed was yours.

## What Ba Zi is

Ba Zi (八字) literally means "eight characters". It reads a person's moment of birth as four pillars: the year pillar, the month pillar, the day pillar and the hour pillar. Hence its other name, the Four Pillars (四柱, Si Zhu), and in English-language writing it is often called the Four Pillars of Destiny.

Each pillar is made of two characters. On top sits one **heavenly stem**, below it one **earthly branch**. Four pillars times two characters makes eight.

Its roots are old. Dating with pairs of stems and branches already appears on Shang dynasty oracle bones. As a way of reading fate, the three-pillar method (year, month, day) is traditionally credited to Li Xuzhong in the Tang dynasty. The hour pillar was added by Xu Ziping in the Song dynasty, which is why the method still used today is often called the Ziping method.

## The ten heavenly stems

There are ten heavenly stems. Each carries one of the five elements, in a Yang or a Yin form.

| Stem | Pinyin | Element |
|---|---|---|
| 甲 | Jia | Yang Wood |
| 乙 | Yi | Yin Wood |
| 丙 | Bing | Yang Fire |
| 丁 | Ding | Yin Fire |
| 戊 | Wu | Yang Earth |
| 己 | Ji | Yin Earth |
| 庚 | Geng | Yang Metal |
| 辛 | Xin | Yin Metal |
| 壬 | Ren | Yang Water |
| 癸 | Gui | Yin Water |

The easiest way to picture Yang and Yin here: 甲 is a great tree, 乙 a climbing vine. Both are wood, with very different natures.

## The twelve earthly branches, or the zodiac

There are twelve earthly branches, and these are what everyone knows as the twelve zodiac animals. Each branch has an element too.

| Branch | Sign | Element |
|---|---|---|
| 子 | Rat | Water |
| 丑 | Ox | Earth |
| 寅 | Tiger | Wood |
| 卯 | Rabbit | Wood |
| 辰 | Dragon | Earth |
| 巳 | Snake | Fire |
| 午 | Horse | Fire |
| 未 | Goat | Earth |
| 申 | Monkey | Metal |
| 酉 | Rooster | Metal |
| 戌 | Dog | Earth |
| 亥 | Pig | Water |

Stems and branches are paired in order, ten against twelve, until they return to the start after sixty pairs. This cycle of sixty names the years, months, days and hours. The year 2026, for instance, is 丙午: Yang Fire over the Horse. That is why 2026 is widely called the year of the Fire Horse.

## The five elements and how they relate

The five elements (五行, wu xing) relate to one another in two cycles. In the generating cycle, wood feeds fire, fire makes earth (ash), earth bears metal, metal gathers water (dew), and water nourishes wood. In the controlling cycle, wood breaks through earth, earth dams water, water puts out fire, fire melts metal, and metal cuts wood.

When a Ba Zi practitioner reads a chart, they look at which elements are plentiful, which are lacking, and how they push against one another. Someone whose chart has almost no wood, for example, is read differently from someone whose chart is full of it.

## The day master: the character that stands for you

Of the eight characters, one gets the most attention: the heavenly stem of the day pillar. It is called the day master (日主, ri zhu). In Ba Zi the day master is taken as the closest picture of the person, and the other seven characters are read in relation to it.

This is the part that surprises people most. Two friends born in the same year share a zodiac sign, but their day masters are most likely different, because the day pillar changes every day. That sixty-day cycle has run without a break for more than two thousand years, untouched by months or years.

## Why the year turns around 4 February, not at Chinese New Year

This is where Ba Zi most often parts ways with the zodiac calendar on the wall. Ba Zi does not use the lunar months and years. It uses the solar calendar: the 24 solar terms (节气, jie qi) that divide the Sun's yearly path into slices of 15 degrees.

The Ba Zi year turns at Lichun (立春, the start of spring), when the Sun reaches a longitude of 315 degrees. That falls around 4 February, give or take a day. Ba Zi months do not follow the moon either. Each month begins at one of twelve solar terms, and the first month, the month of the Tiger, begins exactly at Lichun.

A concrete example from this year. Lichun 2026 fell on 4 February 2026 at 03:02 WIB, while Chinese New Year fell on 17 February. A baby born on 10 February 2026 is already a Horse in Ba Zi, even though the lunar calendar at home still showed the year of the Snake.

The two ways of counting do live side by side. Ba Zi practitioners use Lichun. If you were born in January or early February and your sign in a Ba Zi chart differs from the one you know, this is almost certainly why.

## Why the hour of birth matters

The hour pillar divides the day into twelve Chinese hours of two hours each. The hour of the Rat begins at 23:00, so in Ba Zi someone born at 23:30 already counts as born on the next day. This small detail is often missed, and it makes the day master come out wrong.

The hour also matters if you were born on a day when the Sun enters a new term. On Lichun day, for example, babies born at 02:00 and at 05:00 can have different Ba Zi years. If you do not know your hour of birth, Ba Zi can still be worked out with three pillars. The result is less complete but still useful.

One more thing particular to Indonesia: use the time zone written on the birth certificate. Bali and parts of Kalimantan changed time zones in 1988, so for births before then, the time on the certificate follows the zone in force at the time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Ba Zi the same as feng shui?

No. Both come from the same tradition of Chinese metaphysics and both use the five elements, but they read different things. Ba Zi reads a person's moment of birth. Feng shui reads a place, such as a home or an office. Many practitioners do both, so the two are often mentioned together.

### Do people born on the same day have the same Ba Zi?

Almost always the first three pillars match, but the hour pillar can differ. Two people born on the same day in different Chinese hours have different hour pillars, and the reading shifts with them. The exceptions are days when the Sun enters a new term, and births after 23:00.

### My sign in Ba Zi differs from the one I always knew. Which is right?

Both, each by its own way of counting. To read Ba Zi, including a couple's compatibility, the sign by Lichun is the one used. Yearly zodiac forecasts in magazines or calendars usually switch at Chinese New Year.

### Does Ba Zi conflict with religion?

That comes down to each family's beliefs. Many Chinese Indonesian families, Christian, Catholic and Buddhist among them, treat Ba Zi as part of their culture and a way of honouring their parents, not as a matter of faith. Others choose not to use it. Both are perfectly reasonable.
