The reception is over, your guests are still on the island, and the two of you finally have a free day. If your wedding was in Canggu or on the southwest coast, Tabanan is the closest place to spend the day after. Nuanu Creative City is about 10 minutes from Tanah Lot, and its best programme starts after sunset.

This article covers what to do in Nuanu and Tabanan, which parts suit newlyweds and which suit a group of guests, the 2026 ticket prices for Indonesian and foreign visitors, and two sample day plans. Every price below is quoted from the listed source, with its date, and may change at any time.

## Getting to know Nuanu Creative City

Nuanu is a 44-hectare site on the shore of Pantai Nyanyi in Beraban, Kediri, Tabanan. Its official site still describes it as "under development" and gives distances of 10 minutes from Tanah Lot, 20 minutes from Canggu and 60 minutes from the airport. Bali Untold puts Canggu at about 30 minutes, so allow extra time when the roads are busy.

At its heart are several free art installations, Earth Sentinels, Aurora Media Park and Magic Garden, surrounded by paid venues such as Luna Beach Club, Labyrinth Dome, the Lumeira spa, Alpha Wolf Lodge and Pacha Alpaca. There is a large car park near the entrance, a free shuttle links the pick-up points, and every venue takes cards.

For site entry, Bali Untold (June 2026) lists prices for international visitors of IDR 75,000 on weekdays and IDR 150,000 at weekends at the gate. Online tickets are cheaper: IDR 20,000 for the first 100 buyers each day, then IDR 50,000. Children under 12 enter free, and pre-booked paid activities usually include site entry. We found no separate rate for Indonesian citizens on site entry. Opening hours also differ between sources: Trip.com says 8am to 11pm, Traveloka 7:45am to 11pm, and the official site does not list them.

## Nuanu for newlyweds

**Lumeira.** This wellness complex has a wood-fired sauna, a domed hammam, cold plunges, jacuzzis, a pool, and massages and scrubs. According to Bali.live (May 2025), the complex opens 8am to 10pm and treatments run 10am to 9pm. A wet-area day ticket was then IDR 350,000 per adult plus 21% tax. That figure is 18 months old, so ask for the current price. Treatments must be booked and paid for in advance.

**Aurora Media Park and Earth Sentinels.** Aurora is a 1 km forest trail lined with sculptures, and after 6:30pm its AI-driven light and sound installations switch on, some of them triggered by movement. Allow about an hour and bring insect repellent. Earth Sentinels, two giant faces made of concrete and living plants, get projection mapping in the evening. Both are free.

**Labyrinth Dome.** This 360-degree dome theatre shows digital art, meditation sessions, planetarium-style films and live performances. Prices and schedules vary widely between ticket sellers, from IDR 150,000 to IDR 300,000, and one tiket.com listing shows a lower domestic price than the overseas one. Check the schedule for the day you go.

**The Art & Bali exhibition.** The flagship exhibition *What The Body Remembers* runs at Labyrinth Art Gallery until 29 November 2026. If you are marrying this week, there is still time to see it.

For dinner, Bali Untold names Restaurant 369 in the Luna area as the high-end choice. Outside Nuanu there is Nusantara (Warung Nine Islands) in Beraban, serving Indonesian grills and seafood from noon to 10pm, and Little Spoon Joglo Cafe & Farm on Jalan Pantai Nyanyi.

## Nuanu for a group of guests

**Luna Beach Club** is the easiest option for a group. It has a lagoon pool, Luna Beer Garden, known for its BBQ, Utopia Cave Club with an infinity pool, swim-up bar and water slide, and THK Tower with 3D projection mapping. It is family-friendly by day, with DJs and fire dancers at night.

Headout sells general admission at IDR 273,200, non-refundable. The ticket includes one alcoholic or two non-alcoholic drinks, a towel, Nuanu entry, and the daily fire dance and light show at 6:45pm. If your group wants a daybed or a reserved table, ask the venue directly, because minimum-spend terms are not officially published.

**For families with children.** Alpha Wolf Lodge cares for rescued wolves and wolf-dog hybrids, with "meet the wolves" sessions from IDR 210,000. Pacha Alpaca has seven alpacas and a session of about 20 minutes from IDR 150,000. Magic Garden, with its orchid and butterfly greenhouses, is free. There is also Jungle Kids day care, so parents can go to the spa.

## Tanah Lot and Kedungu in the late afternoon

Tanah Lot is a 16th-century sea temple still in active worship, and it drew more than 1.4 million visits in 2025, 52% of them domestic. The walk from the car park to the temple takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Ticket prices rose on 1 April 2026; see the table below. Kecak dance tickets at Tanah Lot can be bought through the SatuSatu app; check the app for show times and prices.

Kedungu Beach, not far from Tanah Lot, has black sand and left and right waves suited to beginners and intermediates. The best surf comes in the dry season, roughly May to September. The shorebreak is strong and there are sea urchins on the reef at low tide, so wear shoes if you walk on the rocks. The main draw is the sunset light on the wet black sand.

## Jatiluwih and Ulun Danu Beratan

These two highland sights take a full day, so they work best as a separate day with a hired car. Ulun Danu Beratan in Bedugul opens 7am to 6pm according to Traveloka. Bali.live reports that the price rise of 1 July 2026 came with added dance performances.

Jatiluwih has been through a rough patch. On 2 December 2025, a Bali provincial parliament task force found 13 buildings allegedly breaching farmland-protection rules, and several businesses were sealed. Tabanan's deputy regent cited a serious UNESCO warning about the site's World Heritage status as the reason. In protest, farmers put up zinc sheeting and black plastic that blocked the view of the rice terraces.

The farmers took it down on 5 January 2026 after the Tabanan government offered a moratorium. Visits had fallen by up to 80%. During the July 2026 school holidays, domestic visits rose by 50 to 60%. We have found no recent news on its UNESCO status, so check the current situation before you go.

|---|---|---|---|
| Tanah Lot | IDR 40,000 | IDR 100,000 | From 1 April 2026 |
| Ulun Danu Beratan | IDR 50,000 | IDR 100,000 | From 1 July 2026 |
| Jatiluwih | IDR 25,000 (Bali residents IDR 15,000) | IDR 75,000 | Date not confirmed |

Child prices at Tanah Lot are IDR 25,000 for Indonesians and IDR 60,000 for foreigners; at Ulun Danu Beratan, IDR 25,000 and IDR 75,000. Parking at Tanah Lot is IDR 4,000 for a motorbike, IDR 10,000 for a car and IDR 20,000 for a bus. Bring your KTP so you can pay the domestic price.

## A sample day for the couple

- **Midday:** sauna, hammam and a massage at Lumeira. Book treatments a few days ahead.
- **Late afternoon:** drive about 10 minutes to Tanah Lot for the sunset, or to Kedungu if you would rather have a quieter beach.
- **After 6:30pm:** head back to Nuanu for Aurora Media Park and Earth Sentinels.
- **Evening:** dinner at Restaurant 369, or a Labyrinth Dome show if the schedule fits.

If you prefer cooler air, swap in a morning on the Jatiluwih rice-terrace trails followed by Ulun Danu Beratan, and save Nuanu for the next evening. For a longer honeymoon plan, read our [honeymoon budget guide for newlyweds](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/bulan-madu-setelah-menikah-panduan-anggaran).

## A sample day for a group of guests

- **Morning:** a beginner surf lesson at Kedungu, best in the dry season.
- **Midday:** into Luna Beach Club on a general admission ticket, BBQ at the beer garden and a swim in the lagoon pool.
- **Late afternoon:** children to Alpha Wolf Lodge or Pacha Alpaca; anyone who wants to see a temple can go to Tanah Lot.
- **6:45pm:** everyone meets back at Luna for the fire and light show, then walks over to Aurora Media Park.

If some guests are staying in Ubud, remember that central Ubud switched to a one-way system on 8 September 2026, and its first day brought more than four hours of gridlock. Give them extra time for the drive back. ## Before you go

**Foreign guests pay the tourist levy.** The IDR 150,000 levy per foreign tourist has applied since 14 February 2024 and is paid through the official Love Bali portal. Indonesian citizens do not pay it. Send the Love Bali link to your overseas guests before they fly.

**Halal food.** We found no BPJPH halal-certified restaurant in Nuanu or around Tanah Lot, and several Nuanu venues serve alcohol. Swarga Rasa Ristorante in Tabanan is a vegan restaurant, open 7am to 10pm. In nibble.id's list of new Bali restaurants for September 2026, the closest entry is Jannah on Jalan Pantai Seseh, Cemagi, just across the Tabanan border, with no halal status given. Ask the restaurant directly before you book.

**What to wear to the temples.** We found no current written rules for Tanah Lot or Ulun Danu Beratan. Bring your own sarong and sash so you do not have to hunt for one on site.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much time do we need at Nuanu?

Bali Untold recommends a full day. If you are short of time, arrive in the late afternoon and stay until dark, because the free installations only light up after 6:30pm.

### Do Indonesians pay less?

At Tanah Lot, Ulun Danu Beratan and Jatiluwih, yes, and the gap can be more than half. For Nuanu site entry, we have not found a separate rate for Indonesian citizens.

### Is Jatiluwih back to normal?

The zinc sheeting has been down since 5 January 2026, and domestic visits were climbing again by July 2026. The question of its UNESCO status is still unclear, so check the latest news before you go.

### Is Nuanu good for children?

Yes. Children under 12 enter free, there are wolves, alpacas and Magic Garden, and there is Jungle Kids day care.
