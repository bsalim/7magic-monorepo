On the morning of Saturday, 10 October 2026, at 09:01 Korean time (07:01 WIB), actress Kim Seung Yoon posted a set of wedding photos to Instagram with the caption "💍My Love • My Life • My Home💍". Less than a minute later, actor and director Jang Dong Yoon posted photos from the same shoot. His caption was almost a twin: "🤍My Love • My Muse • My Home🤍".

Only one word differs. Kim calls Jang her life, and Jang calls Kim his muse. The word has a basis: Kim starred in both films Jang has directed.

They married that same day. As announced back in August, the ceremony was held privately as a worship service, attended only by the two families. This article sticks to what the couple shared themselves and what Korean media reported.

## What we know so far

- **Tuesday, 25 August 2026:** Jang announced the wedding in a handwritten letter on Instagram. His agency, BH Entertainment, confirmed it the same day.
- **Saturday, 10 October 2026:** the wedding day, in Korea.
- **Ceremony format:** a worship service (예배) closed to the public, attended only by both families.
- **Venue:** not announced.
- **Wedding photos:** posted by the couple at 09:01 and 09:02 KST (07:01 and 07:02 WIB) on the wedding day, from a shoot done beforehand.
- **What they wore in the photos:** Jang wore a black tuxedo with a white shirt. Kim wore a white lace wedding dress and a floral hair piece, and carried a bouquet.

## Who Jang Dong Yoon and Kim Seung Yoon are

Jang Dong Yoon (often written Jang Dong-yoon) was born in 1992 and was 34 when he married. His name first made the news in 2015, when, as a Hanyang University student, he helped catch a robber at a convenience store. A year later he debuted in the web drama *Game Development Girls* (2016). He went on to appear in *School 2017*, played the lead, Jeon Nokdu, in *The Tale of Nokdu* (KBS2, 2019), then starred in *Daily Dose of Sunshine* (Netflix, 2023), *Like Flowers in Sand* (ENA, 2023) and *Queen Mantis* (SBS, 2025). He is signed to BH Entertainment. His next project is the Disney+ series *The Miracles of the Namiya General Store*, set for next year.

Kim Seung Yoon was born in 1997 and studied film at Konkuk University. Her acting debut came in *The Tale of Nokdu*, the drama Jang led. Since then she has appeared in Hong Sang-soo's film *In Water* (2023), then in *A Traveler's Needs* and *In Our Day*, the ENA series *Crash*, and the KBS2 drama *For Eagle Brothers* (2025).

Before August 2026, their relationship had never been reported. The Korea Times wrote that the two had quietly been dating for years. Exactly when they started dating has never been disclosed.

## The director and his actress

Both appeared in *The Tale of Nokdu* (2019), Kim's debut drama, and later worked together as director and actress on *Please Be My Ear* (2023) and *Nuruk* (2026).

*Please Be My Ear* (내 귀가 되어줘) is a short film Jang directed, and Kim was in its cast. *Nuruk* (누룩) is Jang's first feature as a director, released in Korean cinemas in April 2026. Kim plays its lead, Daseul, an 18-year-old from a family of brewers who loves makgeolli and goes looking for the missing nuruk, a traditional fermentation starter. Some English-language outlets call the film *The Yeast*.

Twice Jang has taken the director's chair, and twice Kim has stood in front of his camera. Against that background, the word "Muse" in his caption reads as a working fact as much as a term of affection.

## A letter in August

The news came on Tuesday, 25 August 2026, in a handwritten letter Jang posted to Instagram. Its key line is short: "저는 오는 10월, 오랜 시간 함께해 온 사람과 결혼합니다", meaning "This coming October, I will be marrying someone I have been with for a long time."

In the same letter he explained the format: "양가 가족분들을 모시고 조용히 예배로 예식을 진행할 예정입니다", roughly "we will hold a quiet ceremony in the form of a worship service, with both families in attendance." He also wrote that the two had decided to be on each other's side from the closest place and to live together "재미있고 행복하게", with fun and happiness.

BH Entertainment confirmed the news the same day. According to Sports Kyunghyang, the agency said the couple planned a quiet ceremony in October with only their families in attendance. From that day on, colleagues and fans knew the occasion was for family only.

## The wedding morning

What the public saw on that Saturday was a pictorial: wedding photos from a shoot done before the day, posted in the morning ahead of the ceremony. Each post tags the other's account: Jang is @dongyoon_0712 and Kim is @s2y0o0n.

The photos have a vintage feel, and Sports Kyunghyang described them as film-like. Jang wears a black tuxedo and a white shirt. Kim wears a white lace gown with a floral piece in her hair, holding a bouquet. One photo is framed like a four-cut photo-booth print.

Worth noting: these are shoot photos, not ceremony photos. The place of worship, the officiant, the guest list and any photos from the ceremony itself have not been made public.

## Ideas you can borrow

- **A small worship service for family only.** A church blessing does not have to be followed by a large reception. If you choose this format, say so in your announcement, as Jang did in his letter, so friends and colleagues are not left waiting for an invitation.
- **Photos finished before the day.** With a pictorial shot in advance, you can share the news on the morning without photographing a ceremony you want to keep private. Agree on a posting time with your partner so both posts go up together.
- **Matching captions.** Use three short phrases, two shared and one different for each of you. Pick a word with a story behind it, like "Muse" for Jang, then tag each other in the posts.
- **A "Thanks to" list for your vendors.** Tagging your photographer, make-up artist and florist helps them find new clients, and helps friends who are looking for vendors. Note down each vendor's Instagram account when you sign the contract, and ask whether they would like to be tagged.
- **A simple shoot concept.** A black tuxedo, a white lace gown, a bouquet and one photo-booth-style frame are enough for a vintage feel. A concept like this can be taken to any photo studio.
