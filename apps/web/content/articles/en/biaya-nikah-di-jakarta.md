The first question in the family group chat usually sounds simple: "So how much is this going to cost?" The answer rarely is. In Jakarta, getting married can cost nothing if you only go to the KUA on a weekday, and it can run into hundreds of millions of rupiah once there's a hotel reception. Almost all of that gap sits in the reception, not in the registration.

This article keeps the two apart. The first part covers the official fees set by the state, with the regulations behind them. The second covers the reception, using package prices actually listed on 7Magic rather than estimates. For other items such as extra catering, decoration or make-up, we don't guess figures. We explain what pushes the number up or down, so you can ask vendors the right questions.

## Official fees at the KUA: Rp0 or Rp600,000

For Muslim couples, the marriage registration fee is set by Government Regulation (PP) No. 59 of 2018 on the types and rates of non-tax state revenue (PNBP) at the Ministry of Religious Affairs. Article 5 says there is no registration fee for a marriage. If the akad, the Islamic marriage contract, takes place outside the KUA, the district religious affairs office, a transport and professional service fee applies. The regulation's annex sets that fee at Rp600,000 per marriage.

The Ministry's Central Java regional office puts it more plainly: marrying at the KUA is free as long as it happens at the KUA office during working hours, and the Rp600,000 for a marriage outside the KUA is paid straight into a bank as state revenue. It is not paid to the penghulu, the officiating registrar.

The question that comes up most is a Saturday or Sunday akad. Article 16 of Minister of Religious Affairs Regulation (PMA) No. 30 of 2024, as quoted by detikHikmah in January 2026, says the akad in principle takes place at the KUA on working days and hours, and may take place outside the KUA or outside working days and hours at the couple's request with the KUA head's approval. Reports such as Metro TV's in January 2026 say an akad outside working hours is also charged Rp600,000. The PP itself only says "outside the KUA", so if you plan an akad at the KUA on a holiday, ask the KUA where you register.

One more point that rarely gets mentioned: Article 7(2) of the same PP allows a Rp0 rate for citizens who cannot afford it and who marry outside the KUA. Ask your local KUA about the conditions.

On timing, detikHikmah also notes that the akad can take place no sooner than 10 working days after the KUA receives your file. We've summarised the documents in our article on [marriage documents for the KUA and civil registry](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/syarat-dokumen-nikah-2026-kua-catatan-sipil).

## If you're marrying at the civil registry

For non-Muslim couples, the marriage is recorded at the Population and Civil Registration Office (Dukcapil) after the church blessing or religious ceremony. Issuing the marriage certificate at Dukcapil is free of charge, under Article 79A of Law No. 24 of 2013. The Dukcapil service standard we checked states this in writing.

Fees at the church, vihara, temple or klenteng where the religious ceremony is held differ from one place of worship to another, and we found no official rate that applies across the board. Ask the secretariat when you register, including whether there's a fee for pre-marriage guidance.

Before you compare, keep a few things in mind.

**The guest numbers aren't the same.** Le Meridien's figure is for 50 guests, Luxus Grand Ballroom's for 400. Comparing the price column alone will mislead you. A fairer way is to divide the price by the number of guests for a rough per-person figure. Cordela Senen, for example, is Rp88,000,000 for 100 guests, or about Rp880,000 per guest. That's still rough, because what each package includes differs.

**Package contents need confirming.** Our data records every figure as a starting package, with a note that the inclusions are confirmed with the 7Magic team. So don't assume decoration, make-up or photography are included until you have a written quote.

**"Starting from" is the floor.** The date, the day of the week, extra guests and menu choices can all change it. Ask, too, whether the price differs for weekends or busy dates, such as the weeks before Chinese New Year.

## Other things that drive the cost

We've deliberately left figures out for the items below. Their prices depend heavily on the vendor, the date and your taste, and the numbers floating around online often have no clear source or year. What we can give you is what makes them move.

- **Catering beyond the package.** If you have more guests than the package covers, there's usually a per-guest charge for the extras. Portions don't always equal the number of invitations, and we explain the maths in our article on [how to calculate catering portions](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/cara-menghitung-porsi-katering-pernikahan).
- **Decoration.** The size of the stage, fresh or artificial flowers, and whether the venue requires its partner vendors. That partner-vendor clause is what most often catches couples out.
- **Make-up and outfits.** How many people are made up (just the couple, or parents and family too), how many outfit changes, and whether a traditional ceremony needs its own attire.
- **Photography and entertainment.** Hours of coverage, number of photographers, video, and a band or DJ. Ask, too, whether the venue charges a fee for outside vendors.
- **Venue extras.** Overtime, power for the band, guest parking and a damage deposit. The questions we suggest asking before you sign are in our article on [12 questions before signing a venue contract](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/12-pertanyaan-sebelum-tanda-tangan-venue-pernikahan).

Start with what's certain. The official KUA or Dukcapil fee is clear from day one. Then lock in your guest count, because almost every reception item is calculated from it. Only then ask two or three venues for written quotes for the same number of guests, so the comparison is fair.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to marry at the KUA in Jakarta?

Rp0 if the akad takes place at the KUA office during working hours. Rp600,000 if it takes place outside the KUA, paid into a bank as state revenue. The rule is the same across Indonesia, Jakarta included, because it comes from a Government Regulation.

### Is the Rp600,000 paid to the penghulu?

No. It's paid into a bank as state revenue, and the payment receipt goes to the KUA. If anyone asks for payment outside that, you can raise it with the city's Ministry of Religious Affairs office.

### How much is a hotel reception in Jakarta?

In 7Magic's data as of 2 November 2026, the starting price of a wedding reception package ranges from Rp88,000,000 for 100 guests at Cordela Senen to Rp365,400,000 for 300 guests at The Orient Jakarta. What each package includes needs confirming per venue.

### Is a function building cheaper than a hotel?

Not always. In our data, Graha Mandiri is listed at Rp162,000,000 for 300 guests and Luxus Grand Ballroom at Rp195,000,000 for 400, while several 3 and 4-star hotels start below Rp120,000,000 for 100 to 200 guests. Compare at the same guest count.
