Plenty of couples arrive at their first meeting with the church choir holding a song list they've spent months on. It has *A Thousand Years* on it, *Perfect*, maybe *The Prayer*. Then the choir or the priest explains that most of those can't be used inside the Mass.

The usual assumption is that it's about language. It isn't. English, Latin and even German songs are on the list recommended for wedding Masses. What decides it is the kind of song: whether it was written for the liturgy, or is a love song that happens to be beautiful.

This article lays out song choices in the order of the Mass, with an English or Latin alternative for every part. For the ceremony itself, from registering at the parish to the civil registration, read [Catholic Wedding Ceremony: Full Process from Parish to Mass](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/prosesi-pemberkatan-pernikahan-katolik).

## The Archdiocese of Jakarta's four song groups

The Archdiocese of Jakarta (KAJ), through its Membangun Rumah Tangga team and its Family Apostolate Commission, sorts songs for the wedding Mass into four groups. Pen@ Katolik published the list in June 2025. The principle is simple: songs for Mass are written for the liturgy, so they can't simply be swapped for a pop worship song or a romantic song the couple picked.

| Group | What it means | When it's sung | Foreign-language examples on the list |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Liturgical weight. Includes every wedding hymn in Madah Bakti and Puji Syukur | Anywhere in the Mass | *Ave Verum* (Mozart), *Ave Maria* (Arcadelt), *Oh How Precious Thy Love, My Lord*, *Ich bete an die Macht der Liebe* |
| **B** | Liturgical weight with a strong regional character | Recommended according to where the wedding takes place | *Nderek Dewi Mariyah* (Javanese), *Janji Setia* in Batak style, *Tuhanlah Pelindungku* (keroncong) |
| **C** | Little or no liturgical weight | After the liturgical parts: after communion or the blessing, or as the closing of Mass | *Ave Maria* (Schubert), *Ave Maria* (Bach-Gounod), *Climb Every Mountain*, *You'll Never Walk Alone*, *May the Good Lord*, *The Hawaiian Wedding Song* |
| **D** | Songs for outside the Mass | Best sung outside the Mass, for example at the reception | *And I Love You So*, *Love Story*, *She Wears My Ring* |

One thing that surprises people: Schubert's *Ave Maria*, probably the best-known version, is in group C, not A. It's still allowed, but it belongs at the end, not in the middle of the liturgy. If you want an *Ave Maria* for the Marian devotion during Mass, choose a version from group A or from the hymnal.

This list was drawn up for the Archdiocese of Jakarta. Other dioceses may have their own guidelines, and every parish still has the final word.

## Songs for each part of the Mass

The table follows the order of a wedding Mass. The first column has Indonesian songs from the hymnals or the KAJ list. The second has English or Latin options that are also liturgical. Puji Syukur (PS) numbers come from online Mass song indexes and a choir's list. Your parish's edition may differ, so check them against the book your choir uses.

| Part of the Mass | Indonesian options | English or Latin options | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entrance procession** | *Beriring Menuju Altar yang Mulia*, *Menuju Altar Tuhan* (both group A) | Instrumental. See the note below the table | Needs a tempo you can walk to. Time the aisle at rehearsal |
| **Kyrie, Gloria, Sanctus, Agnus Dei** | Misa Kita II (PS 351, 392, 413), Misa Kita IV (PS 353, 393, 414) | *Kyrie* from the J.A. Korman Mass (group A) | The Mass ordinary. Usually follows the choir's habit |
| **Responsorial psalm** | A family-themed psalm such as *Berbahagialah* or Psalm 127 (group A) | Rare. The psalm is almost always sung in the language of the Mass | Follows the readings chosen with the priest |
| **Parents' blessing / sungkem** | *Mohon Restu*, *Kami Mohon Restu Ayah Ibu*, *Keparenga Putra Sungkem* (group B) | *Wedding Prayer* (Fern Glasgow Dunlap) | Not every parish includes this. Ask first |
| **Vows and blessing** | *Berkatilah ya Tuhan Nikah Ini* (PS 611), *Berkatilah* (Y. Totok Pujianto), *Janji Setia* | *Oh How Precious Thy Love, My Lord* | The vows themselves are usually said in silence. The song comes after |
| **Offertory** | *Cinta Kasih Allah* (PS 659), *Kupersembahkan Pada-Mu ya Tuhan Seluruh Diri dan Hidup* (group A) | Instrumental | Only when the wedding is within a Mass |
| **Lord's Prayer** | Bapa Kami (PS 404, PS 405) | *Pater Noster* (PS 402) | Often sung with the congregation |
| **Communion** | *Bahagialah Tiap Rumah Tangga* (PS 613), *Kasih Pasti Lemah Lembut*, *Rahmat Kasih Perkawinan* (group A) | *Ave Verum Corpus* (Mozart) | The longest part. Prepare two or three songs |
| **Marian devotion** | *Salam Bagimu Maria* (PS 628), *Nderek Dewi Mariyah* (group B) | Gregorian *Ave Maria* (PS 625), *Ave Maria* (Arcadelt or J. Haagh) | Ask whether your parish includes this part |
| **Closing / recessional** | A festive group A song, or a group C song | *Ave Maria* (Schubert), *May the Good Lord*, *You'll Never Walk Alone* (all group C) | This is where group C songs fit |

**On the entrance procession.** The KAJ list has no English song written specifically for the procession. For an international feel, choirs usually turn to classical instrumentals. Parish guidelines abroad often name the *Air* from Handel's *Water Music*, Bach's *Air on the G String* and Pachelbel's *Canon*. Some parishes there, on the other hand, refuse the Wagner (*Here Comes the Bride*) and Mendelssohn wedding marches. Both come from opera and stage music, not from the liturgy. Ask your organist whether your parish has the same rule.

**If the wedding is not within a Mass.** When one partner isn't Catholic, the wedding is usually a Liturgy of the Word. There is no offertory, no Lord's Prayer within the Eucharist and no communion, so the song list is much shorter: entrance, psalm, blessing, Marian devotion (if agreed) and closing.

## Signing the register and the photo session

After the final blessing, the couple, the witnesses and the priest sign the church marriage register, and there is usually a photo session at the altar. This is outside the core of the liturgy, and it's where many choirs sing more popular worship songs.

For example, the Angelii Vox choir lists English worship songs for this part, such as *10,000 Reasons*, *The Power of Your Love*, *Father We Commit to You* and *This Is the Day*, alongside Indonesian songs like *Dua Insan Bersatu* and *Berkatilah*. These songs aren't on the KAJ group list, so ask your parish whether they can be sung inside the church.

## Popular English songs couples ask for

*The Prayer* (Celine Dion and Andrea Bocelli), *A Thousand Years* (Christina Perri), *Perfect* (Ed Sheeran) and *Beautiful in White* (Shane Filan) turn up on a lot of church-wedding song recommendations. None of the four is on the KAJ group list. The popular love songs that are on it, such as *And I Love You So* and *Love Story*, are in group D: songs for outside the Mass.

So if a pop song means a lot to you both, the safest approach is:

1. **Play it at the reception**, for the entrance or the first dance. For ideas, see [30 romantic first dance songs](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/30-lagu-romantis-untuk-first-dance-wedding-yang-bikin-momen-jadi-unforgettable).
2. **Ask the parish** whether it can be performed after Mass has ended, for example during the photo session. Parishes answer this differently.
3. **Don't slip it quietly onto the choir's list.** Some parishes ask for the song list to be submitted and approved in advance, and changing songs at rehearsal makes things hard for everyone.

## What to ask your parish

- Does the parish have its own song list, or does it follow the diocese's?
- Which hymnal is used: Puji Syukur, Madah Bakti, or both? Song numbers have to match that book.
- Do you have to use the parish organist and choir, or can you bring your own?
- When is the song list due, and who approves it?
- Is there a parents' blessing or a Marian devotion, and how long is it?
- Can a group C song be used for the recessional?

## Frequently asked questions

### Are English songs banned at a Catholic wedding Mass?

No. The KAJ list has English, Latin and German songs in group A, which can be sung within the Mass. What's limited is pop and love songs that aren't liturgical, in any language.

### Can we have Schubert's Ave Maria?

On the KAJ list, Schubert's *Ave Maria* is in group C. That means it can still be considered, but it goes after the liturgical parts, for example after communion or as the closing. For a Marian devotion in the middle of Mass, use a group A version or the Gregorian *Ave Maria* (PS 625).

### We're marrying outside Jakarta. Does this list apply?

The group list was drawn up for the Archdiocese of Jakarta. The principle, separating liturgical from non-liturgical songs, holds across the Catholic Church, but the details may differ in other dioceses. Songs from the wedding sections of Puji Syukur and Madah Bakti are generally safe anywhere.

### Can we play a recording from a phone or speaker?

That's up to each parish, and we found no general rule. Ask when you hand in your song list.

## Next steps

Ask the parish office for its song list and marriage guidelines on your first visit. Pick one song for each row of the table above, mark which ones are English or Latin, and take it to a meeting with the choir at least two weeks before the day. You don't have to drop your favourite pop songs. Move them to the reception, where they'll land best anyway.
