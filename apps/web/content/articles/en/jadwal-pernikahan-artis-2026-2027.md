Closer to home, Mangkunegaran in Solo has just announced the schedule for Gusti Sura's royal wedding in early November.

This page lists only weddings whose date or month has been announced by the couple, their family or their agency, and reported by at least two different outlets. Rumours stay out. Couples who are engaged but have no official date yet are listed separately further down. We re-checked every entry on 8 October 2026.

## October 2026

### Song Ji Ho and Kim So Ri

K-drama viewers know Song Ji Ho as Im Geum, older brother of Im Sol (Kim Hye Yoon) in *Lovely Runner*. Kim So Ri is a singer and actress who debuted in 2016, and she is two years older than Song.

The couple announced their wedding on Tuesday 6 October 2026 through Song's agency, Inyeon Entertainment, along with pre-wedding photos and handwritten letters on Instagram. In their letters, both wrote that they would marry this October. The day and the venue have not been officially announced.

### Jang Dong Yoon and Kim Seung Yoon

Jang Dong Yoon is an actor best known for the drama *The Tale of Nokdu*. On 25 August 2026 he posted a handwritten letter saying he would marry actress Kim Seung Yoon, someone he had been with for a long time, in October. The ceremony will be a simple Christian service attended by both families. Several Korean outlets report that it takes place on Saturday, 10 October 2026, but neither the couple nor their agency has confirmed it.

There is an interesting link here. Kim Seung Yoon and Cha Gyeong Eun, the bride on the 14 November date below, are sisters. That makes Jang Dong Yoon and Lee You Jin future co-brothers-in-law, both married into the same family.

### Kang Kyun Sung and Yoo Ha Jin: Friday 30 October

Kang Kyun Sung is a vocalist of the group NOEL, which debuted in 2002. His bride, Yoo Ha Jin, is an actress who started out in the MBC drama *One Spring Night* in 2019. There are 14 years between them.

Kang told his fans first, with a handwritten letter on Instagram in early August, and his agency, S27M Entertainment, then confirmed the date of 30 October. The wedding will be held in Seoul, at a venue that has not been made public.

## November 2026

### Yoon Jong Hoon and Han Eun Seo: Sunday 1 November

Yoon Jong Hoon is best known as Ha Yoon Cheol in *The Penthouse*. Han Eun Seo is an actress who debuted in 2008, and she is ten years younger than Yoon. They met while working together on the SBS drama *Return* (2018) and dated for several years.

Yoon's agency, YK Media Plus, announced the wedding on 7 September 2026. It will take place in Gangnam, Seoul, at a venue that has not been named. The format has not been announced.

### Gusti Sura, Mangkunegaran: Tuesday 3 to Saturday 7 November

G.R.Aj. Ancillasura Marina Sudjiwo, known as Gusti Sura, is the older sister of K.G.P.A.A. Mangkoenagoro X (Gusti Bhre), head of the Mangkunegaran royal house in Solo. On 8 October 2026, Mangkunegaran announced that the Dhaup Ageng, Gusti Sura's royal wedding, will run over five days, from 3 to 7 November 2026. Her fiancé does not come from the celebrity world, so we do not name him here.

If you are interested in Javanese weddings, coverage of this Dhaup Ageng is a chance to see the rites and attire of the Mangkunegaran tradition.

### Lee You Jin and Cha Gyeong Eun: Saturday 14 November

Lee You Jin is an actor and director, known for *Age of Youth 2* and *Be Melodramatic*. Cha Gyeong Eun is an actress and a regular cast member of *SNL Korea*. They met on the project that was Lee's directing debut, and started dating in June 2026.

On 6 October 2026 they announced the wedding, and on the same day both sides confirmed that Cha is pregnant. The ceremony will be private, held in Seoul for both families and close friends only, at an undisclosed location.

### Kwak Si Yang and Yoo Jiae: Saturday 28 November

Kwak Si Yang is an actor known for *Oh My Ghost*. Yoo Jiae is a member of the girl group Lovelyz who now also acts. They have been together for about a year.

Kwak's agency, 9Ato Entertainment, confirmed the date of 28 November 2026. The wedding will be private, for family, relatives and close friends. The venue has not been officially announced.

## December 2026

### Ji Ye Eun and Vata: Saturday 12 December

Ji Ye Eun is an actress and a regular on *Running Man*. Vata is a dancer and choreographer who leads the crew We Dem Boyz, runner-up on the Mnet show *Street Man Fighter* (2022). The two know each other from the same church.

They chose 12 December 2026, Vata's birthday, as their wedding date. Ji's agency, CP Entertainment, and Vata's agency, A-RA, confirmed the plan on 2 September 2026. The wedding will be held in Seoul, at a venue that has not been named.

## January 2027

### Baek Jin Hee: Saturday 23 January 2027

Baek Jin Hee is known for the sitcom *High Kick: Revenge of the Short Legged* and the drama *Empress Ki*. Her fiancé is not a celebrity; he is an office worker who lives in Canada. They met while Baek was on a language course in Canada and then kept up a long-distance relationship.

Her agency, Management AM9, announced the date of 23 January 2027 in early September. The wedding will be private, at a hotel in Seoul. After the wedding, Baek will divide her time between Korea and Canada and keep acting.

## If you are marrying in these months too

Four of the Korean weddings on this schedule fall on a Saturday. If you are also aiming for a Saturday in November or December, ask your venue about availability as early as you can and keep one backup date in mind. A Friday or a Sunday, as Kang Kyun Sung and Yoon Jong Hoon chose, can work as an alternative. Our list of [12 questions to ask before signing a venue contract](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/12-pertanyaan-sebelum-tanda-tangan-venue-pernikahan) can help when you visit venues.

Keep an eye on the weather as well. BMKG, Indonesia's meteorology agency, expects the 2026/2027 rainy season to start in many regions in November and December 2026, with peaks spread from December 2026 to February 2027. If you choose a garden or beachfront venue, ask for a rain plan, such as a tent or an indoor space, and make sure it is written into the contract.

If your date is close to Christmas, note that 24 December 2026 is a collective leave day and 25 December is Christmas Day, which makes 24 to 27 December a long weekend. Some guests may already have holiday plans for those days, so send your news or a *save the date* early.

Finally, Ji Ye Eun and Vata's way of choosing a date is easy to borrow. A date that means something to the two of you, such as a birthday or the day you first met, makes the wedding day easier to remember. If your family also goes by weton or shio, our piece on [how to choose a wedding date](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/menentukan-tanggal-pernikahan-weton-shio) can help you bring the two together.
