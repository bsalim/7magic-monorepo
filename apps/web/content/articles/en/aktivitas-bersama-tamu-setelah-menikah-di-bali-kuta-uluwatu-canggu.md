Many guests at a Bali wedding come from Jakarta, Surabaya or overseas, and few fly home the next morning. That is why plenty of couples plan one easy day with family and friends after the reception, whether that is a dinner together, an afternoon at a beach club or the Kecak dance.

This article covers south Bali and Canggu: Kuta and Legian, Seminyak, Jimbaran, Uluwatu, Nusa Dua, Sanur, then Canggu through to Pererenan and Seseh. If your reception is in Tabanan or Ubud, we have separate guides to [Nuanu and Tabanan](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/wisata-nuanu-tabanan-setelah-menikah-di-bali) and to [Ubud and Kintamani](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/ubud-kintamani-untuk-pengantin-baru-dan-tamu-pernikahan).

## Two things to sort out before you set off

**Uluwatu traffic.** The Sterling Shortcut, which linked Bingin with Dreamland, Balangan and Jimbaran, closed in February 2026, apparently for good. Since 2 June 2026 a traffic-flow trial has run every day from 2pm to 10pm at six intersections in Pecatu. Some turns are banned, and during those hours vehicles with four or more wheels cannot head west from Baler Setra and Blimbing Sari towards Jl. Uluwatu. Officials say peak-hour trips can take three hours or more. We have found no report on whether the trial has been made permanent, so ask your driver the day before.

In practice, a group staying in Seminyak or Kuta should not make a round trip just for the sunset. Leave around midday, spend the afternoon and evening on the Bukit, and head back after dinner.

**The foreign tourist levy.** Foreign guests must pay a tourist levy of IDR 150,000 each, in force since 14 February 2024 and paid through the official Love Bali portal. Indonesian citizens do not pay it. Foreign visitors who have not paid can be turned away at attractions, so send your foreign guests the Love Bali link before they fly.

## Uluwatu: the Kecak at the clifftop temple

Uluwatu Temple is open from 7am to 7pm WITA. As of 3 January 2026, entry for Indonesian citizens is IDR 40,000 (adults) and IDR 30,000 (children aged 4 to 12), and for foreign visitors IDR 60,000 and IDR 40,000. Car parking is IDR 4,000. Tickets are sold only at the entrance, and the counters take cash and QRIS (GoPay, OVO, DANA) but not international debit or credit cards. Foreign guests without an Indonesian e-wallet should bring cash. Some other listings give IDR 50,000 for adults without a date, so carry a little extra.

The Kecak is performed by two troupes that alternate day by day. On-site tickets are about IDR 150,000 for adults and IDR 75,000 for children (checked 5 January 2026), or about IDR 135,000 if bought online through Hey Bali. The Kecak ticket is separate from temple entry. Seats sell out fast in peak season, so arrive between 4pm and 5pm. Warn guests about the long-tailed macaques, which grab sunglasses, hats and phones.

## Melasti and the Bukit beach clubs

Melasti Beach in Ungasan also has a Kecak show, sold through resellers, with beach entry and parking paid separately. An undated Traveloka listing gives beach entry as IDR 10,000 for Indonesian adults and IDR 15,000 for foreign adults. For dinner, Nibble included Tropical Temptation Beach Club at Melasti in its romantic dinner list dated 22 August 2023. That list is three years old, so check the place is still open.

For groups with children, Ulu Cliffhouse (updated 23 June 2026) has free entry every Friday and at its Sunday sessions, which start at 12pm. The Sunday sessions also have a free Kids Club. In Bingin, Motel Mexicola opened an outpost in July 2026, better suited to guests who still want to party.

## Jimbaran: seafood on the sand

For dinner with a big group, Jimbaran is the easiest choice: long tables on the sand, grilled fish and the sunset. Nibble's article on places to eat in Jimbaran (19 October 2021) names Made Bagus Cafe, which grills seafood over coconut-shell charcoal, Menega Cafe on the beachfront, Bela Seafood with its sand floor and candlelit tables at night, and Warung Mami Ikan Bakar, once recommended by food writer Bondan Winarno. New Bendesa Café has long tables and live music in the evening, which suits a big family. Nyoman Cafe prices its seafood by weight, so ask for the weight and price before it is cooked.

For a quieter evening for two, Nibble's romantic dinner list (22 August 2023) names Sundara at Four Seasons Jimbaran Bay, with live jazz and a Romantic Dinner package, and HATIKU, with tables on the sand and dishes such as salted egg or garlic butter seafood. Both Nibble articles are a few years old, so confirm your booking directly with the restaurant.

Remember that Uluwatu to Jimbaran can take over an hour at peak times since the Sterling Shortcut closed. The Kecak followed by dinner in Jimbaran on the same day is still possible, as long as you hire one car with a driver and do not race the clock.

## Seminyak: Alunê and padel

Many of south Bali's newest openings are in Seminyak. W Bali Seminyak has opened Alunê, a sunset beach club on the former Woobar site, with private jacuzzi cabanas for up to six people. Alunê also made Nibble's list of new Bali restaurants for 7 October 2026, alongside Rocinha by La Favela in Seminyak. One cabana is enough for the two of you and your closest friends.

For active guests, Club Lacoste opened two outdoor padel courts at W Bali Seminyak in September 2026. The courts are open to the public every day from 8am to 8pm. Book a morning slot before the heat, then go on to breakfast together.

For dinner by the sea, Nibble's 2023 list names Seasalt at Alila Seminyak and La Lucciola on Petitenget Beach, both from 5pm, and KU DE TA on Seminyak Beach.

## Kuta and Legian: Waterbom and afternoon tea

Waterbom Bali in Kuta is generally open from 9am to 6pm and closes for Nyepi. Its official packages page lists IDR 519K for adults and IDR 421K for children aged 2 to 11, without making the ticket type clear. Resellers sell a separate rate for Indonesian ID holders; Travelocity, for example, lists US$23 for KTP holders against US$37.93 for everyone else (undated). Bring your KTP if you want the local rate.

For parents and older guests, Padma Resort Legian serves a Bali Heritage Afternoon Tea at The Deli, ten bites inspired by the regions of Bali. It is calm and needs little walking.

## Nusa Dua and Sanur

Nusa Dua suits guests staying at the resorts who want a polished dinner. Nibble (2023) recommends Kayuputi and its Nyegara Gunung package. Queen Tandoor, an Indian restaurant with branches in Nusa Dua, Kuta and Seminyak, is labelled halal by Nibble without naming a certifying body. We found no evidence of BPJPH certification for any restaurant in this article, so Muslim guests should check for themselves on SiHalal. Nibble's list "10 Nasi Campur Terenak di Bali, Ada yang Halal Juga!" can help.

Sanur has a paved beachfront path of about 5 km linking resorts, cafes and warungs. It makes for an easy morning with family. Sanur is also the main harbour for Nusa Penida. Since 1 September 2026, foreign visitors pay an island entry fee at the port, while Indonesian visitors pay only the ticket at each site. For a group, a Penida trip works better as a separate day.

## Canggu: beach clubs for the group

For guests who want a long afternoon with a pool and a DJ, the beach clubs in Berawa are the most practical. FINNS Beach Club (Jl. Pantai Berawa No. 99) and Atlas Beach Club (Jl. Pantai Berawa No. 88) both have free general entry on regular days, while daybeds and reserved seating carry a minimum spend that one group can share. Minimum spends vary between sources and events, so confirm with the venue directly before booking. At Atlas, some events are ticketed.

Do not mix up the two FINNS venues. The one that closed is FINNS Recreation Club, the sports venue at Jl. Pantai Berawa No. 15, which is being rebuilt as a resort according to Bali Discovery (14 July 2025). FINNS Beach Club remains open.

If your group is more relaxed, The Lawn offers a calm oceanfront afternoon with food and cocktails, while La Brisa has a boho feel, several pools and Spanish-inspired food. One source says La Brisa holds a market every Sunday from 10am to 4pm. After dark, many beach clubs turn into parties that run past midnight, so go early if guests are still tired from the reception.

## Canggu mornings and evenings: surfing, Pererenan, Seseh

Mornings suit a group surf lesson. Batu Bolong has softer, slower waves for beginners, with surf schools nearby. Berawa suits intermediates, while Echo Beach and the Sandbar are for experienced surfers. The wind usually picks up towards midday, so choose a morning session.

For the sunset, Batu Bolong is the busiest spot, and Honeycombers suggests arriving about 45 minutes early. For somewhere quieter, especially for the two of you, choose Pererenan or Seseh. Sunset is around 6pm to 6:30pm. Seseh also has Udara, an adults-only yoga studio, for a calm morning after the guests have gone.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Indonesian guests have to pay the IDR 150,000 Bali tourist levy?

No. The levy applies only to foreign tourists and is paid through the Love Bali portal. Foreign guests should pay it before they fly.

### Can we watch the Kecak at Uluwatu and then have dinner in Seminyak?

You can, but with the Sterling Shortcut closed and the 2pm to 10pm traffic trial running since 2 June 2026, afternoon and evening trips around Uluwatu can take a very long time. Dinner in Uluwatu or Jimbaran is the safer plan.

### How do we pay for Uluwatu Temple tickets?

In cash or by QRIS through GoPay, OVO or DANA at the entrance counter. International cards are not accepted.

### What is the minimum spend for a daybed at FINNS or Atlas?

It varies by source, day and event. Contact the venue directly before booking for a group.
