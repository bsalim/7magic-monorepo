In Sicily, Elton John sat down at the piano and played "Your Song" for the newlyweds. By then Dua Lipa and Callum Turner had actually been legally married for almost a week. The Sicilian party was the second chapter.

The first chapter was much simpler: a civil ceremony on Sunday, 31 May 2026 at Old Marylebone Town Hall, London. The legal part was done in London, then the big celebration moved to Italy, and the guests followed.

For Indonesian couples this format sounds familiar. Many marry at the KUA (the religious affairs office where Muslim marriages are registered) or at the civil registry near home, then hold the reception in the family's home town or in Bali a few weeks later. Dua Lipa's wedding shows how the two chapters can have different characters without feeling disconnected.

## How the wedding unfolded

The Sicilian celebration ran from 5 to 7 June 2026. Here's what was reported across the whole sequence:

- **Sunday, 31 May 2026:** civil ceremony at Old Marylebone Town Hall, London.
- **Friday, 5 June 2026:** welcome cocktails at the Galleria d'Arte Moderna, in the Sant'Anna complex, Palermo.
- **Saturday, 6 June 2026:** ceremony and reception at Villa Valguarnera, Bagheria. Guests stayed at Villa Igiea.
- **Saturday, 20 June 2026:** the wedding photos were posted with a short caption, "Mr & Mrs".

## Two chapters, two jobs

The London ceremony was the legally binding part. The Sicilian ceremony was the part for celebrating with about 200 guests, among them Charli XCX, Joe Alwyn, Troye Sivan, Mark Ronson, Donatella Versace and Chris Stapleton.

Separating the two has practical advantages. Paperwork and the schedule of the penghulu (the marriage registrar) or the civil registry officer don't get tangled up with vendors and out-of-town guests. If the reception has to be pushed back because of the venue or the weather, your marriage isn't delayed with it.

In the Indonesian version, the first chapter might be an akad at the KUA, or a church blessing and civil registration attended only by close family. The second chapter is a reception you design with more freedom. A one-week gap like Dua Lipa's also makes sense for couples who want a short rest before hosting their guests.

What needs thought from the start is how you communicate with guests. With two chapters you effectively have two invitation lists: a short one for the akad or civil registration, and a long one for the reception. Make it clear who is invited to which event, so relatives who weren't asked to the first chapter don't feel left out. Separate digital invitations for each event are usually enough.

## The civil look: a suit, not a long gown

For the London ceremony, Dua Lipa wore ivory Schiaparelli Haute Couture designed by Daniel Roseberry. It consisted of a blazer, an asymmetric skirt and a blush bustier. She added white gloves, a hat by Stephen Jones and Christian Louboutin pumps.

The choice suits the smaller, more formal setting of a registry office. For an akad at the KUA or a simple church blessing, you could wear a short kebaya, a suit or something you'll wear again, and save the grander gown or traditional outfit for the reception.

## Sicily: two looks for the bride, one tuxedo

At the welcome cocktails in Palermo, Dua Lipa wore a Bottega Veneta look by Louise Trotter. For the ceremony at Villa Valguarnera she wore a Chanel Haute Couture gown by Matthieu Blazy. The halter-neck gown carried about 480,000 beads and 25,000 feathers, paired with an embroidered veil and a feathered train about 2 metres long.

Callum Turner wore a Louis Vuitton tuxedo by Pharrell Williams, with a hand-made red peony on his lapel.

The lapel flower is an easy detail to copy. Indonesian grooms usually wear a brooch or a string of jasmine. A small flower in a colour that matches the decor or the bride's outfit can link the two of them visually in photos.

## Music and local food

One of the musical moments was Elton John playing "Your Song" on the piano. For dessert, guests were served Sicilian sweets by chef Tony Lo Coco.

If you hold your reception out of town, food from that region is a simple way to give guests a taste of the place. In Yogyakarta that could mean jajanan pasar (traditional market snacks), in Bali it could be jaje Bali (Balinese rice cakes). Ask your caterer to set up a dedicated table for it.

As for music, a moment like "Your Song" translates to a much smaller scale. What makes it memorable is that the song is played live, by one person, for a couple who are listening. If a relative or friend plays the piano or guitar well, ask them to perform one song that means something to the two of you. Tell your wedding organiser and MC early so there's a dedicated pause in the running order.

## What you can borrow

- Separate the legal part from the celebration if your schedule or location calls for it. The akad or civil registration can take place near home with close family.
- Dress for the scale of each event. A suit or short kebaya for the civil chapter, something grander for the reception.
- For an out-of-town reception, plan a welcome event the day before so guests travelling from afar have time to get to know each other.
- Put guests in one hotel, as Villa Igiea was used for the guests in Sicily, so transport is easier to manage.
- Serve the local specialities of the place where the reception is held.
- Add a small flower to the groom's lapel or beskap (Javanese formal jacket) that matches the bride's outfit.
