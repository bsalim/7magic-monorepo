Bali is not only for chapel blessings and Balinese Hindu ceremonies. Many Muslim couples from Jakarta, Surabaya and other cities choose Bali for their akad nikah, the Islamic marriage contract, from a small akad at a family villa to one in a hotel ballroom in Nusa Dua. Luna Maya and Maxime Bouttier, for example, held a [Yogyakarta-style akad in Bali](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-luna-maya-maxime-bouttier-bali-jakarta) before a second reception in Jakarta.

Under the rules, an akad in Bali is no different from one anywhere else. What makes it feel complicated is distance: you live in one district, the akad is in another district on another island, and the wali, the bride's guardian, or the witnesses may not be able to fly in. This article sets out the process.

## The process in brief

1. Get a **marriage cover letter** from the kelurahan or village office where you live.
2. Get a **marriage recommendation letter** from the KUA (Office of Religious Affairs) of your home district, because the akad is outside the district where you live.
3. **Register your intention to marry** with the KUA of the district where the venue is in Bali, in person or online through SIMKAH, at least 10 working days before the akad.
4. Attend the **marriage preparation course** and the marriage examination.
5. **Pay the state fee** if the akad is outside the KUA office or outside working hours.
6. The **akad** is led by a penghulu (marriage registrar) from the KUA in Bali, and that KUA records the marriage.

## Which KUA?

Under Minister of Religious Affairs Regulation No. 30 of 2024 on the Registration of Marriage, the intention to marry is registered with the KUA where the akad will take place, or online through SIMKAH, at least 10 working days before the akad. So for an akad in Bali, you go to the KUA of the district where the venue is, not the KUA in your home city.

As a guide, venues in Nusa Dua, Jimbaran and Uluwatu are in the Kuta Selatan district, venues in Canggu in Kuta Utara, venues in Seminyak and Legian in the Kuta district, and venues in Ubud in the Ubud district of Gianyar. Confirm the district from the venue's full address before you register.

**If it is less than 10 working days.** Both Ministry of Religious Affairs sources we read say a late registration needs a dispensation from the district head or a stamped statement explaining the reason. For a wedding planned from another island, do not rely on this. Aim to have every document in a month before the akad.

## The recommendation letter from your home KUA

This is the document most often forgotten. A bride or groom marrying outside the district where they live needs a marriage recommendation letter from the KUA of their home district. The sources word differently whether this applies to each partner, so if you live in different districts, ask each KUA whether you both need one.

The order: the cover letter from the kelurahan first, then your home KUA for the recommendation, then the complete file sent or uploaded to the KUA in Bali. The kelurahan letter and other documents are covered in our article on [marriage documents in 2026](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/syarat-dokumen-nikah-2026-kua-catatan-sipil).

The main documents named by the Kebumen office of the Ministry of Religious Affairs in its explanation of Regulation 30/2024:

- A marriage cover letter from the village or kelurahan office.
- Copies of your birth certificate, identity card and family card.
- A marriage recommendation letter from your home KUA, if you marry outside the district where you live.
- A health certificate.
- The consent of both bride and groom.

Plus conditional documents: parental permission if under 21, a court dispensation if under 19, a commanding officer's permission for members of the armed forces and police, and a divorce or death certificate if either of you was married before.

## The marriage preparation course

The Ministry of Religious Affairs requires couples to attend a marriage preparation course (bimbingan perkawinan, or bimwin) before marrying. According to the Central Sulawesi office of the ministry, the basis is Director General of Islamic Guidance Circular No. 2 of 2024, and couples who have not attended cannot have their marriage book printed. Because you register with a KUA in Bali, ask that KUA whether you can attend in your home city, online, or must do so in Bali.

## When the wali cannot fly to Bali

A wali is a condition of a valid akad for the bride, and who may act as wali is covered in full in our [article on the rules for a wali](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-islami/aturan-wali-nikah-dan-urutan-duduk-saat-akad). In Bali the question is usually practical: what if the father cannot fly because of illness or age?

The answer is **taukil wali bil kitabah**, the wali delegating his role in writing. Under the earlier marriage registration rules, Regulation No. 20 of 2019, a wali who cannot attend makes a letter of delegation before the head of the KUA, witnessed by two people. The letter is made at the KUA where the wali is, not the KUA where the akad takes place. A father in Jakarta, for example, goes to his district KUA in Jakarta, the letter is taken to the KUA in Bali, and the penghulu conducts the akad on the wali's behalf.

Regulation 30/2024 has replaced the earlier registration rules, so ask the KUA in Bali whether the procedure and form are still the same. On a wali joining by video call, do not assume. Research on how delegation works in practice records that delegation by telephone or video was not accepted because there was no written proof, and policy can differ between KUAs.

Plan this early, because delegation requires the wali to go in person to his own KUA, and the original letter must reach Bali before the day of the akad.

## At the KUA, a mosque or a venue?

**At the KUA office, during working hours.** Free. This suits a very small akad, followed by a celebration elsewhere.

**Outside the KUA office or outside working hours.** An akad at a venue, home, villa or mosque, including at the weekend, can take place at the couple's request with the approval of the head of the KUA. The fee is a state charge of Rp600,000 under Government Regulation No. 59 of 2018, paid into the state treasury through a bank, not in cash to an official. The ministry has stated that there is no other charge beyond this.

**At a mosque.** The Ibnu Batutah Grand Mosque in the Puja Mandala complex in Nusa Dua is the best-known mosque in the southern tourist area. It stands beside a Catholic church, a Protestant church, a Buddhist temple and a Hindu temple in a single complex. For an akad at any mosque, contact its management committee first about scheduling, capacity and rules on photography.

**At a venue or hotel.** Ask the venue whether it has hosted an akad before and whether there is a quiet room for the ijab kabul, a place for ablutions and the direction of the qibla. For a morning akad followed by a reception, make sure the gap between them and moving the guests have been planned.

## Halal catering

In Bali, halal status cannot be assumed. The Halal Product Assurance Law requires caterers to be halal certified, but a certificate covers the kitchens and outlets registered under it, not automatically a whole hotel. Ask for:

- The halal certificate number of the kitchen that will cook for your event, not just that of one of the hotel's restaurants.
- The list of dishes covered by that certificate.

Certificate numbers can be checked on SIHALAL, the halal agency BPJPH's system. If the venue uses an outside caterer, ask the caterer the same questions.

## The calendar: Ramadan, Eid and Nyepi 2027

According to the Ministry of Religious Affairs' Indonesian Hijri Calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 AH is expected on 8 February 2027, and Eid al-Fitr is listed as a public holiday on 10 and 11 March 2027. Nyepi falls on 8 March 2027, within Ramadan. During Nyepi, Ngurah Rai Airport closes for 24 hours and the whole of Bali stops, so guests cannot arrive or leave that day, and travel home for Eid in the same week will be very busy.

If you want to marry before Ramadan, the window is narrow. ## Registration

For Muslim couples, the marriage is recorded by the KUA that conducts the akad, not by the civil registry. Under the Population Administration Law, the KUA then passes the record to the civil registry. After the akad, you only need to update your family card at the civil registry where you live.

This differs from Christian, Catholic or Hindu couples, who register their marriage with the civil registry. If you are comparing options, see also our article on [Christian and Catholic weddings in Bali](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/pemberkatan-nikah-kristen-katolik-di-bali).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an akad nikah in Bali valid?

Yes, just like an akad in any other city, as long as its conditions and pillars are met and it is recorded by the KUA of the district where it takes place.

### Can a penghulu from Jakarta come to Bali?

The akad is recorded by the KUA where it takes place, so the penghulu comes from the KUA in Bali. A preacher or relative from Jakarta can still give the marriage sermon and prayers.

### How much does an akad at a hotel cost?

The official state fee is Rp600,000 for an akad outside the KUA office or outside working hours. Venue, catering and decoration costs are of course separate.

### My father cannot come to Bali. Do we need a wali hakim?

No. As long as your father can still express his wishes, he remains the wali and can delegate in writing through taukil wali at the KUA where he is. A wali hakim, a guardian appointed by the state, is for other situations, for example when there is no wali from the family line.

### When should we start?

Three months ahead is comfortable: enough time for the kelurahan letter, the KUA recommendation, the preparation course and, if needed, the wali's delegation. The official deadline is 10 working days before the akad.
