Around the time of the wedding, the Empire State Building in New York was lit blue. The lights were described as "her something blue", following the old Western bridal tradition. Not far away, inside Madison Square Garden, Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce got married.

The wedding on Friday, 3 July 2026 was confirmed by her publicist, Tree Paine. The arena, usually home to concerts and games, was turned into a "secret garden", with peach drapery and carpet and a tunnel lined with photos of the couple. There were about 1,000 guests.

That number sounds big by American standards, but it's ordinary for an Indonesian reception. Precisely because the scale is similar, many of Taylor and Travis's choices can be used directly by couples here, from who stands beside the couple to how you keep that many guests entertained.

## How the wedding unfolded

- **Thursday, 2 July 2026:** rehearsal dinner with about 100 guests.
- **Friday, 3 July 2026:** ceremony and reception at Madison Square Garden, New York, with about 1,000 guests. The ceremony was officiated by Adam Sandler.
- **Around the wedding day:** billboards in the city read "JUST&T MARRIED!", and the Empire State Building was lit blue.

## Siblings at their side

Taylor and Travis had no bridesmaids or groomsmen. The people standing beside them were their own siblings. Austin Swift, Taylor's brother, was man of honor. Jason Kelce, Travis's brother, was best man.

The idea fits Indonesian families well. At many of our weddings, brothers and sisters already play important roles, whether welcoming guests, carrying the rings or walking alongside the couple in the procession. Making them the only attendants also saves you from choosing between friends, coordinating bridesmaid outfits and splitting jobs among many people.

If you don't have siblings, a close cousin can fill the same role. What matters is that the roles are clear from the start: who walks in with you, who holds the rings and who looks after the couple if something comes up.

## The officiant and self-written vows

The ceremony was led by Adam Sandler, and the couple read vows they had written themselves.

In Indonesia, the sacred part of a wedding has a fixed form. The ijab kabul, the Islamic marriage contract, is spoken in prescribed words, and a church blessing follows the order of service. Vows you write yourself still have a place, just outside that part. Many couples read them at the start of the reception, during a couple's photo session, or put them in a letter read in front of close family after the akad.

Adam Sandler's role as officiant has an equivalent here too. The penghulu or pastor can't be replaced, of course, but you can ask a respected uncle, teacher or friend to give marriage advice or say a prayer at the reception. Ask well in advance and give them a time limit, around five minutes, so the running order doesn't overrun.

## White outfits from one fashion house

Taylor and Travis both wore white Dior Haute Couture designed by Jonathan Anderson, with custom Christian Louboutin shoes. Taylor finished her look with Cartier jewellery.

A bride and groom in the same colour and by the same designer look unified in photos. In Indonesia this is already common with traditional dress, such as a kebaya and beskap (Javanese formal jacket) cut from the same fabric. For an international-style reception, you could ask one designer to dress you both so your whites don't clash in tone.

## Entertaining 1,000 guests

Paul McCartney performed "I Want to Hold Your Hand", and Stevie Nicks performed too. The food came from the couple's favourite New York restaurants. Guests also played raffle games, and one of the prizes was a 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle.

To Indonesian guests, that sounds like two very familiar things: gubukan and doorprizes. Gubukan, the food stalls set up around a reception, filled from your favourite places, say the noodle shop you went to while dating, make the reception food feel personal. A doorprize draw gives guests a reason to stay until the end, and the prizes don't have to be expensive to liven up the room.

Guests included Selena Gomez, Ed Sheeran, Bradley Cooper, Hugh Grant, Gigi Hadid, Tom Brady and Andy Reid.

## Small details for guests

According to one report, the invitation specified a black-tie dress code. Another report said guests received monogrammed handkerchiefs with the lyric "so it's gonna be forever". Each of these details comes from a single source.

Either way, both are easy ideas to copy. State the dress code clearly on the invitation, especially if you want guests in a particular colour. A monogrammed handkerchief or fan with one line that means something to you both is a light, useful favour that rarely gets thrown away.

The photo tunnel at Madison Square Garden is easy to adapt as well. Guests usually just pass through the entrance of a reception hall while queuing to greet the couple. A row of photos of the two of you through the years along that route gives guests something to look at and talk about while they wait.

## What you can borrow

- Make a brother, sister or close cousin your main attendant, and divide the roles clearly.
- If you want to read your own vows, place them at the reception or a family moment, outside the akad or the blessing service.
- Fill the gubukan with food from your favourite places to eat.
- Plan a doorprize draw to keep guests there until the end.
- Write the dress code on the invitation in plain language.
- Choose a small, useful monogrammed favour, such as a handkerchief or a fan.
