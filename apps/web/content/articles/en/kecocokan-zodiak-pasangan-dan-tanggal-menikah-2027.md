## The twelve signs, their elements and modalities

Western astrology divides the circle of the sky into twelve equal parts of 30 degrees each. Your sign is the part the sun was in when you were born. Each sign carries one of four **elements** and one of three **modes**, which English-language astrology books usually call *modality*.

| Sign | Birth dates (approximately) | Element | Modality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | 21 March to 19 April | Fire | Cardinal |
| Taurus | 20 April to 20 May | Earth | Fixed |
| Gemini | 21 May to 20 June | Air | Mutable |
| Cancer | 21 June to 22 July | Water | Cardinal |
| Leo | 23 July to 22 August | Fire | Fixed |
| Virgo | 23 August to 22 September | Earth | Mutable |
| Libra | 23 September to 22 October | Air | Cardinal |
| Scorpio | 23 October to 21 November | Water | Fixed |
| Sagittarius | 22 November to 21 December | Fire | Mutable |
| Capricorn | 22 December to 19 January | Earth | Cardinal |
| Aquarius | 20 January to 18 February | Air | Fixed |
| Pisces | 19 February to 20 March | Water | Mutable |

The boundary dates can shift by a day depending on the year and the hour of birth. If you were born right on a changeover date, see the questions at the end.

**Elements** describe basic temperament. Fire is spirited and spontaneous. Earth is practical and steady. Air likes to think and to talk. Water is sensitive and led by feeling. Traditionally, fire and air are said to feed each other, the way air keeps a flame alight. Earth and water pair up too, the way water makes soil fertile.

**Modality** describes how a person moves. Cardinal signs like to start things. Fixed signs like to hold on and see things through. Mutable signs adapt easily. Two cardinal people both want to hold the wheel, and two fixed people both find it hard to give way.

## How to read two signs: count the distance

This is the key that rarely gets explained. Western astrology does not rate sign pairs one by one. What it looks at is the **distance** between the two signs on the zodiac wheel, because that distance decides whether their elements and modalities line up.

It's easy to do. Start from your sign and count the steps to your partner's sign in the order of the table above, taking the shorter way round. The answer is always between zero and six.

| Distance | Astrological name | What happens | Traditional reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Conjunction (same sign) | Same element, same modality | You understand each other without much explaining, but your weak spots double up too |
| 1 | Semi-sextile (neighbours) | Different element, different modality | Two different ways of living, with a lot to learn from each other |
| 2 | Sextile | Friendly elements, different modality | Light and friendly, easy to work with |
| 3 | Square (*quartile*) | Same modality, clashing elements | There is friction, and that friction is what pushes both of you to grow |
| 4 | Trine (*trigon*) | Same element, different modality | The smoothest match by tradition, like old friends |
| 5 | Quincunx | Different element, different modality | Easy to misread each other, so adjusting takes conscious effort |
| 6 | Opposition (opposite signs) | Same modality, friendly elements | A strong pull, each has what the other lacks |

Distances of four (trine) and two (sextile) are the ones that usually make the "most compatible" lists. Distance three (square) is the one that usually makes the "fight the most" lists. Astrologers call the square a tense aspect, and the word is chosen on purpose. Many astrologers actually consider a couple with some tension more durable than one that is too alike.

An example: Leo and Scorpio are three steps apart. Both are fixed, and their elements are fire and water. The reading is two people who both hold their ground, with different ways of feeling. Astrologers don't read it as a prohibition. The note is that this couple needs to learn a way of giving in that doesn't feel like losing.

## Your sun sign is only the start

The sign you know from everyday life only records the position of the sun. Astrologers who read couple compatibility (a branch called **synastry**, or *chart comparison*) compare both people's full birth charts. The points they watch most closely for a relationship:

- **The Moon**: emotional needs and what makes you feel safe. Many astrologers consider Moon compatibility more important than sun-sign compatibility for living together.
- **Venus**: how you love and what you find pleasurable.
- **Mars**: how you go after what you want, and also how you fight.
- **The Ascendant** or rising sign: first impressions and how you face the world. The Ascendant changes roughly every two hours, so you need a birth time to work it out.

To make this concrete, we use the same fictional couple as the example in our Ba Zi article. Raka was born on 5 May 1995 at 10:00 WIB in Jakarta. Nadia was born on 14 April 1996 at 07:30 WITA in Denpasar. We calculated their planetary positions with an astronomy library.

| | Raka | Nadia | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Taurus | Aries | 1, neighbours |
| Moon | Cancer | Pisces | 4, trine (both water) |
| Venus and Mars | Venus in Aries | Mars in Aries | 0, conjunction |

Looking only at sun signs, Taurus and Aries are often written up as a couple that "doesn't quite click": one wants slow and sure, the other wants fast. But both their Moons sit in water signs, which synastry reads as a sense of security that comes easily at home. Raka's Venus also falls at almost exactly the same degree as Nadia's Mars, a combination astrologers consider one of the most classic signs of attraction.

The lesson is that one line on a zodiac list can't stand in for two people's whole charts. If you want to read further, look for a site that calculates a birth chart from your date, time and city of birth. Remember to enter the time zone on your birth certificate, just as you would when working out Ba Zi.

## The 2027 sky for couples planning a wedding

Western astrology has a branch devoted to choosing times, called electional astrology. The two rules astrologers repeat most often for weddings: avoid Venus retrograde, since Venus is the planet of love, and if possible avoid Mercury retrograde, which is linked to misunderstandings and stalled paperwork. Some astrologers also avoid eclipse weeks.

The good news for 2027: **there is no Venus retrograde all year**. The Venus retrograde under way right now ends on 14 November 2026, and the next one only begins on 11 May 2028. So couples who follow this rule are free to pick any month in 2027.

From that table, a Western astrologer would usually point to two windows. The first is **15 May to 9 June 2027**, while Venus is in Taurus, with no Mercury retrograde and no eclipse. The second is **14 September to 6 October 2027**, while Venus is in Libra, before the third Mercury retrograde begins. Some astrologers also avoid a few weeks before and after a Mercury retrograde (the shadow period), so if you consult an astrologer, the window they give you may be narrower.

## When calendars meet

There is one interesting thing in that table. The first solar eclipse falls exactly on Chinese New Year, and the second falls exactly on the first day of the Hungry Ghost Month, which the Hong Kong Observatory table puts at 2 to 31 August 2027.

That's no coincidence. A solar eclipse can only happen at a new moon, and the Chinese lunar calendar begins every month at a new moon as well. So every solar eclipse lands on the first day of a Chinese lunar month.

For Muslim families, the first Mercury retrograde (10 February to 3 March) sits almost entirely inside Ramadan, which the Ministry of Religious Affairs calendar expects to run from 8 February to 9 March 2027. Receptions are rarely held during the fasting month anyway, so for many couples this retrograde changes nothing.

If your family follows more than one calendar, the Venus in Taurus and Venus in Libra windows can also be checked against the [good months to marry in 2027 by the Javanese calendar](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/bulan-baik-menikah-2027-kalender-jawa), the 2027 Year of the Goat, and the 2027 pretty wedding dates.

## What the research says

We need to be honest here. Zodiac compatibility has been tested against real marriage data several times, and the result is always the same.

In 1971, psychologist Bernard Silverman of Michigan State University compared the signs of couples who married and couples who divorced in Michigan. Couples with "compatible" signs were no less likely to divorce. In 2007, sociologist David Voas of the University of Manchester used the 2001 census of England and Wales, which recorded the birth dates of more than ten million married couples. Every sign combination turned up as often as chance would predict. In 2020, Jonas Helgertz and Kirk Scott of Lund University used Swedish register data from 1968 to 2001 and found no consistent evidence that astrologically better-matched couples were more likely to marry or less likely to divorce.

So if your signs and your partner's land on a "least compatible" list, no data says your marriage is at greater risk.

## So why read it at all?

Many couples use astrology as something to talk about, and that is where it turns out to be quite useful. Reading that a Taurus needs certainty and an Aries needs movement can open a conversation that has been hard to start, such as who will hold the wedding budget, or how soon you want a home of your own.

A few healthy ways to use it:

- Read the description of your partner's sign, then ask which parts they think are true and which miss. Their answer is often more interesting than the horoscope.
- If your distance is a square or an opposition, treat it as a reminder to agree on how you argue before the wedding day, rather than as a bad omen.
- If the wider family follows weton, shio or Ba Zi, put their reckoning first. Western astrology is rarely the reason parents object, and it shouldn't become the reason you argue with them.

## Frequently asked questions

### I was born on a changeover date. Which sign am I?

The sun changes sign at a particular hour, and that hour differs every year. Someone born on 20 April, for instance, could be an Aries or a Taurus depending on the year and time of birth. The surest way is to calculate a birth chart with your date, time and city of birth. The term "cusp" for people born on the boundary is mostly used in popular astrology. In birth chart calculation, the sun is always in exactly one sign.

### Is 2027 a good year to marry according to Western astrology?

By the rules astrologers use most often, fairly good, because there is no Venus retrograde. Mercury still goes retrograde three times, and some astrologers advise avoiding the eclipse weeks in early February and early August.

### Which matters more, the Western sign or the Chinese zodiac?

They come from different systems and don't replace each other. The Western sign is worked out from the sun's position on your date of birth, the Chinese zodiac from your year of birth. If your family is of Chinese descent, the Chinese zodiac and Ba Zi are usually what parents ask about, and you can check them with our [Ba Zi Couple Calculator](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-tools/kalkulator-bazi-pasangan).

### My partner and I share the same sign. Is that good?

In Western astrology, the same sign means the same element and the same modality. The reading is that you understand each other easily, but you also share the same blind spots. A Capricorn and Capricorn couple, for example, might be very tidy about the budget but both forget to plan time for fun.
