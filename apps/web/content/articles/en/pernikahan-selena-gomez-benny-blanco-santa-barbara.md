On the inside of her ceremony corset, Selena Gomez kept something her guests never saw. A small heart was embroidered there, holding the initials "S + B" and the wedding date. The public only found out when the details of the dress were reported.

Selena married Benny Blanco, who usually writes his name all in lower case, on Saturday, 27 September 2025 in Santa Barbara, California. There were about 170 guests. For a pop star's wedding that's a small number, and many of their choices feel close to what ordinary couples do: outfits that change with each part of the day, a heart-shaped cake and barefoot dancing.

Here's what was reported from the day, along with the parts you can borrow, whether your reception ends up in a Jakarta ballroom or in the family's garden.

## How the wedding unfolded

- **In the run-up:** a rehearsal dinner at a private estate in the Hope Ranch area.
- **Saturday, 27 September 2025:** a portrait session before the ceremony, then the ceremony and reception at Sea Crest Nursery, Santa Barbara, also in the Hope Ranch area.
- **Afterwards:** both posted photos on social media. Selena's caption was just the date, "9.27.25". Benny wrote "i married a real life disney princess", lower case and all.

## The venue and the guest count

The ceremony and reception were held in one place, Sea Crest Nursery. The rehearsal dinner took place at a private estate in the same area. Guests and family didn't have to travel far between the two events, and that's a logistical decision couples often underrate.

In Indonesia the pattern resembles a pengajian (prayer gathering) or midodareni (the Javanese eve-of-wedding night) held at the family home the day before the akad, the Islamic marriage contract. When the family home is far from the venue, many couples move the evening event somewhere closer so their parents aren't worn out the next morning.

The guest list ran to about 170 names, including Taylor Swift, Paul Rudd, Steve Martin, Martin Short, Paris Hilton and Ed Sheeran. That's closer to the size of a big-family akad than to a typical ballroom reception. If you're weighing a smaller wedding, this is an example of how a short guest list can still feel festive, as long as everyone who comes is genuinely close to the two of you.

## Three Ralph Lauren looks

Selena wore three outfits over the day, all by Ralph Lauren. Each was worn for a different part of the event:

1. **For the pre-ceremony portraits:** a halter dress in double-faced silk satin, with hand-cut lace and a macramé collar.
2. **For the ceremony:** a dress whose corset carried about 300 lace flowers, each shaped by hand. The embroidered heart was hidden inside this corset.
3. **For the reception:** a tulle dress.

To finish the look, Selena wore 12-carat Tiffany & Co. earrings. Benny wore a tuxedo, also by Ralph Lauren.

Indonesian brides are used to changing outfits, usually a kebaya for the akad and a gown or another traditional outfit for the reception. The way Selena split hers can serve as a guide. There's an outfit for the photo session, one for the most solemn moment and one for the party. If you plan to change outfits, first ask what each one is for, then choose the cut and fabric to suit.

## A hidden message inside the dress

An embroidered heart with "S + B" and the wedding date is a cheap detail to copy. Any kebaya tailor or gown designer can stitch initials and a date on the inside of an outfit, behind the lining or along the inner hem of the skirt. No guest will see it, and nobody needs to.

Many Indonesian couples already hide the wedding date in the figures of the mahar, the bride's marriage gift. Embroidery is another way to keep the same date, this time on something you'll keep for years. If your akad kebaya is later handed down to a child, that small stitch becomes part of its story.

## The bouquet, the cake and the barefoot dancing

Selena's bouquet was made of lily of the valley, a small white bell-shaped flower. The wedding cake was heart-shaped and read "Just Married". At the reception Selena and Benny danced barefoot. The day was photographed by Petra Collins.

A single-flower bouquet also makes the florist's job easier. If the flower you want is hard to find in Indonesia or only comes in as an import, ask early how long it lasts in the heat and what the substitute will be if stock runs out close to the day. Small white flowers that grow locally can often give a similar effect.

Barefoot dancing is easy to pull off at any reception. Put a basket of sandals or flat shoes near the dance floor, especially if the party is in a garden or on grass. Female guests who have stood for hours in heels will be grateful.

## What you can borrow

- Decide what each outfit is for before you order it. An outfit for photos, one for the ceremony and one for dancing have different needs.
- Ask your tailor to embroider your initials and wedding date inside the kebaya or gown.
- Hold the night-before event somewhere close to the main venue.
- If you want a small wedding, build the guest list from people who are truly close. Around 170 people is plenty for a warm party.
- Provide comfortable footwear for guests near the dance floor.
- Choose a single flower for the bouquet so it looks clean and reads clearly in photos.
