The first photo to go around came with no caption at all. On Sunday, 11 October 2026, actress Kim Go Eun posted an Instagram Story with a photo of a bride and groom standing side by side in front of a hanok, a traditional Korean house. She simply tagged the account @demi_kimee and added a single teary-eyed emoji.

The account belongs to Kim Yong Ji, an actress K-drama viewers know from *Mr. Sunshine* and *Tale of the Nine Tailed*. In the photo she wears an ivory off-the-shoulder gown and holds a red bouquet. There had never been any report that she was dating, let alone planning to marry.

On the same day, Bae Jung Nam, her co-star in *Mr. Sunshine*, posted a photo of himself standing between the bride and groom. Two days later, Kim Yong Ji confirmed the news herself on Instagram, with a caption just two lines long.

## What we know

- **Sunday, 11 October 2026:** Kim Yong Ji's wedding, held privately with no announcement and no dating reports beforehand.
- **Setting:** an outdoor space with a hanok backdrop in Seoul. The venue's name was not disclosed.
- **Bridal look:** an ivory off-the-shoulder gown, a lace veil and a red bouquet. The gown designer and the florist were not named.
- **The groom:** her husband is not a public figure. In the photos friends posted, his face is covered with an emoji.
- **Tuesday, 13 October 2026:** Kim Yong Ji announced the wedding herself on her Instagram account @demi_kimee, with several wedding photos.

## Who Kim Yong Ji is

Kim Yong Ji (also written Kim Yong-ji, 김용지) was born in 1991 and was 35 when she married. She started out as an advertising model in 2015. Her acting debut came in 2018 in the tvN drama *Mr. Sunshine*, as Hotaru, a silent astrologer.

After that she appeared in *Watcher* and *The Lies Within* on OCN, then in *The King: Eternal Monarch* on SBS. Many viewers remember her as Ki Yu-ri in *Tale of the Nine Tailed* (tvN, 2020), and as Sunwoo Eun-ho in its follow-up, *Tale of the Nine Tailed 1938* (2023). She has also been in the Netflix series *Somebody* and *The Art of Sarah*.

Kim Yong Ji and Kim Go Eun are close friends. Both were born in 1991, and they have been friends since both appearing in *The King: Eternal Monarch* (2020). In June 2026, Korean media reported on photos of Kim Yong Ji out with Kim Go Eun and Lee Sang Yi, also born in 1991. It makes sense that news of her wedding first surfaced on her friend's account.

## A ceremony in a hanok courtyard

The wedding took place in an outdoor space in Seoul. In the photos that circulated, the bride and groom stand side by side facing their guests, with a hanok as the backdrop. There was no announcement beforehand, and the public only found out after the ceremony, through her colleagues' posts.

This kind of setting has close equivalents in Indonesia. A joglo pendopo or the yard of a rumah adat, a traditional house, can make an equally strong backdrop for a ceremony. A traditional building brings its own atmosphere, so you need far less decoration. If you choose a place like this, ask the people who run it about a rain plan, because a ceremony in an open yard depends on the weather.

## An ivory gown and a red bouquet

Kim Yong Ji wore an ivory off-the-shoulder gown and a lace veil. The strongest colour in her look came from the red bouquet she held. The groom wore a dark suit.

The combination is simple, and that is exactly why the photos stick in the mind. Against an ivory gown and a hanok backdrop, the red bouquet draws the eye at once. For your own wedding, consider choosing just one accent colour and keeping everything else neutral. That colour can appear in the bouquet and in a small flower in the groom's jacket pocket, without being repeated in every corner of the decoration.

## Her dogs in the photos

In the photos she posted herself, Kim Yong Ji also poses with her dogs. Liputan6 noted two cream-coloured dogs posing with the couple in the hanok courtyard. Soompi said the dogs added a touch of charm to the special day.

If your pets are part of the family, there is no reason not to bring them to the photo session. Choose one person your pet knows well to look after them and take them home once the session is done, because the couple cannot manage that themselves. Make sure the venue allows animals, too, and schedule the photos with them early, before the crowd makes them restless.

## News that came from friends

Kim Go Eun's post that Sunday had no words in it, only the photo and the tag, plus a single emoji. Bae Jung Nam wrote "용지야 결혼 축하한데이", roughly "Yong Ji, congratulations on your wedding!" The word 축하한데이 is Gyeongsang dialect. In the photo he posted, the groom's face is covered with an emoji.

On Tuesday, 13 October 2026, Kim Yong Ji posted several wedding photos with an instant-film look. Her caption was two lines: "저는 이제 유부입니다🦦 / 축하해주셔서 감사합니다", meaning "I'm married now. Thank you for your congratulations." The word 유부 is Korean slang for a married person.

You can do the same if your wedding is small. Not everyone needs to know the date in advance. Post one or two photos after the day, write a short line that sounds like you, then say thank you. Relatives who were not invited tend to take news like this more easily than hearing it from someone else's post.

## A *Tale of the Nine Tailed* reunion

On 13 October, Kim Bum posted a photo from the reception captioned "We have a new family member". The photo Kim Bum posted shows Lee Dong Wook, Jo Bo Ah, Kim Bum himself, Hwang Hee and Ryu Kyung Soo, fellow cast members of the *Tale of the Nine Tailed* series, gathered at one table. Fans in Indonesia can see Kim Bum soon, since his 20th debut anniversary fan-meeting tour, *PAGE∞*, opens in Jakarta on Saturday, 24 October 2026.

A table like this is easy to arrange. Group your guests by where they know you from, for example colleagues from one project or friends from university. They relax more sitting with people they already know, and a one-table photo like Kim Bum's becomes a shared keepsake for them.

## What you can borrow

- Use a traditional house as the ceremony backdrop, such as a joglo pendopo or a rumah adat, and cut back on decoration because the building already tells a story.
- Have a rain plan ready if the ceremony is held in an open yard.
- Pick one strong accent colour, such as a red bouquet with an ivory gown, and keep the other elements neutral.
- Bring your pets to the photo session, with one person whose only job is looking after them.
- Announce the wedding after the day with a few photos and a short caption.
- Seat friends from the same circle at one table.
