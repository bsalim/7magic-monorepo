The first video call between the two sets of parents usually goes sweetly until one sentence. The Jakarta mum says, "We'll sort out the sangjit, then." The Singapore mum nods, then turns to her child and asks, "What's sangjit? Is that the Guo Da Li?" The couple look at each other. They haven't talked about it yet either.

The answer is yes. Sangjit in Indonesia and Guo Da Li (过大礼) in Singapore are the same ceremony: the groom's family brings betrothal gifts to the bride's family, and the bride's family returns part of them. What confuses cross-border couples usually isn't the meaning but the logistics. Whose custom applies, which country it happens in, when, and how to get trays of food through an airport.

## Different names, the same framework

The framework of the two ceremonies is almost identical. According to SingaporeBrides' Guo Da Li guide (2024), the groom presents the gifts with a matchmaker or an older female relative considered to bring good fortune. Among them is a red packet with the bride price, called ping jin, and the bride's family returns a portion of the gifts while presenting the bride's dowry. In Indonesia, the money is known as uang susu (milk money) and uang pesta (banquet money), alongside the return gifts, which we cover in our article on [Chinese wedding angpao and ang bao](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/angpao-pernikahan-tionghoa).

What differs isn't really the country. The contents of the trays depend more on the family's ancestry, whether Hokkien, Teochew, Hakka or Cantonese, and all of those groups live in both countries. We've mapped them in our article on [sangjit by dialect group](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/sangjit-hokkien-khek-tiociu-kanton-beda-aturan), so we won't repeat it here. A Hokkien family from Jakarta and a Hokkien family from Singapore may well have more in common than two Jakarta families from different groups.

## Whose custom leads

Traditionally, the tray contents follow the groom's family's customs, since the gifts come from them. The ceremony itself, though, takes place at the bride's family's home, and it's the bride's family who receive the guests. So one ceremony has two hosts with two areas: the groom's family decides what is brought, and the bride's family decides how they are received.

For a cross-border couple, that split actually helps. The groom's family doesn't have to guess another country's habits for the trays, and the bride's family doesn't have to receive guests in an unfamiliar way. What needs agreeing up front is only what touches both: how much of the banquet money is returned, what the bride's side gives back, and who buys the jewellery.

## Which country it happens in

Because the ceremony happens where the bride's family is, the "where" usually answers itself. If the bride is from Singapore, the Jakarta party flies over. If she's from Jakarta, the Singapore family comes.

What more often needs deciding is whether it stays at home or moves to a restaurant or hotel. For a party travelling from abroad, a restaurant is often more practical, because a sangjit almost always runs into lunch, and guests staying at a hotel don't have to find a family home. Some families also combine the sangjit with a visit for another event so the party only has to fly once.

## Tray items that travel

This is where cross-border couples get the biggest surprise. Sangjit trays hold food and drink, and both are tightly controlled at the border. If your party is flying from Jakarta to Singapore, these are some of the Singapore Food Agency (SFA) rules as of 2 November 2026:

- **Meat products, including canned ones, are only allowed from approved source countries.** For pork, Indonesia isn't on SFA's list of sources. For poultry, it is. If your family usually brings canned pig's trotters, check the country of origin on the can, not where you bought it.
- **Other processed food, such as biscuits, cakes, nuts and wine, is limited to 5 kg in total per person.**
- **Fruit and vegetables are allowed from any country, in a small, reasonable hand-carry quantity.** Eighteen oranges and eighteen apples for a single tray need careful counting.

For alcohol, Singapore Customs gives a duty-free concession to travellers aged 18 and over who have spent at least two days outside Singapore, for example one litre of spirits and one litre of wine, with other combinations available. Anything above that must be declared at the Red Channel and is charged duty and GST. For the other direction, from Singapore to Jakarta, check Indonesian customs rules before you travel.

What most often solves all of this is buying the perishable or heavy tray items in the destination city. In Singapore, wedding supply shops sell Guo Da Li sets by dialect group, and SingaporeBrides' guide lists several of them. From home, bring only the items that carry special meaning for the family, such as jewellery or fabric.

## When to hold it before Chinese New Year 2027

SingaporeBrides' guide says Guo Da Li is usually held two to four weeks before the wedding. In Jakarta, a sangjit is usually held between one week and one month before the wedding day, between 10am and 1pm. The two norms are close, so families from the two countries rarely disagree on the gap.

What needs attention is the holiday calendar. Chinese New Year 2027 falls on Saturday, 6 February 2027. In Indonesia, the joint ministerial decree set in September 2026 makes 6 February a public holiday and Friday, 5 February a collective leave day. In Singapore, the Ministry of Manpower has set 6 and 7 February as the Chinese New Year holidays, plus Monday, 8 February because the second day falls on a Sunday. These long weekends in both countries make it easier for families to gather, but they also mean a lot of people travelling on the same dates.

Some Chinese families also want the wedding to take place before Chinese New Year because of beliefs around the 2027 Year of the Goat, which we cover in our article on [marrying in the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/menikah-tahun-kambing-2027-tahun-janda). If that's your plan, the sangjit or Guo Da Li falls in December 2026 or January 2027, and flights and a venue for the travelling party are best sorted now.

## Who hosts what

For a quick overview, this is the split you'll most often find, based on the sources we read. Every family may have its own version.

| Event | Who gives or travels | Who receives or hosts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sangjit or Guo Da Li | Groom's family | Bride's family | Tray contents follow the groom's family's customs |
| Return gifts | Bride's family | Groom's family | Handed over at the end of the same visit |
| Tea ceremony | The couple | Each family, at home or combined at the venue | According to SingaporeBrides, the groom's family is served first |
| Banquet money | Groom's family | Bride's family, who usually keep only the tail | If the bride's family keeps all of it, they pay for the celebration |

On the tea ceremony, SingaporeBrides' guide (2020) describes the Singapore custom: on the wedding day, the groom's family is served in the morning after the groom has fetched the bride, and the bride's family in the afternoon. The two can be combined into one session at the venue as long as both families agree and the groom's family is still served first. For families living in two countries, that combined session is often the middle ground.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is sangjit the same as Guo Da Li?

Yes, both are the betrothal gift ceremony from the groom's family to the bride's before the wedding. Sangjit is the term used in Indonesia, Guo Da Li the one used in Singapore. The contents depend more on the family's dialect group than on the country.

### Can we bring canned pig's trotters from Jakarta to Singapore?

It depends on where the product was made. As of 2 November 2026, SFA's pork list doesn't include Indonesia, so Indonesian-made pork products aren't among those allowed. Canned products from countries on SFA's list may be brought in for personal consumption. Check the latest list on the SFA website before you travel.

### When should the sangjit be for a wedding before Chinese New Year 2027?

Using the one-week-to-one-month norm, the sangjit for a late January 2027 wedding falls roughly between late December and mid-January. Make sure the date is also worked out by the elder in your family who chooses dates.

### Does a marriage held in Singapore need registering in Indonesia?

Yes, if one of you is an Indonesian citizen. The registration steps, from ROM to Dukcapil, are explained in our article on [marrying an Indonesian in Singapore](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/wni-menikah-di-singapura).
