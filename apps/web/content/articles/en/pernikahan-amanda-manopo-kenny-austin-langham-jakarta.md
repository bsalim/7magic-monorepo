On 10 August 2025, Kenny Austin proposed to Amanda Manopo on the summit of Mount Merbabu. Two months later, on Friday, 10 October 2025, the two exchanged their vows at The Hampton Garden, the garden at The Langham, Jakarta.

Two months is a very short time to prepare a wedding. Many couples take six months to a year, especially if they want a hotel venue on a weekend. This couple, who first met on the set of the sinetron (TV drama) "Cinta Yasmin", showed that fast preparation can still be well run, as long as the division of work is clear.

We've gathered the details of their wedding that are most useful to you, especially if you're also racing the clock. Many of their choices still make sense on a much smaller budget.

## How the day unfolded

- **10 August 2025.** Kenny proposes to Amanda on the summit of Mount Merbabu.
- **Friday, 10 October 2025.** A Christian wedding ceremony at The Hampton Garden, The Langham, Jakarta, in the SCBD area of South Jakarta. The service was led by Pdt. (Reverend) Gideon Simanjuntak.
- **The same day.** The reception in The Langham's ballroom.

## Two months, with a planner in charge

The wedding was handled by Hilda by Bridestory, with Meyliana Tanoto as wedding planner. The decor was done by Classy Decor.

With so little time, a planner stops being a luxury. Their job is to make sure decisions are made quickly and vendors move at the same time, not one after another. If you have less than six months to prepare, consider a full-service planner, or at least one coordinator who holds every vendor's schedule.

There's also a simple way to speed things up. Choose a venue that can hold every part of the day, lock in the date first, then build the other decisions from there. The guest list, outfits and decor are much easier to decide once the date and place are fixed.

## A garden for the vows, a ballroom for the reception

The ceremony was held in the hotel garden, while the reception moved to the ballroom on the same day. Everything happened in one building.

This format has several practical advantages. Guests don't have to change venues or get stuck in traffic between the two events. Vendors only need to set up decor in one location. The couple can also rest and change outfits in a hotel room between events.

If you like the idea of an outdoor ceremony, ask the venue what the backup plan is if it rains. For a wedding in the wet months, an indoor backup space should be booked from the start, not hunted for on the day.

## A classic gown and a tailored suit

Amanda wore a gown designed by Hian Tjen, inspired by the style of Grace Kelly. Kenny wore a suit from Wong Hang Tailor.

Naming one person as inspiration is an effective way to talk to a designer. Rather than explaining cuts and fabrics at length, one reference both of you know can line up your pictures straight away. Bring a few photos of someone you admire to the first consultation, then let the designer translate them to your body and the concept of your day.

For the groom, a tailored suit has advantages over a rental: it fits properly, and the jacket can be worn again after the wedding. With a short lead time, make sure the tailor can finish the order with enough fittings.

## Favours the guests chose themselves

From the guests' side, this was the most memorable part. Every guest could choose one of three items: a 32-inch Android TV, a microwave or a juicer. On top of that there was a goodie bag containing a small piece of gold bullion.

The scale of these gifts may be far beyond most couples' budgets, but the idea can be applied. Giving guests a choice means the favours actually get used, and nothing ends up in a drawer. You could prepare two or three simple options, such as a small plant, a kitchen item or snacks, and ask guests to choose when they RSVP or at the favour table.

If you use a choice system, count your stock carefully. Ask guests to choose through the RSVP so you know how many of each item you need before ordering.

## Two hosts

The reception was hosted by Astrid Tiar and Evan Sanders. Having two MCs is common now, and the reasoning makes sense. One can lead the programme on stage while the other keeps things flowing with the planning team and vendors behind the scenes.

Whatever you choose, make sure your MC receives the final running order and the list of names to mention at least a few days before the event. With short preparation, this is easy to miss. Write down how to pronounce any unfamiliar family names too, so nobody gets mispronounced in front of the guests.

## What you can borrow

- **Use a planner if your lead time is short.** One person holding every vendor's schedule saves a lot of stalled decisions.
- **Lock in the date and venue first.** Other decisions can be arranged once those two are certain.
- **Hold the ceremony and reception in one place.** Guests don't have to move, and the couple has somewhere to rest.
- **Have a rain plan for an outdoor event.** Book a backup space from the start.
- **Bring one style reference to your designer.** One name or a few photos line up your vision faster than a long explanation.
- **Let guests choose their favours.** Two or three simple options through the RSVP make the favours more likely to be used.
