Once the reception is over, many newlyweds realise that guests from Jakarta, Surabaya or abroad still have two or three days in Bali and are waiting for a plan. At the same time, the two of you want time alone after months of preparation. The days after a Bali wedding work best when you separate those two needs from the start.

This article is the big-picture map. We cover how to plan the days after the wedding, the best picks in each area, where to stay a night or two for a short honeymoon, and the practical points that apply specifically to Indonesian travellers. The detail for each area is in the series articles we link to in every section.

## How to plan the days after the wedding

**Keep the first day for rest.** Do not book a pre-dawn tour for the morning after the reception. Leave that day empty for sleep, a late breakfast and packing up. Guests who still have energy can be pointed to something close to their hotel.

**Separate couple time from group outings.** Groups of guests do best with activities that are easy to organise for many people, such as a shared beach-club daybed, a seafood dinner on the sand, or brunch at a café with a view. For the two of you, choose something slow and quiet, for example a *melukat* purification ritual, a spa, or a night out of town. One group outing is enough; let guests choose the rest from a list you share.

**Traffic decides, not distance.** Since 8 September 2026, central Ubud has run Phase III of its one-way scheme. Tour buses are barred from the centre and sent to Dalem Puri, and the first day brought more than four hours of gridlock. In Uluwatu, the Sterling Shortcut closed in February 2026, and since 2 June 2026 a turn-restriction trial has run daily from 2pm to 10pm at six Pecatu intersections. Officials warned that peak trips can take three hours or more. So pick one area per day and stay there into the evening rather than driving across the island to catch a sunset.

## Nuanu and Tabanan

Nuanu is a 44-hectare creative district on Pantai Nyanyi in Tabanan, about 10 minutes from Tanah Lot according to its official site. It comes alive after dark.

- **For the two of you:** a day at Lumeira, a complex with a wood-fired sauna, cold plunges and a hammam, then a walk along Aurora Media Park, a 1 km forest trail with light installations that switch on after 6:30pm and are free.
- **For guests:** Luna Beach Club, with a fire and light show every day at 6:45pm.
- **For everyone:** sunset at Tanah Lot, which draws a large share of domestic visitors.

Prices, hours and options for families with children are in our article on [Nuanu and Tabanan after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/wisata-nuanu-tabanan-setelah-menikah-di-bali).

## Ubud and Kintamani

Ubud suits couples, while Kintamani suits a group with mixed fitness levels.

- **For the two of you:** the *melukat* ritual at Tirta Empul (IDR 75,000 per adult as of March 2026, sarong and sash required), then the Campuhan Ridge Walk at sunrise.
- **For guests:** the Legong and Barong dances at Ubud Palace every night from 7:30pm.
- **For guests in Kintamani:** brunch at a caldera café on Jalan Raya Penelokan between 9am and 11:30am, after the sunrise crowds clear and before the clouds roll in. Pahdi Specialty Coffee has a lift, which makes it comfortable for older guests.

Because of the one-way scheme, large groups should get off at Dalem Puri and walk or switch to small cars. More in our article on [Ubud and Kintamani for newlyweds and wedding guests](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/ubud-kintamani-untuk-pengantin-baru-dan-tamu-pernikahan).

## Kuta, Seminyak, Uluwatu and Jimbaran

South Bali wins on sunsets, but it is where you lose the most time on the road.

- **Uluwatu Kecak:** two troupes alternate nightly. Arrive between 4pm and 5pm because seats can sell out in peak season, and plan to stay on the Bukit all afternoon.
- **Seafood dinner in Jimbaran:** nibble.id names Made Bagus Cafe and Menega Cafe, where you pick your own fish. Check before you go, since those articles are undated.
- **Alunê at W Bali Seminyak:** a new beach club on the old Woobar site, with private jacuzzi cabanas for up to six people.
- **Waterbom in Kuta:** for guests travelling with children.

More options and timings are in our article on [things to do with guests after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/aktivitas-bersama-tamu-setelah-menikah-di-bali-kuta-uluwatu-canggu).

## Canggu

Canggu is the most practical choice for a group of guests.

- **A shared daybed at FINNS or Atlas in Berawa:** general entry is free, while reserved seating carries a minimum spend the group can split. Third-party resellers quote different amounts, so confirm directly with the venue. What closed in 2025 was FINNS Recreation Club, the sports venue. FINNS Beach Club remains open.
- **A morning surf lesson at Batu Bolong:** the waves are softer for beginners, and the wind usually picks up by mid-morning.
- **For the two of you:** a quieter sunset at Pererenan or Seseh, at around 6pm to 6:30pm.

The detail is in the same article as south Bali, [things to do with guests after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/aktivitas-bersama-tamu-setelah-menikah-di-bali-kuta-uluwatu-canggu).

## A night or two beyond the hubs

The best short honeymoons lie two to three hours from Canggu or Seminyak, and they work better as overnights than as day trips. For a longer trip, see our [honeymoon budget guide](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/bulan-madu-setelah-menikah-panduan-anggaran).

### Amed and Lempuyang

To take the "Gates of Heaven" photo at Lempuyang from Canggu, Seminyak or Kuta, you need to leave at about 2:30am. From Amed it is about 30 minutes. According to Travjoy, the dry-season queue is under 30 minutes if you reach the terminal by 5:15am, about 1.5 to 2 hours at 8am, and 4 to 6 hours from 11am on busy weekends. Worth knowing: the "lake reflection" in the photos is a mirror held under the phone camera. Bring cash, because the counter takes cash only. Tirta Gangga is nearby and has a lower rate for domestic visitors.

### Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Penida

Nusa Lembongan, under an hour by fast boat from Sanur, is calmer for a couple. Take boat safety seriously: a ferry capsized near Nusa Penida on 5 August 2025, with at least two dead. Choose an operator with proper life jackets, a radio and Ministry of Transportation registration, check BMKG swell warnings, and postpone the crossing if the swell is above about 1.5 metres. Most incidents have happened in the rainy season, from November to April.

Since 1 September 2026, Klungkung has run a "One Gate One Destination" system on Nusa Penida. Foreign tourists pay an island entry fee at the port plus a ticket at each site, while Indonesian tourists pay only the site tickets. We have not found the new fee amounts in an official source. The first day brought long queues at Kelingking and Crystal Bay, so buy QR tickets before you cross.

### Munduk and North Bali

A Lovina sunrise dolphin trip followed by Sekumpul Waterfall is usually sold as a day trip of about 8 hours. A night in Munduk, cool and surrounded by waterfalls, makes the next morning far more relaxed.

## Practical notes for Indonesian travellers

Many attractions in Bali charge two prices, and the gap widened in 2026. Bring your KTP, because the domestic rate at Monkey Forest applies to Indonesian citizens and KTP holders.

| Site | Indonesian adult | Foreign adult | Effective from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tanah Lot | IDR 40,000 | IDR 100,000 | 1 April 2026 |
| Ulun Danu Beratan | IDR 50,000 | IDR 100,000 | 1 July 2026 |
| Monkey Forest, Ubud | IDR 90,000 | IDR 130,000 | 1 April 2026 |
| Uluwatu Temple | IDR 40,000 | IDR 60,000 | Price as of 3 January 2026 |
| Jatiluwih | IDR 25,000 | IDR 75,000 | Effective date not confirmed |

At Jatiluwih, Bali residents pay IDR 15,000. Check the price at the counter, since rates can change.

**The foreign tourist levy.** Since 14 February 2024, every foreign tourist pays a levy of IDR 150,000 through the official Love Bali portal. Indonesian citizens do not pay it. In 2025 only about a third of foreign visitors paid, so send your foreign guests the Love Bali link before they fly.

**QRIS and cash.** The Uluwatu Temple counter takes cash and QRIS (GoPay, OVO, DANA) but not international credit or debit cards. That is easy for you, but foreign guests need to carry cash. Monkey Forest takes QRIS, cards and cash.

**Halal food.** In our research, not a single venue was confirmed as halal-certified by BPJPH or SiHalal. nibble.id's list of halal restaurants in Seminyak (25 July 2023) does not name a certifier, and its nasi campur list also includes halal picks. Plant-based cafés such as KYND, Zest and Alchemy use no pork, but they are not halal-certified. Babi guling, for example at Warung Ibu Oka in Ubud, is clearly not an option for Muslim guests. Check each venue on SiHalal before you recommend it to family.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Indonesian guests pay the IDR 150,000 levy?

No. The levy applies only to foreign tourists and is paid through Love Bali. Since 1 September 2026, Indonesian citizens also do not pay the Nusa Penida island entry fee.

### Which area is best for a group outing with guests?

Canggu for a shared beach-club daybed, Jimbaran for a seafood dinner, and Kintamani for brunch with a view of Mount Batur. Pick the one closest to where most of your guests are staying.

### Is it safe to cross to Nusa Lembongan in the rainy season?

It can be, provided you choose a registered operator and check BMKG swell warnings before you leave. Most boat incidents have happened between November and April, so have a backup plan if the sea is high.

### Do I need to leave before dawn for Lempuyang?

If you are staying in Canggu or Seminyak, yes, at about 2:30am. It is more comfortable to spend a night in Amed, only 30 minutes from Lempuyang.
