On Sunday morning, 26 April 2026, gamelan had been playing in the Dian Ballroom at Raffles Hotel Jakarta since 10:00. At around 11:00, El Rumi spoke the ijab kabul, the vow that seals an Islamic marriage. Habib Usman bin Yahya noted that El said it in a single breath.

The akad itself took only a few minutes. The preparation ran for months, from a proposal in January to a siraman two days before. Plenty of the decisions along the way work for ordinary couples too: how to pick a colour for the siraman, how to build the mahar, how to hold an akad in a hotel through the KUA, and how to split the work between two wedding organisers.

## From proposal to akad

- **Wednesday, 21 January 2026**: the lamaran (formal proposal) at Hutan Kota by Plataran, Senayan, in the Melati Glass House. It aired on SCTV the next day, Thursday 22 January.
- **Friday, 24 April 2026**: the siraman in the Bangka area of South Jakarta.
- **Sunday, 26 April 2026**: the akad nikah in the Dian Ballroom, Raffles Hotel Jakarta. Gamelan from 10:00, ijab kabul at around 11:00.

From proposal to akad took about three months. A window that short calls for a vendor team that's complete from the start. At this wedding, two wedding organisers worked together.

## A proposal in a glasshouse

El and Syifa's lamaran was held in the Melati Glass House, part of Hutan Kota by Plataran in Senayan. The decor was by Rangkai Riefinka, and both of them wore Studio Boh.

There are two things to take from this. First, a proposal doesn't have to happen at home. A glass-walled room among the trees gives a two-family event a more relaxed feel, and makes it easier for the photographer to work with natural light. Second, dressing both of you from one label makes you look matched straight away without much coordination.

## A sky-blue siraman and seven sources of water

For the siraman, Syifa and El's outfits were designed by Didiet Maulana. The colour was sky blue, El's favourite, inspired by the series Bridgerton. The outfit carried crystals in seven colours to stand for the seven sources of water used in the siraman. Even the ronce melati, the strings of jasmine, were set with crystals, and the cloth was batik in the Wahyu Tumurun motif.

The lesson is in how they tied details to a story. The colour came from one partner's favourite, while the number of crystal colours followed the number of water sources in the ritual. You don't need crystals to copy this. Just make sure every big choice in your outfits and decor has a reason you can tell.

## An akad in a hotel, still through the KUA

The akad took place in a hotel, but it was registered through the KUA Setiabudi, the local religious affairs office. For couples who want their akad in a venue or hotel, this is an important reminder: the paperwork with the KUA has to be finished well before the day, including the schedule for the penghulu, the official who comes to the venue.

At this akad, the penghulu acted as wali hakim, the official guardian who gives the bride away. Who will act as wali is something to settle with the KUA right at the start of the paperwork, whatever your family situation. The sooner it's discussed, the calmer the akad day.

The two witnesses were Sakti Wahyu Trenggono and Adhyaksa Dault. Guests included President Prabowo Subianto, Bahlil Lahadalia, Ayu Ting Ting, Lesti Kejora and Ari Lasso.

## A mahar that spells the date

El's mahar to Syifa was 26 grams and 4 grams of logam mulia (gold bullion), plus £2,026 in cash. Read in order, the numbers give the akad date: 26, 4, 2026.

This approach keeps gaining ground because guests grasp the meaning at once and the value can be scaled. Use grams of gold for the day and month, then a cash amount for the year, in whatever currency you like. The day and the month can also be two separate pieces, as El did.

## The akad look and the team behind it

Syifa's akad kebaya was made by Vera Anggraini of Vera Kebaya, paired with a classic brown batik. Her make-up was by Bennu Sorumba, the paes by Ambar Paes, the headpiece by Rinaldy Yunardi and the jewellery by Lotus Gold.

Note that the make-up artist and the paes artist were two different people. Paes is the forehead make-up of a Javanese bride and calls for specialist skill. If you're following Javanese adat, ask your chosen make-up artist whether they also do paes, or whether you need a separate artist.

The wedding was handled by Hilda by Bridestory together with MKE Wedding Organizer. Two WOs at one wedding can work well as long as the split of duties is written down early: who looks after the family, who looks after the vendors, and who has the final say on the day.

## The reception and the first dance

For the reception, Syifa changed into a gown by Tex Saverio. For their first dance the couple chose "Back At One", and the song was performed live by the man who sang it, Brian McKnight. Dewa 19 also played. El is the son of Ahmad Dhani and Maia Estianty, and the younger brother of Al Ghazali, who married in June 2025; you can read about his brother's wedding in [our article on Al Ghazali and Alyssa Daguise](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-al-ghazali-alyssa-daguise-st-regis-jcc).

The original singer may be out of reach, but the idea isn't. Pick one song that truly belongs to the two of you, then ask your reception band or singer to perform it live for the first dance. Send them the version you want a few weeks ahead so they have time to rehearse.

## What you can borrow

- Consider a green, light-filled venue for the proposal. The mood is more relaxed and the light is good for photos.
- Tie the colour of your siraman or akad to a personal story, such as one partner's favourite colour.
- Build the mahar from the numbers in your akad date. Grams of gold for the day and month, cash for the year.
- If your akad is in a hotel or venue, settle the KUA paperwork and the question of the wali well ahead.
- For Javanese adat, confirm who will do the paes, because not every make-up artist does.
- If you're using two WOs, write down the split of duties before the contracts are signed.
