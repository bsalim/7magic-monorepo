If you have a prewedding shoot booked at Barelang Bridge or along Marina Bay this week, your photographer has probably already sent you the same message: the sky is white, the bridge is barely visible, do you still want to go ahead? Smoke haze from forest and land fires in Sumatra and Kalimantan is covering the Riau Islands and Singapore, and NEA expects Singapore's air to stay unhealthy for the next few days.

The good news is that your shoot does not have to be cancelled. This article helps you decide using the air quality index, then move the session indoors without losing the story you want to tell.

## Conditions as of 12 October 2026

The figures below can change within a day. Check again the evening before your shoot and on the morning itself.

**Singapore.** At 6pm on 11 October, the National Environment Agency (NEA) recorded a 24-hour PSI of 112-155, in the *Unhealthy* range, and forecast that it would stay there. According to NEA, the smoke is coming from southern Sumatra and West Kalimantan. Two days earlier, on 9 October, the reading was higher still at 135-192, with a forecast of *Unhealthy* to the low end of *Very Unhealthy*.

**Batam.** Batam's air quality index reached 177 on the morning of 9 October, with PM2.5 at 107 µg/m³, and was still at 165 on 11 October. BMKG recorded visibility of only 1 to 3 km. Around 473 cases of acute respiratory infection (ISPA) were recorded in Batam on 5 October, and PAUD, primary and junior high pupils in Batam city have been learning from home since 29 September.

**Bintan and Tanjungpinang.** Tanjungpinang's education office moved PAUD to junior high schools to learning from home from 8 October, "until air quality improves". BMKG describes the air across the Riau Islands as unhealthy, with visibility of 1 to 3 km. We have not found separate data for the Lagoi resort area, so assume conditions are similar until a reading shows otherwise.

Where the smoke comes from is clear. On 7 October, BMKG detected 4,806 hotspots on Sumatra, 3,689 of them in South Sumatra and 438 in Jambi.

## Three numbers, three scales

This is where many couples misread the situation. Three indices are in circulation, and their numbers cannot simply be compared.

- **ISPU** is Indonesia's official index. Under Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation No. 14 of 2020 its categories are: 0-50 Good (Baik), 51-100 Moderate (Sedang), 101-200 Unhealthy (Tidak Sehat), 201-300 Very Unhealthy (Sangat Tidak Sehat), and above 300 Hazardous (Berbahaya). You can follow it at [ispu.kemenlh.go.id](https://ispu.kemenlh.go.id), updated every 30 minutes, and in the SatuSehat app.
- **PSI** is Singapore's index, published by NEA at haze.gov.sg. Its bands are the same as ISPU's (0-50 Good, 51-100 Moderate, 101-200 Unhealthy, 201-300 Very Unhealthy, above 300 Hazardous), but it is a different index from different monitoring stations, so do not treat Singapore's PSI as Batam's ISPU.
- **AQI** in weather apps and on sites such as IQAir or aqi.in uses yet another formula. The 177 and 165 figures for Batam above come from a scale like this.

A simple rule of thumb: for a shoot in Batam or Bintan, decide on ISPU. For a shoot in Singapore, use NEA's PSI. Treat AQI from an app as a rough guide only.

## Go ahead, move indoors, or reschedule

NEA gives clear activity advice for each PSI band. The ISPU bands use the same cut-offs, so you can borrow this advice for Batam and Bintan, with a little caution.

**Good or Moderate (0-100).** NEA advises normal activities for everyone. An outdoor shoot can go ahead as planned. Choose the morning if you can, because in Singapore rain tends to fall in the afternoon and evening.

**Unhealthy (101-200).** For healthy people, NEA advises *reducing* prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion. The elderly, pregnant women and children are asked to *minimise* it, and people with chronic lung or heart disease to *avoid* it. For a prewedding, our advice: move the core of the session indoors. If there is one outdoor backdrop you really want, take it in 10 to 15 minutes, then go back inside.

**Very Unhealthy (201-300).** NEA asks healthy people to avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion. At this point, take the outdoor part off the schedule. An indoor session can still go ahead, as long as the trips between locations are short and you are not waiting around outside.

**Hazardous (above 300).** Everyone is asked to minimise or avoid outdoor activity. Reschedule. The photos you would get are not worth the risk, especially if your parents are coming along.

There is another consideration besides health. Even at Unhealthy levels, the sky turns a whitish grey and distant backdrops disappear. If the reason you chose a location was the Marina Bay skyline or the span of Barelang Bridge, the photo you have in mind will not happen that day, whatever the index says.

## Indoor locations

Book an indoor backup now, before you actually need it. When the haze thickens, every couple with a shoot near your date will be looking for the same places.

### Singapore

Singapore has several indoor locations that do take wedding photo sessions, and all of them need a booking or permit in advance.

- **Flower Dome and Cloud Forest, Gardens by the Bay.** These two cooled conservatories are the closest answer for couples who want a garden feel without breathing the outside air. The operator requires approval for commercial photography, applied for at least 14 days ahead, and does not allow tripods on weekends and public holidays.
- **National Gallery Singapore.** Columned corridors and domed halls in the former Supreme Court and City Hall buildings. Wedding photo sessions must be booked at least 10 working days ahead, with a maximum of five people per group, handheld equipment only, and no artworks in frame.
- **Jewel Changi Airport.** The HSBC Rain Vortex and Canopy Park suit couples who have just landed. Jewel requires an application for all wedding photo and video sessions.

The costs, best times and permit process for all three are in our [guide to 14 unique prewedding locations in Singapore](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/photography/lokasi-prewedding-unik-singapura). Hotel lobbies and ballrooms are often overlooked too. Many [five-star hotels in Singapore](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/singapore) have grand staircases, long corridors and floor-to-ceiling windows that stay bright even when the sky outside is grey.

### Batam

Batam has no conservatories or galleries on Singapore's scale, so hotels are your mainstay. An empty ballroom outside event hours, a lobby with a staircase, a restaurant before service and a suite with big windows can give you three or four different backdrops in one building, without stepping into the smoky air between outfit changes.

The options we list include [Harris Waterfront Batam](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/batam/harris-waterfront-batam), [Oakwood Hotel & Apartments Grand Batam](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/batam/oakwood-hotel-apartments-grand-batam) and [Harris Resort Barelang](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/batam/harris-resort-barelang). See them all in our [Batam venue list](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/batam). We have not found a Batam hotel that officially lists a prewedding photo package, so ask the sales or events team directly: which spaces you may use, at what times, and whether there is a usage fee.

A photo studio is a sensible choice here too. A plain backdrop or themed set makes your photos completely independent of the weather, and many couples use the [minimalist, negative-space prewedding style](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/photography/prewedding-negative-space-minimalis) that is strongest in a studio anyway.

### Bintan

Resorts in the Lagoi area generally have ballrooms for weddings. In the haze, look for the enclosed parts: the ballroom, a glass-walled restaurant, the lobby, or a villa with a large living room. As in Batam, we have not found a resort that openly offers a prewedding permit or package on its official website, so contact the resort before you book the ferry.

If you are crossing from Singapore or Batam, also ask the ferry operator about possible schedule changes due to visibility. At the time of writing we had found no reports of disrupted sailings, but visibility in the Riau Islands has dropped as low as 1 km.

## Making the most of haze light

Smoke haze works like a giant diffuser. Shadows soften and skin looks even, which is something portrait photographers usually go looking for. Towards sunset, the sun can appear as a large orange disc you can look at directly, and a few silhouette frames with a sun like that can become the most memorable photos in your album.

What you lose is depth. Distant buildings, the open sea and the contrast of the sky all fade. So ask your photographer to shift the plan towards tighter frames: half-length portraits, details of hands and rings, and backdrops close to you. Avoid plans that depend on the horizon.

## Looking after the couple and crew

The Ministry of Health asks the public to wear masks and limit outdoor activity during the haze, especially children, pregnant women, the elderly, and people with asthma or lung disease. On the day of the shoot, a few things help:

- **N95 masks between shots.** NEA says an N95 is not needed for short exposure or indoors. For people outdoors for several hours, NEA suggests an N95 when the PSI is above 300, or above 200 for vulnerable groups. The elderly, pregnant women and people with heart or lung conditions should consult a doctor before wearing one.
- **Parents wait inside.** If family members are joining, set up an air-conditioned waiting room and bring them out only for the group frames.
- **Eyes and makeup.** Smoke makes eyes sting and water, and makeup fades fast in humid air. Bring eye drops and set aside time for touch-ups. Makeup tips for this kind of climate are in our [guide to long-lasting makeup for tropical weather](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/beauty-fashion/makeup-tahan-lama-cuaca-tropis).
- **Fewer outdoor outfit changes.** Every change means walking back and forth. Group all the outdoor backdrops into a single outfit.

## Before you pay a deposit

Smoke haze is not a rare event in this region, and this year it has already lasted for weeks. If you are about to book a photographer, studio or venue for a session in the coming months, ask these questions and get the answers in writing:

- How many times you can reschedule free of charge, and how many days' notice you must give.
- Whether haze and bad weather count as grounds for rescheduling, or only force majeure such as a disaster.
- Whether the deposit is forfeited if you move the session to another location run by the same vendor.

We have collected similar questions for reception venues in [12 questions to ask before signing a venue contract](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/12-pertanyaan-sebelum-tanda-tangan-venue-pernikahan).

Smoke haze forces you to change your plans, but it rarely forces you to cancel them. Watch the ISPU or PSI from the evening before, and book an indoor backup while there are still free slots.
