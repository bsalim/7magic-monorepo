Many Bali prewedding albums look alike, because couples go to the same spots at the same hour. Yet the island still has grassland with Gunung Agung rising behind it, and a waterfall that drops inside a narrow rock cleft, with shafts of light coming down from above.

We put this list together mainly for Indonesian couples flying to Bali just for the shoot, and also for couples from abroad. The tourist levy and most entrance tickets differ for domestic and foreign visitors, so we give both. For each location we describe what the photos look like, how to get there, and whatever fee or permit we could confirm. We checked every rule and fee below on 7 October 2026. If you are also weighing up a neighbouring country, we have put together [14 unique prewedding locations in Singapore](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/photography/lokasi-prewedding-unik-singapura) in the same format.

One thing to know from the start: only four locations on this list have a prewedding fee from a source we can stand behind, namely Danau Tamblingan, Taman Soekasada Ujung, Kertha Gosa and Kelingking Beach. The others sell entrance tickets but publish no prewedding tariff, and the fee is usually negotiated on the spot. Confirm the shoot fee with the site manager before the day.

## Rules that apply everywhere

### The foreign tourist levy

Every foreign tourist visiting Bali pays a levy of Rp150,000 per person, paid electronically. It rests on Bali Provincial Regulation (Perda) No. 6 of 2023, in force since 14 February 2024, as amended by Perda No. 2 of 2025, in force since 1 July 2025. A "foreign tourist" here means someone from outside Indonesia who is not an Indonesian citizen, so **Indonesian couples do not pay this levy**. If one of you is not an Indonesian citizen, that person pays.

Perda 2/2025 says the levy is paid "sebelum atau selama berwisata di Bali" (before or during your stay in Bali). Love Bali encourages paying before arrival, at [lovebali.baliprov.go.id](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/) or through the Love Bali app. Payment is non-refundable, and you receive a digital voucher. Seven groups are exempt (Perda 2/2025, Article 4A(2)):

- crew on a means of transport
- KITAS or KITAP holders
- diplomatic and service visa holders
- family-unification visa holders
- student visa holders
- golden visa holders
- "pemegang jenis visa lainnya" (holders of other visa types)

Golden visa and "other visa" holders apply for the exemption through Love Bali at least 5 days before arrival. If you apply later than that, you pay. Foreign tourists who do not pay face an oral warning, a written warning and/or refusal of service at tourist attractions.

Nusa Penida adds its own charge. From 1 September 2026, Klungkung Regency collects a levy under Perda Kab. Klungkung No. 4 of 2026, on top of the provincial levy. Foreign visitors to Nusa Penida or Lembongan-Ceningan pay an area levy of Rp25,000 per adult and Rp15,000 per child aged 4-13. On top of that, each tourist attraction (DTW) charges Rp25,000 per adult and Rp15,000 per child, paid by foreign and domestic visitors alike.

### Code of conduct: Governor's Circular No. 07 of 2025

The code of conduct in force today is Bali Governor's Circular (SE) No. 07 of 2025, "Tatanan Baru bagi Wisatawan Asing selama berada di Bali" (the new code for foreign tourists in Bali). Governor Wayan Koster signed it on 21 March 2025, and it replaces SE 4/2023. As of 7 October 2026, we found no newer circular. It is addressed to foreign tourists, but as an Indonesian couple you should still follow its temple rules. The parts that matter most for a shoot:

- **Obligations.** Respect the sanctity of temples, pratima and religious symbols, and local customs during ceremonies. Dress modestly at sacred areas, tourist attractions and public places. Be accompanied by a licensed tour guide at tourist attractions, and follow each site's own rules.
- **Inner and middle temple courts.** Do not enter the Utamaning Mandala and Madyaning Mandala, except to pray in Balinese or prayer dress, and only when not menstruating.
- **Climbing.** Do not climb sacred trees or sacred buildings.
- **What you wear in photos.** The circular bans conduct that desecrates sacred places, temples and religious symbols, such as climbing sacred buildings and "berfoto dengan pakaian tidak sopan/tanpa pakaian" (posing for photos in indecent clothing or without clothing). For any shoot near a temple, this is the line that matters most when you choose a prewedding wardrobe.
- **Environment.** Do not pollute lakes, springs, rivers or the sea, and do not use single-use plastics.
- **Working without papers.** Do not work or do business without official documents. The circular says nothing specific about photography.

Violations can be reported to the "Siaga" WhatsApp line at +62 812-8759-0999, and Satpol PP Bali enforces the rules. The circular does not cover drones.

### Temple and sacred-site etiquette

Many locations on this list sit next to a temple or are held sacred by local people. Wear a kamen or sarong; as a general rule the sarong falls below the knee, with your shoulders covered. At Pura Besakih, the kamen and udeng are described as "pakaian wajib bagi wisatawan" (required dress for visitors), and at Pura Ulun Danu Beratan the kamen is required, with a selendang (sash) optional. Women who are menstruating do not enter a temple. Do not climb shrines, sacred buildings or sacred trees. In 2022, MDA Tabanan went as far as asking customary villages to post prohibition signs in three languages after a tourist climbed a sacred banyan tree.

Ceremonies can also close a site. Pura Luhur Lempuyang, for example, closes to tourists for every pujawali (piodalan), as it did on 23-27 April 2025. When you book, ask the site manager about the ceremony calendar of the nearest temple. At Jatiluwih, the manager's own rules forbid entering the sacred area or a temple without a guide.

### Drones

The national basis is Minister of Transportation Regulation PM 37 of 2020, signed on 2 June 2020, promulgated on 8 June 2020, and still in force. The points that matter for a shoot:

- Flying in controlled airspace or inside an airport's KKOP (airport safety zone) needs approval from the Director General of Civil Aviation.
- In uncontrolled airspace, a drone may fly up to "400 feet (120 m) tanpa persetujuan Direktur Jenderal" (without the Director General's approval). Anything higher needs approval.
- A drone under 55 lbs used "untuk kepentingan komersial harus mendapatkan penilaian safety assessment dari Direktur Jenderal" (for commercial purposes must get a safety assessment from the Director General), and non-hobby use must meet CASR Part 107.
- For "pemotretan atau pemfilman, operator harus memiliki surat persetujuan dari institusi/pihak yang berwenang" (photography or filming, the operator must hold an approval letter from the competent institution or party). The application attaches a letter from the competent institution and/or the owner of the object being filmed.
- Camera drones may not operate within 500 m of the outer edge of a prohibited or restricted area.

PM 37/2020 does not say whether a prewedding shoot with a paid photographer counts as "commercial", and we will not guess for you. Ask your photographer which permits they already hold, then confirm with the site manager. We also found no Bali-wide ban on drones over temples, because the rules are set site by site. Desa Penglipuran bans drones, while Jatiluwih allows them with prior authorisation and an extra charge of IDR 300,000. For the other 13 locations we could not confirm the drone rules or fees, so we do not repeat that in each section.

### Weather and seasons

In a press release of 22 September 2026, BMKG (Indonesia's meteorology agency) forecast that the 2026/2027 rainy season would begin in part of Bali in November 2026 and in most of Bali in December 2026. The peak is forecast for January 2027 in central Bali and February 2027 in most of Bali. Nationally, the rainy season starts later than normal across 61.08% of Indonesia's area. BMKG also notes that a "very strong" El Niño is under way, and El Niño conditions are expected to persist into early 2027.

In practice, a shoot in October 2026 still falls before the rainy-season onset BMKG forecasts (November in part of Bali), while a shoot in January or February 2027 falls at its peak. Two locations run on their own seasons, the Jatiluwih rice fields and the Tianyar grassland; the details are in their sections.

### Nyepi 2027

Nyepi, the Saka New Year 1949, falls on Monday, 8 March 2027, under the joint ministerial decree (SKB 3 Menteri) on 2027 national holidays. It lasts 24 hours, from 6am to 6am WITA the next day. Tourists must stay at their accommodation, and the Catur Brata Penyepian applies: amati geni (no fire or lights), amati karya (no work), amati lelungan (no travel) and amati lelanguan (no entertainment).

On Nyepi 2026, Ngurah Rai Airport stopped regular commercial flights from 6am WITA on 19 March to 6am WITA on 20 March, and ferries such as Ketapang-Gilimanuk and Padangbai-Lembar stopped too. The 2027 airport closure has not been announced yet. It is expected to follow the usual 24-hour closure, but check again closer to the date. Do not schedule any shoot on 8 March 2027.

### Two well-known locations we left out

Pura Lempuyang Luhur, with its famous gate, is an active temple that closes to tourists for every pujawali, and we found no statement from its managers allowing prewedding shoots there. If you visit anyway, Karangasem's official tourism portal asks visitors not to use coarse language, says menstruating women may not climb Gunung Lempuyang because the area is sacred, and asks for a kamben in the main temple area.

We also skipped TWA Sangeh in Badung. On 3 December 2025, a tree fell there and killed a tour guide. We could not confirm whether the park has reopened, or on what terms, so for now we do not recommend it for a shoot.

## 1. Jatiluwih: time your date to the colour of the rice

The terraces of Desa Jatiluwih, in Penebel, Tabanan, follow the contours of the land, with Gunung Batukaru rising behind them. The area is part of Bali's subak cultural landscape, inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012, and in 2024 UN Tourism named it one of its Best Tourism Villages. Jatiluwih is clearly no secret. What can set your album apart is the colour of the fields, and that colour can be planned.

**Best time:** the manager publishes its own crop calendar. The fields are green ("sea of lush greenery") in April and October, the rice turns gold ("golden paddies") in June and November, July is harvest season, and the harvest is listed as completed in December. August is ploughing and planting time, and according to the manager also the month with "the highest guest visits". By that calendar, October is a green month; for a golden frame, aim for November.

**Getting there:** about 40 km from Denpasar, or a 1.5-2 hour drive. From Tabanan city it is 26 km north, about 45 minutes. It sits 700 m above sea level. The manager asks for activities to be booked at least 2 days in advance.

**Fees and rules (as of October 2026):** entrance tickets are IDR 75,000 for a foreign adult and IDR 50,000 for a foreign child; IDR 25,000 for a domestic adult and IDR 15,000 for a domestic child, in effect since 1 April 2025. The Jatiluwih management body's site lists no prewedding fee, so ask the manager directly. Drones may fly only with prior authorisation, at an extra charge of IDR 300,000. Every visitor needs a ticket, no vehicles are allowed on the trekking routes, and you may walk only on the paths provided, without picking rice, fruit or plants. Sacred areas and temples, including Pura Luhur Sri Rambut Sedana and Pura Luhur Bhujangga Waisnawa Gunung Sari, may not be entered without a guide.

In December 2025, farmers covered fields with metal sheets and black plastic to protest the sealing of businesses by the Bali legislature's TRAP special committee, and the regent promised a land-tax (PBB) exemption. We could not confirm that all the covers have been removed, so ask the manager before you book. If you are also looking for a wedding venue with a similar feel, see these [rustic Bali venues with wood details and rice-field views](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/mau-nikah-rustic-di-bali-ini-5-venue-dengan-nuansa-kayu-view-sawah-yang-super-aesthetic).

## 2. Kebun Raya Bali (Eka Karya): forest and glasshouses in the highlands

The Bali Botanic Garden, long known as Kebun Raya "Eka Karya" Bali, lies in Desa Candikuning, Baturiti, Tabanan. It covers 157.5 ha. BRIN handles conservation, while educational tourism is run by PT Mitra Natura Raya. The air is cool, and its forest and glasshouse backdrops are something you will not find on the coast. The official visitor guide for July-September 2026 lists several "Interesting Spot" entries, including the Giant Ficus Tree, the Kumbakarna Laga statue, the Cactus Greenhouse and Begonia Greenhouse, the Cyathea (tree fern) garden, the orchid and rhododendron gardens, and Lake View. According to DJKN, the garden is already used for prewedding shoots and music videos.

**Fees and permits:** the official site offers a "Wedding Package" and venue rental, but we could not read the prices. We could not confirm a prewedding fee; contact the garden through [kebunraya.id/r/bali](https://kebunraya.id/r/bali).

**Nearby:** Pura Ulun Danu Beratan is in the same village. This Kahyangan Jagat temple, pictured on the Rp50,000 note, is already heavily photographed, so we did not give it a section of its own. Its official site shows a "PREWED AT BERATAN" tile, but no price. If you stop by, remember that a kamen is required there.

## 3. Danau Tamblingan: a quiet lake in a sacred forest

In Desa Munduk, Banjar, Buleleng, about 1,000 m above sea level, Lake Tamblingan is ringed by the Alas Mertajati forest. On its shore stand Pura Ulun Danu Tamblingan and Pura Dalem Tamblingan, and on the water rest traditional pedau (or pedahu) boats, some of them now made of fibreglass. It is quiet and has no beach clubs, the opposite of south Bali.

The lake and forest are sacred to the four villages of Adat Dalem Tamblingan, known as the Catur Desa: Munduk, Gobleg, Gesing and Umajero. The area lies within the Danau Buyan-Danau Tamblingan nature tourism park (TWA), 1,847.38 ha, one of five conservation areas run by BKSDA Bali, and the head of BKSDA has said the area is, in principle, not for mass tourism. We found no ban on prewedding shoots here (checked again on 7 October 2026). Even so, shoot only with permission from BKSDA and the temple custodians (pengempon), and keep away from the temple courts.

**Fees and permits:** fees in conservation areas are national non-tax revenue (PNBP) under PP 36/2024, signed and promulgated on 30 September 2024 and in force 30 days later. An Antara report (21 February 2026) that interviewed the head of BKSDA Bali, Ratna Hendratmoko, lists the rate as "Video dan foto prewedding sebesar Rp1 juta" (Rp1 million for prewedding video and photos). That figure is the reporter's own text, not a direct quote, and we could not read the regulation's tariff annex. No rate for foreign couples was given either. Confirm the package terms and the rate for foreign couples with BKSDA Bali. You will also need a Simaksi (an entry permit for a conservation area), which BKSDA Bali is moving online and to its field posts.

## 4. Sekumpul Waterfall: a curtain of water in one valley

Sekumpul means a group or cluster, and that is exactly what you see: several waterfalls dropping side by side in a green gorge with sheer walls. The scale is what makes photos here different, because you stand in front of a curtain of water rather than a single drop. With luck, a rainbow appears in the spray.

It lies in Desa Sekumpul, Sawan, Buleleng. Sekumpul and the hidden fall are run by the Unit Pengelola BUMDes Sekar Bank Sekumpul, while neighbouring Air Terjun Fiji is run by Desa Lemukih.

**Getting there:** you walk down hundreds of steps, and then you have to climb back up. Wear safe footwear and carry your shoot outfit in a bag.

**Watch out for unofficial ticket posts:** in November 2023, the Buleleng tourism office permanently closed four illegal ticket posts in the Desa Lemukih area after a tourist complained of being asked for Rp300,000. Buy tickets only at the official post.

**Fees:** sources disagree on the entrance price, so we do not list it. We also found no prewedding fee; ask the BUMDes manager.

## 5. Melasti Beach: limestone walls along the road

The road down to Melasti Beach, in Desa Ungasan, South Kuta, Badung, was cut through a limestone hill. The result is a pair of tall, carved white cliff walls framing white sand and clear water below. You get a cliff-and-road composition typical of the Bukit without any hiking. The beach is run by BUPDA Desa Adat Ungasan through its Unit Usaha DTW Pantai Melasti, and has been open since August 2018.

**Fees:** reported ticket prices vary and there is no official page, so check at the ticket booth. The only prewedding rate we could read dates from 2022, too old to rely on. Ask the manager for the current rate.

**Drones:** Melasti is on the Bukit, not far from Ngurah Rai Airport. Make sure your photographer checks whether the flight spot falls inside the airport's KKOP, which needs approval from the Director General of Civil Aviation.

## 6. Palasari, Jembrana: a dam and a Catholic church with a Balinese spirit

In Desa Ekasari, Melaya, Jembrana, two backdrops can share one session. Bendungan Palasari is a reservoir in West Bali, far from the crowds of the south. In the same village stands the Gereja Hati Kudus Yesus Palasari (Sacred Heart of Jesus Church), a Catholic parish church whose community uses Balinese elements such as the language, dress, penjor and gebogan. For Catholic couples, this is the only location on our list that brings a church together with Balinese tradition.

**Permits:** this is an active parish church. Our advice is to ask the parish for permission first; this is our suggestion, not a written rule we found. We could not confirm a prewedding fee or the drive time from Denpasar.

## 7. Campuhan Ridge, Ubud: a path along a hilltop

The Campuhan Ridge Walk starts near Pura Gunung Lebah, Ubud, Gianyar, with the entrance through the gate of the Ibah hotel. Its paved path runs for about 2 km along a ridge between two river valleys, with open green slopes on either side. For photos, the path gives you a long leading line through the grass, and it is all just minutes from central Ubud.

**Best time:** morning or late afternoon. In the morning the path is used by joggers, so be patient if you want an empty frame.

**Fees and etiquette:** entry is free. We could not confirm a prewedding fee or rules. The trailhead passes a temple, so the temple etiquette above applies.

## 8. Desa Penglipuran: step into the bamboo forest

The main street of Desa Penglipuran, with its row of uniform traditional compounds, already appears in many albums. For a different frame, walk north of the village into the bamboo forest of about 45 ha. There is a footpath, light breaks through the bamboo leaves, and it is much quieter than the village centre. Mongabay once saw tourists scouting the light in this forest for "pre-wedding" photos.

Desa Adat Penglipuran is in Kelurahan Kubu, Bangli district, Bangli Regency, about 45 km from Denpasar. Do not confuse it with Kubu district in Karangasem. The village is run by the Pengelola Desa Wisata Penglipuran, headed by I Wayan Sumiarsa.

**Best time:** our advice is to come on a weekday morning, before the village gets busy.

**Fees (as of October 2026):** the Paket Pelipur Lara 1 covers the main street, the houses and the bamboo forest. It costs Rp25,000 for an Indonesian adult and Rp15,000 for a child, and Rp50,000 for a foreign adult and Rp30,000 for a child, with free safety insurance included. We could not confirm a prewedding fee; ask the manager.

**Rules:** drones are banned in Penglipuran. Motor vehicles may not enter the village, and dress must be modest. In temple courts, SE 7/2025 applies.

## 9. Tukad Cepung Waterfall: shafts of light inside a rock cleft

Tukad Cepung falls inside a narrow, cave-like cleft that is open at the top, and shafts of sunlight come down through the gap. The enclosing rock walls and the light falling from above make it look like nothing else on our list.

The waterfall is in Banjar Penida Kelod, Desa Tembuku, Tembuku, Bangli, about 53 km or 75 minutes from Ngurah Rai Airport. From the car park, you walk down stairs for about 15-20 minutes.

**Etiquette:** local people have long held this waterfall sacred and use it for melukat (ritual cleansing). Follow temple etiquette and choose modest clothing.

**Best time:** the sources we read disagree on both the opening hours and the best time for the light beams. Ask the site manager before you build your schedule.

**Fees:** media reports in 2024 gave a ticket price of Rp30,000 per person, while in 2023 it was still described as donation only, and there is no official page we could check. Confirm with the site manager, and ask about the prewedding fee at the same time.

## 10. Bukit Desa Pinggan, Kintamani: sunrise above the fog

In Desa Pinggan, Kintamani, Bangli, a hill looks out over valleys and villages filled with fog at sunrise, the view often called "negeri di atas awan" (a land above the clouds). You get that mood without climbing Gunung Batur.

**Best time:** around 5-6am WITA. That means your make-up team needs to start well before dawn.

**Fees:** Bangli Regency collects a Kintamani area levy at posts around Penelokan and the Batur caldera. According to detikBali (24 March 2026) and Antara (19 January 2026), both quoting the Bangli tourism office, the adult rates are Rp50,000 for foreign visitors, Rp25,000 for Indonesian visitors and Rp10,000 for holders of a Bali ID card (KTP). We could not confirm whether the trip to Pinggan passes one of those posts. We also could not confirm a prewedding fee; ask the village.

## 11. Taman Soekasada Ujung: a water palace with the sea behind

Taman Soekasada Ujung, usually shortened to Taman Ujung, was built from 1909 by the Raja of Karangasem with a Dutch architect and a Chinese architect, so European and Balinese styles mix here. There are large pools, bridges, and a roofless pavilion of tall pillars on the hill, looking out to the sea at Pantai Ujung. detikTravel names the pools, the bridge and the pillared roofless building as the usual prewedding spots. The result is a palace look with the sea in the background.

It lies in Banjar Ujung, Desa Tumbu, Karangasem district. Under Karangasem Regent Regulation (Perbup) 38/2017, the garden is a joint asset of Karangasem Regency and Puri Agung Karangasem, run by the Badan Pengelola Taman Soekasada Ujung.

**Best time:** open daily 7am-7pm WITA. Morning or late afternoon is more comfortable, as it is not too hot.

**Getting there:** about 2 hours, or 64-70 km, from Denpasar.

**Fees (as of October 2026):** since 1 January 2024, ticket prices follow Karangasem Regency's Perda 8/2023: Rp35,000 for a domestic adult and Rp25,000 for a child (aged 5-12); Rp100,000 for a foreign adult and Rp75,000 for a child. For a location permit for photography, Kemenpar (the tourism ministry) writes that the fee "berkisar mulai dari Rp1.000.000" (starts from around Rp1,000,000), so work with a figure from Rp1 million for domestic couples. We could not confirm whether that fee changed with the 2024 ticket rise, or what foreign couples pay today. Ask the manager before you book.

## 12. Taman Tirta Gangga: spring-fed pools and a water tower

Tirta Gangga is a royal water garden with spring-fed pools, statues and large koi. Its icon is a tiered water tower about 10 m tall. Here, water and carved statues can share a single frame.

It is on Jalan Raya Abang, Desa Ababi, Abang, Karangasem, and Anak Agung Made Kosalya heads its management body. Since both are in Karangasem, Tirta Gangga can be paired with Taman Ujung on the same day.

**Fees (as of October 2026):** since 1 March 2025, foreign tickets are Rp90,000 for an adult, Rp70,000 for visitors aged 65 and over, and Rp45,000 for a child (aged 5-12). Domestic tickets are Rp45,000 for an adult, Rp35,000 for an adult with a Bali ID card (KTP), and Rp25,000 for a child. The pool and museum are included. The only prewedding rate we found dates from 2023, before two price rises, so we do not list it; ask the manager.

## 13. Savana Tianyar: grassland at the foot of Gunung Agung

In Desa Tianyar, Kubu, Karangasem, open grassland stretches out with Gunung Agung behind it. In the dry season the grass turns yellow-brown, until it feels like Africa, and in the rainy season it turns green. Local horses graze there. It is the only savanna on our list. detikTravel saw prewedding sessions here in October 2021, and IDN Times (2024) recommends it for outdoor prewedding shoots.

**Best time:** the dry season, if you want the yellow look. BMKG forecasts the 2026/2027 rainy season to begin in November-December 2026, so this year's window is closing.

**Getting there:** according to the official Karangasem tourism portal, about 2.5 hours or 77 km from Denpasar, and about 40 km from Amlapura.

**Fees and permits:** not confirmed. Ask Desa Tianyar before you go.

## 14. Kertha Gosa, Klungkung: a royal hall with Kamasan ceilings

The Kertha Gosa complex at Jl. Kenanga No. 11, Kelurahan Semarapura Kelod, Klungkung district, gives you the Bale Kerta Gosa, the Bale Kambang ringed by the Taman Gili moat, and the palace gate. Its ceilings are painted in the traditional Kamasan wayang style. The complex still belongs to Puri Klungkung, is run by the Klungkung tourism office (Dinas Pariwisata), and is part of the "Semarapura City Tour" package. It is already a popular prewedding spot; the draw is the pavilion in its moat and the painted ceilings.

**Fees (as of October 2026):** Perda Kab. Klungkung No. 4 of 2026 lists "kertha gosa" as a levy object. Diskominfo Klungkung, RRI and Bali Post (August 2026) reported a rate for "sewa lokasi (pembuatan) foto prewedding/komersial" (location rental for prewedding or commercial photos) of Rp300,000 for domestic and Rp500,000 for foreign couples per package. The regulation's tariff annex is not in the published PDF, so we could not confirm which rate line applies to Kertha Gosa. Confirm with Dinas Pariwisata Klungkung before you book. For film or video, the reported rates are Rp250,000 (educational), Rp1,000,000 (social) and Rp2,000,000 (commercial). Entrance for a foreign adult is Rp50,000, while reports of the domestic ticket vary, so check at the booth.

## 15. Kelingking Beach, Nusa Penida: stay on the clifftop

The "T-Rex" headland above a white-sand cove, seen from the clifftop, is already a widely known frame. For a prewedding shoot, stay at the top. The path down to the beach is very steep, 70-80 degrees, with only basic handholds, and it is no place for a long gown.

There is one more thing to check in every frame. A glass-lift structure of about 180 m still stands on this cliff. Governor Koster ordered it dismantled on 23 November 2025. On 3 September 2026, the Denpasar Administrative Court (PTUN) granted the investor's suit (case 17/G/2026/PTUN.DPS) against Satpol PP Bali's letter of 27 November 2025, and the provincial government has said it will appeal. In the meantime, the structure stays in status quo. Ask your photographer to check every frame and choose angles that keep the structure out.

**Getting there:** take a boat from Sanur, about 45 minutes to 1 hour, then drive about 45-50 minutes from the port. The beach is in Desa Bunga Mekar, Nusa Penida, Klungkung.

**Fees (as of October 2026):** Perda Kab. Klungkung No. 4 of 2026 was adopted and promulgated on 29 July 2026, has been in force since 1 August 2026, and its tourism levies started on 1 September 2026. According to Diskominfo Klungkung (18 August 2026), also reported by RRI and Bali Post, the rate for "sewa lokasi foto prewedding komersial" (location rental for a commercial prewedding shoot) is Rp300,000 for domestic and Rp500,000 for foreign couples, per package, and a commercial film or video clip is Rp2,000,000 per package. The regulation lists Kelingking as one of 17 levy objects in Nusa Penida, alongside Angel's Billabong-Broken Beach, Diamond and Atuh. Its tariff annex is not in the published PDF, so we could not confirm which rate applies where; confirm with Dinas Pariwisata Klungkung. Also budget for the Nusa Penida levies from the general rules: foreign visitors pay the area levy plus a per-attraction levy, while domestic visitors pay the per-attraction levy.

## Checklist before you book

- If one of you is not an Indonesian citizen, pay the Rp150,000 levy through Love Bali before you travel. Golden visa and "other visa" holders apply for the exemption at least 5 days before arrival.
- For locations without an official fee, ask the site manager to state the shoot fee in writing before the day, including the rate for foreign couples.
- For Danau Tamblingan, arrange the Simaksi and permission from BKSDA Bali and the temple custodians.
- For Kertha Gosa and Kelingking, confirm the fee with Dinas Pariwisata Klungkung, and budget for the Nusa Penida levies.
- Ask about the ceremony calendar of temples near each location, because a pujawali can close a site to tourists.
- Bring a kamen or sarong for locations near temples, and choose outfits that stay modest under SE 7/2025.
- Plan without a drone unless your photographer already holds permits under PM 37/2020 and the site manager's consent. In Penglipuran, drones are banned.
- Keep 8 March 2027 (Nyepi) clear of your schedule, and check for the airport-closure announcement closer to the date.
- For golden rice at Jatiluwih or yellow grass at Tianyar, match your date to the season.
- At Sekumpul, buy tickets only at the official post.
