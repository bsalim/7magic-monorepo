Many couples from Jakarta, Surabaya or Medan picture their church wedding in a glass chapel facing the sea or on a clifftop in Uluwatu. For some couples that is possible. For others, Catholic couples in particular, the rules are different from what the wedding package brochure says.

Three things shape your plan: whether you are Catholic or Protestant, whether the ceremony is in a church or a venue chapel, and how the marriage is registered with the state. This article covers all three. We have written separately about the usual process in your home church, for [Catholic weddings](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/prosesi-pemberkatan-pernikahan-katolik) and [Protestant weddings](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/tahapan-pemberkatan-pernikahan-kristen-protestan). Here the focus is on what changes when the ceremony is in Bali.

## In short: three different routes

| | Catholic | Protestant | Symbolic blessing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | A parish church or chapel in Bali | A church, or a venue chapel if your church allows it | Anywhere |
| Who leads it | A priest, with the Bishop of Denpasar's permission | A minister from your church or a church in Bali | A celebrant or MC |
| Can be registered with the state | Yes | Yes, with a marriage certificate from the church | No |
| Preparation at home | Canonical investigation at your home parish | Pre-marriage classes or counselling at your home church | None |

## Catholic couples: the Diocese of Denpasar's rules

Bali belongs to the Diocese of Denpasar, which also covers Lombok and Sumbawa. The diocese has firmer rules than many couples expect.

In 2017 the Catholic magazine HIDUP quoted a policy letter from the Bishop of Denpasar, Mgr Silvester San, dated 26 April 2015, on the celebration of Catholic marriage. It states that a Catholic marriage may not be celebrated outside the Catholic Church's official places of worship, including hotels, villas, private homes and tourist sites. Hotel and villa chapels are among the places not allowed. The chapel of a monastery, seminary, retreat house or the diocese may be used only when the parish church or chapel is not possible, and with the parish priest's approval.

The reason, according to the priests who asked for the rule, is that weddings in hotels, villas, on beaches and on cruise ships had become a tourist package business, while a Catholic marriage is a sacrament. The same letter also does not allow marriages during Lent, Holy Week and Advent.

We found no sign that the rule has been withdrawn, and parishes in Bali continue to celebrate marriages in church. In April 2026, for example, the parish of Maria Bunda Segala Bangsa in Nusa Dua held a group wedding blessing in its church. Because rules can be updated, confirm directly with the parish you have in mind before booking anything.

**What this means for you:** if you are both Catholic and want a marriage that is valid in the Church, the ceremony takes place in a parish church in Bali and the reception at the venue of your choice. Check the distance from the church to the reception venue early, so guests can move from church to reception on the same day without rushing.

### Getting permission from outside Bali

According to the same policy letter, couples from outside the Diocese of Denpasar need the permission of the Bishop of Denpasar. The process runs roughly like this:

- **The canonical investigation takes place at your home parish**, not in Bali. This is an interview with the parish priest to confirm there is no impediment to the marriage.
- **Your home diocese sends a letter of request** to the Bishop of Denpasar, with the canonical investigation documents.
- **The officiating priest needs delegation.** If you want a priest from your home parish to marry you, he needs delegation from the Bishop of Denpasar or from the parish priest where the wedding takes place.

The paperwork can differ between dioceses, so start by asking your home parish office: "We want to marry at Parish X in Bali. What do we need to prepare?" Your home parish has most likely handled this before.

**Timing.** For a Catholic wedding in your own city, preparation ideally starts 4 to 6 months ahead, because of the marriage preparation course and the canonical investigation. For Bali, add time for the correspondence between dioceses, and secure the date at the Bali church before paying a venue deposit.

**The 2027 calendar.** Because marriages are not allowed during Lent and Advent, watch the dates. In 2027 Ash Wednesday falls on 10 February and Easter on 28 March, and the first Sunday of Advent falls on 28 November. Wedding dates between 10 February and 27 March 2027, and from 28 November 2027, are almost certainly unavailable. Nyepi, on 8 March 2027, also falls in Lent.

### What if one of you is not Catholic?

A mixed marriage, between a Catholic and a Christian of another church, or with someone who is not baptised, needs permission or a dispensation from the local ordinary. HIDUP notes that in certain cases, including marriage to someone who is not baptised, a ceremony outside the church building may be possible with permission. That is the diocese's decision, not the venue's or the wedding planner's, so ask early.

Civil registration for couples of different religions is another matter, and currently very limited. We will cover it in a separate article.

## Protestant couples: more choice, but secure the certificate

Protestant churches do not share a single rule on where a wedding can take place. Each synod and congregation sets its own: some are willing to bless a marriage outside the church building, including in a venue chapel, while others require the church building. So your first question is not for the venue but for your own church.

There are two common routes:

- **A minister from your home church comes to Bali.** This is the simplest option, because the congregation knows you and your pre-marriage classes take place there. Ask whether your church allows a wedding outside the church building and outside your city, and whether your church will issue the church marriage certificate.
- **Working with a church in Bali.** The Protestant Christian Church in Bali (GKPB), a synod founded in Bali in 1931, has married couples who come from overseas, as a 2014 study from Politeknik Negeri Bali records. Ask the GKPB congregation nearest your venue whether it also serves couples from other Indonesian cities.

**What matters most is the church marriage certificate.** The civil registry records a marriage on the basis of a marriage letter from a religious leader. If the ceremony is led by someone who cannot issue that letter on behalf of a church, your marriage cannot be registered. Before choosing a chapel package, ask the venue or wedding planner: who is the minister, which church are they from, and who issues the certificate?

## Church wedding or symbolic blessing?

Many Bali chapel packages offer a "wedding blessing". The term can mean two very different things.

**A church wedding** is led by a priest or minister, is recognised by their church, produces a church marriage certificate, and can be registered with the civil registry. This is what makes you legally husband and wife.

**A symbolic blessing** is led by a celebrant and produces no document that can be registered. It is a good choice for couples who are already legally married at home and want to celebrate in Bali, or for a vow renewal. For couples who are not yet married, a symbolic blessing alone does not make the marriage legal.

If you are Catholic, remember that the Diocese of Denpasar's rule on places applies to the celebration of marriage. If you have already married in church in Jakarta and want a symbolic ceremony in Bali, discuss its form with your parish priest.

## Civil registration in Bali

A church ceremony does not yet register your marriage with the state. Three rules apply.

**Registered in Bali, not at home.** Under Article 34 of the Population Administration Law, a valid marriage is reported to the civil registry (Disdukcapil) where the marriage takes place. If the ceremony is in Bali, it is registered with the civil registry of the regency or city where it took place, for example Badung for Kuta, Seminyak, Canggu, Uluwatu and Nusa Dua, or Gianyar for Ubud.

**Notify the civil registry before the day.** Under Article 3 of Government Regulation No. 9 of 1975, notice of the intended marriage goes to the registrar where the marriage will take place at least 10 working days beforehand. Because you live outside Bali, arrange this well in advance, through your wedding planner or directly with the registry.

**Report within 60 days.** The same Article 34 sets a limit of 60 days from the date of the marriage. After that, the process becomes more complicated.

The documents required include the religious leader's marriage letter, colour photographs and both partners' population documents. The Badung civil registry also has a 3-in-1 service that issues the marriage certificate together with the related population documents. The full list can differ between regencies, so ask the registry you will use for its current list. Our article on [marriage documents in 2026](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/syarat-dokumen-nikah-2026-kua-catatan-sipil) gives a general overview.

If one of you is a foreign national, there are additional documents from your home country. For Australian couples we have written a full guide on [how to get legally married in Bali](https://7magicwedding.com/en/legally-married-in-bali).

## Choosing a venue with this plan

Once you know your route, choosing a venue becomes easier:

- **Catholic:** look for a reception venue near the parish church, and fit the reception around the Mass time the parish gives you.
- **Protestant:** if your church allows a chapel wedding, you can consider one of [Bali's glass chapels](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/5-glass-chapel-paling-aesthetic-di-bali-buat-kamu-yang-mau-nikah-ala-pinterest). Check that the chapel allows an outside minister.
- **Symbolic blessing:** the widest choice of venue, from clifftops to beaches.

To compare areas and atmosphere, see [where to get married in Bali, from Uluwatu to Ubud](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-venue/lokasi-nikah-di-bali-uluwatu-nusa-dua-canggu-seminyak-ubud) and our [list of Bali wedding venues](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-venue/bali).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a Catholic couple marry in a hotel chapel in Bali?

No, according to the Diocese of Denpasar's policy letter as quoted by HIDUP. A Catholic marriage is celebrated in a parish church or chapel. The reception can be at any hotel or venue.

### Can a priest from Jakarta marry us in Bali?

Yes, with delegation from the Bishop of Denpasar or the parish priest where the wedding takes place, and still in a permitted church or chapel. Arrange it through your home parish.

### Is a Protestant wedding in a beach chapel valid?

It is valid in the church's eyes if it is led by a minister recognised by their church and that church issues a marriage certificate. It is valid in law once it is registered with the civil registry. Ask your church where it stands on weddings outside the church building.

### Where is our marriage certificate issued?

By the civil registry where the marriage takes place, which means in Bali, not in your home city.

### How far ahead should we start?

For Catholics, start with your home parish 6 months or more before your preferred date, because of the correspondence between dioceses. For Protestants, ask your church for its class schedule. For both, notify the civil registry at least 10 working days before the day.
