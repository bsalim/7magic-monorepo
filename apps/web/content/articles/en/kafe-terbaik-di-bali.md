The morning after a reception tends to follow the same rhythm. The couple want to slip out on their own for a while, family and friends who flew in from Jakarta or Surabaya want a brunch together before their flights home, and nearly everyone is looking for good coffee to take back as a gift. Bali has hundreds of cafes, so we have narrowed this list to the names that keep appearing on 2025 and 2026 editorial lists, then grouped them by area so you can pick the ones close to your venue or hotel.

We have deliberately left out star ratings and menu prices. The sources we used do not give them, and cafe menus in Bali change quickly.

## How "best" is this list?

Let us be honest first. The World's 100 Best Coffee Shops 2026 was announced at CoffeeFest Madrid on 16 February 2026, and Time Out's coverage does not mention a single cafe from Indonesia. Time Out's 13 November 2026 coverage of the Asia-Pacific list does not name a Bali cafe either, although that article does not print the full list. So at the time of writing, no Bali cafe is confirmed on either list.

What you can rely on is editorial consensus. The names that appear on more than one 2025 to 2026 list (Honeycombers, Time Out, Tatler and nibble.id) are Revolver, KYND, Seniman Coffee Studio, Suka Espresso, BGS, BAKED., The Avocado Factory, St. Ali, Blacklist Coffee Roasters and Melali. These are the ones we put first.

## Canggu, Pererenan and Berawa

This is the densest cafe area, and many wedding villas are close by. **BAKED.** (Berawa, Pererenan, Batu Bolong, and also Uluwatu) is known for sourdough, croissants and eggs benedict with gochujang hollandaise. **The Avocado Factory** in Batu Mejan serves avocado-everything breakfasts and avocado lattes, and houses The Goat Father coffee kiosk. Both are on the Honeycombers and Tatler lists.

For more serious coffee, **St. Ali** in Pererenan brings Melbourne-style specialty coffee, while **Blacklist Coffee Roasters** has two Canggu locations and runs tasting menus and workshops. **BGS Bali**, a surf shop that doubles as a coffee bar, is known for its almond-milk latte.

If your group includes children, **Milk & Madu** on Beach Road has a kids' menu and a playground. **Openhouse** in Pererenan and Seseh serves build-your-own plates, and the Seseh branch is quieter. **Copenhagen** is known for its Scandinavian brunch boards, but the queues are long at busy times. nibble.id (2 June 2026) adds **Project Coffee Canggu** on Jl. Nelayan for a dirty latte made with single-origin Indonesian beans.

## Seminyak and Kerobokan

**Revolver** is the most consistent name: it appears on both 2026 Honeycombers guides and on Time Out, with specialty coffee from Indonesian beans and Aussie-style brunch. Little Steps Asia notes that Revolver has been running since 2012. It has two Seminyak branches, plus Canggu and the airport.

**KYND Community**, with its all-pink interior, is the couple photo spot shared most often on Instagram, and its whole menu is plant-based. **Pison** in Petitenget uses Sumatran beans roasted in house, but Honeycombers and nibble.id both warn about the queues. **Livingstone Café & Bakery** is roomy enough for a group.

From nibble.id's dirty latte list there is **Little Langkah Coffee** in Seminyak, which also has a branch in Cilandak, Jakarta, and **Calmaa Social Space** in Kerobokan Kaja.

## Ubud

**Seniman Coffee Studio** is a micro-roastery with a manual brew bar and workshops, and you can buy its beans as gifts. **Melali** on Jl. Sri Wedari, with rice-field views and Mediterranean-style breakfasts, is on the Honeycombers 2026 list and on nibble.id's list of Instagrammable Ubud cafes (31 July 2025).

That nibble.id list also includes **Taman Dedari** on the Ayung River with about 50 angel statues, **Yellow Flower Cafe** with views of Mount Agung and the rice fields, the fully plant-based **Zest Ubud** above the Campuhan valley, and **Wedja Bali** with its lotus pond and Balinese duck. For beans, **Ubud Coffee Roastery** roasts Kintamani coffee on site.

One important point: since 8 September 2026 central Ubud has run Phase III of its one-way scheme, and tour buses are barred from the centre and sent to Dalem Puri. The first day brought more than four hours of gridlock. If your group travels by bus, plan to get off at Dalem Puri and walk or switch to small cars. Our full guide to the area is in our article on [Ubud and Kintamani for newlyweds and guests](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tips-hubungan/ubud-kintamani-untuk-pengantin-baru-dan-tamu-pernikahan).

## Uluwatu, Bingin and Jimbaran

**Suka Espresso** in Pecatu (also in Ubud) is on both 2026 Honeycombers guides, with specialty coffee and hearty breakfasts at friendly prices. It is often full. In Bingin there is **Analog Uluwatu**, a retro surfer cafe, and **Alchemy Uluwatu**, the second outpost of Alchemy Ubud, with a raw and vegan menu. KYND, BAKED. and BGS also have Uluwatu branches now, and St. Ali has opened a new branch in Jimbaran.

Cafes on the Bukit work best in the morning. The Sterling Shortcut has been closed since February 2026, and since 2 June 2026 a turn-restriction trial has run daily from 2pm to 10pm at six Pecatu intersections. Officials warn that peak-hour trips can take three hours or more. Brunch in Uluwatu and heading back before 2pm makes far more sense for guests staying in Seminyak. More ideas for the south are in our article on [things to do with guests after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/aktivitas-bersama-tamu-setelah-menikah-di-bali-kuta-uluwatu-canggu).

## Sanur

Sanur suits couples who want breakfast with the sunrise. **Huma Beach by Goldmine** on Bangsal Beach serves early coffee and an ocean-view breakfast, while **Soul on the Beach** on Sindhu Beach is family- and pet-friendly. Honeycombers calls **7AM Bakers** on Jl. Segara Ayu a new arrival, known for French pastries. **Sala Bistro** serves Aussie brunch with Seniman coffee, and **Simply Brew Coffee Roasters** sells bags of Bali-roasted beans.

## Kintamani

Kintamani is the best choice if your group is mixed: the keen hikers can do the Mount Batur sunrise trek, and everyone else waits at the caldera cafes around Jl. Raya Penelokan. **AKASA Specialty Coffee** uses local beans and looks out over Batur. **Pahdi Specialty Coffee** has four glass-walled floors and a lift, so it is comfortable for older guests or anyone with a stroller. **Paperhills** is pool-club style, and its front-row sofas carry a minimum spend. **Kintamani Eco Bike Coffee** is a farm-to-cup roastery.

According to Bali Holiday Secrets (9 April 2026), the best hours are 9am to 11:30am, after the sunrise crowds leave and before clouds build after 1pm. Dawn at about 1,500 m can be 14 to 16°C, so bring a jacket. The same guide notes that **Himalila Café is under renovation**, and that there is a Geopark area entry fee of IDR 50,000 per adult, with no local rate stated. This fee is separate from the IDR 150,000 levy that foreign visitors pay through the Love Bali portal, which Indonesian citizens do not pay.

nibble.id has a list of 10 Kintamani cafes, but it was published on 6 January 2022, so some of its details are old. Two of its picks, Kava and Batur 1926, no longer appear on the April 2026 list. We took one tip from it that still holds: try *mujair nyat-nyat*, tilapia from Lake Batur.

## Tabanan and Nuanu

In Tabanan, we have not found a named cafe inside Nuanu Creative City on any editorial list. Nuanu's first phase officially opened on 30 August 2025, and there is a "Nusantara" area with regional food and Indonesian coffee traditions, but we could not find its opening date. If you are staying there, St. Ali and BAKED. in Pererenan are the closest picks on this list. A full day plan for Nuanu is in our article on [Nuanu and Tabanan after a Bali wedding](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/wedding-preparation/wisata-nuanu-tabanan-setelah-menikah-di-bali).

## Choosing by need

| Need | Picks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brunch for groups and families | Milk & Madu, Livingstone, Openhouse Seseh | Roomy or child-friendly. Copenhagen, Suka and Pison often have queues, which makes them a poor fit for big groups. |
| Photos as a couple | KYND, Melali, Taman Dedari, Yellow Flower, Kintamani cafes | In Kintamani, the view is clearest from 9am to 11:30am. |
| Coffee to take home | Seniman, Ubud Coffee Roastery, Simply Brew | All three sell or roast their own beans. |
| Guests who do not eat pork | KYND, Zest, Alchemy, Give Cafe | Plant-based menus, but not halal-certified. |
| Older guests | Pahdi Specialty Coffee | Has a lift and is wheelchair-friendly. |

## What has changed since last year

Shelter Cafe in Seminyak is now called **Hut Bali by Shelter**, serving Middle Eastern and Mediterranean food in a two-storey space full of plants. KYND has opened in Canggu and Uluwatu, Artisan has added a branch in Pererenan, St. Ali has opened in Jimbaran, and 7AM Bakers has arrived in Sanur.

Habitat Cafe in Ubud is listed as "temporarily closed" on TripAdvisor. Crate Cafe, Sisterfields and Clear Café did not appear on the 2025 to 2026 editorial lists we read. That does not mean they have closed, so if you want to go, check their Google Maps listing or Instagram first.

## A note for Muslim guests

We have not found a single cafe on this list that is confirmed as BPJPH halal-certified. Cafes with fully plant-based menus, such as KYND, Zest, Alchemy and Give Cafe, use no pork or lard, which makes them a safer choice for guests who avoid pork. That is still not halal certification. If some guests eat only at certified places, check the cafe on BPJPH's SiHalal or ask the cafe directly before you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is any Bali cafe on the World's 100 Best Coffee Shops 2026 list?

Not in the coverage we read. Coverage of the 2026 global and Asia-Pacific lists names no cafe from Indonesia, although the full Asia-Pacific list was not printed in the article we used.

### Which cafe is best for brunch after the reception?

Choose one close to your guests' hotels. For a large group or families with children, Milk & Madu in Canggu and Livingstone in Petitenget have more room. Save the cafes known for queues for a visit as a couple.

### Are vegan cafes in Bali halal?

Plant-based cafes do not use pork, but we found no halal certification for any cafe on this list. Check SiHalal or ask directly.

### When is the best time to visit a Kintamani cafe?

From 9am to 11:30am, according to Bali Holiday Secrets (April 2026). After 1pm, clouds often cover the view of Batur.
