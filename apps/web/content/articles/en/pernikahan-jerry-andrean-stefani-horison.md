"Saya berjanji setia kepadanya, dalam untung dan malang, di waktu sehat maupun sakit dan saya mau mencintai dan menghormatinya seumur hidup saya." ("I promise to be faithful to him, in good times and in bad, in sickness and in health, and I will love and honour him all the days of my life.") Stefani Horison posted those words of the marriage vow on Instagram around midday on Sunday, 4 October 2026, above a portrait of herself with Jerry Andrean. In the photo Fani, as she is usually called, wears a high-neck, long-sleeved white lace gown, and Jerry wears a black tuxedo with a bow tie.

The day before, Saturday, 3 October 2026, the two received a Catholic wedding blessing in the chapel of Taman Doa Our Lady of Akita, PIK 2, Tangerang, Banten. The chapel is built of wood, in a Japanese architectural style.

MasterChef Indonesia viewers know them as two champions from different seasons. Netizens have even nicknamed the couple "Bapak dan Ibu Negara Kuliner", roughly the First Couple of Indonesian cooking. The news of the wedding reached the public through Fani's posts that Sunday, and that morning she kept it short: "One chapter ends, another begins. / This time, together ❤️".

## What we know so far

- **Saturday, 3 October 2026:** a Catholic wedding blessing, followed by a reception on the same day.
- **Place of the blessing:** the chapel of Taman Doa Our Lady of Akita, PIK 2, Tangerang, Banten. The press calls it Gereja Katolik Taman Doa Our Lady of Akita.
- **Announcement:** two Instagram posts by Fani (@fanimci5) on Sunday, 4 October 2026, at 09:38 and 14:16 WIB.
- **Fani's look:** a white lace gown with sequin detail, long sleeves, a full skirt and a long train, worn with a long veil and a bouquet of white calla lilies.
- **Jerry's look:** a black tuxedo, white shirt, bow tie and boutonnière. The suit was made by Wong Hang Tailor.
- **Photo and video:** Kairos Works, with photographer Davy Reinaldo.
- **Wedding planner and organiser:** Riviera Event Organizer.

## Who Jerry Andrean and Stefani Horison are

Stefani Horison was born in Sampit, Central Kalimantan, on 11 November 1996, which made her 29 on her wedding day. She won season five of MasterChef Indonesia in 2019. Sampit is her hometown, and the city would later host the family lamaran.

Jerry Andrean won season seven. In the top-two grand final, which aired on Sunday, 27 December 2020, he beat Audrey. Today he owns the Rempah Bakar restaurant, which opened a branch in Tangerang Kota on Tuesday, 30 September 2025. Both of their Instagram handles still carry the season each of them won: @fanimci5 and @jerrymci7.

They met in 2021, when both were invited as guest judges on season eight of MasterChef Indonesia. Their relationship began in 2024 and became public at the end of that year, when Jerry threw Fani a birthday surprise. By the wedding day, they had been together for about two years.

## From Disneyland Shanghai to Sampit

Jerry proposed to Fani at night on Sunday, 14 December 2025, at Disneyland Shanghai. He knelt with a ring under the fireworks. Fani confirmed the date herself, because the date printed on her photo turned out to be wrong: "Btw itu potonya salah edit ya harusnya 14/12/2025 HAHAHAHA" ("Btw the photo was edited wrong, it should be 14/12/2025 HAHAHAHA").

Five days later, on Friday, 19 December 2025, Jerry posted a video of the proposal. In the caption he wrote that Disney had always been the dream and favourite place of his "Ibu Negara", his First Lady, and that he was grateful to make her small dream come true. Fani wrote in her own post: "Engaged to this man @jerrymci7. Terima kasih untuk semua perjuangan, pengertian, dan segala usaha yang kamu kerahkan untukku dan untuk kita." ("Thank you for all the effort, understanding and everything you have given for me and for us.")

The theme-park proposal was followed by a family one. On Saturday, 21 March 2026, Jerry and his family travelled to Sampit to ask for Fani's hand in front of both families. The sequence is common in Indonesia: a moment for the two of you first, then a formal meeting between the families in the bride's home town. If your partner's family lives in another city, set aside a travel budget for the family party early on.

## Four months before the big day

The pre-wedding photos came first. On Thursday, 28 May 2026, Jerry posted a video captioned "POV : Vibing on prewed Day ❤️". According to Jawa Pos, the concepts for their pre-wedding shoot ranged from classic to nature-themed.

About two weeks later, on Friday, 12 June 2026, Fani posted a video captioned "4 BULAN LAGIIIII MENIKAH 😌🙏🏻" ("4 MONTHS TO GOOOO till the wedding"). She had taken "si Bapak", her man, to the Bridestory Fair at JICC Senayan, Halls A and B, which ran from 12 to 14 June 2026, to sort out the "perintilan", the small bits and pieces, still left to do.

This is the part most relevant to you if you are planning. Four months out, the main vendors are usually locked in, and what remains are the small tasks that eat up time. A wedding fair lets you compare many suppliers in a single day. Go with a clear list, for example souvenirs, invitations or accessories, so you do not come home with brochures from vendors you have already booked.

## A blessing in a Japanese-style wooden chapel

"Taman Doa" means prayer garden. Taman Doa Our Lady of Akita is a Marian pilgrimage garden in PIK 2, built by Agung Sedayu Group. Cardinal Suharyo blessed it on Saturday, 4 May 2024. Its chapel is wooden and Japanese in style, modelled on the Seitai Hoshikai church in Akita, Japan.

The choice suits couples who want a Catholic blessing with a different feel from a parish church. The architecture already works as decoration in the photos, so the need for extra styling can be kept down. If you want a comparison with a large church in the city centre, read the story of [Brisia Jodie and Jonathan Alden's wedding at Jakarta Cathedral](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-brisia-jodie-jonathan-alden-katedral-jakarta).

The reception was held on the same day, after the blessing. Its location was not named in the couple's posts or in press coverage.

## Lace, sequins and a tailored suit

Fani's gown was white lace with sequin detail, high-necked and long-sleeved. The skirt was full with a long train, finished with a long veil. Her hair was in a neat updo, and she carried a bouquet of white calla lilies. Fani did not name the designer of her gown.

Jerry wore a black tuxedo, white shirt, bow tie and boutonnière. For the suit, Fani named the tailor in her first post: "Grooms Suit by @wonghangtailor". Kairos Works also tagged Wong Hang Tailor in its own post.

A made-to-measure suit gives the groom a cut that fits at the shoulders and sleeves. If you go this route, allow time for several fittings, and agree on a finish date with the tailor well before your photo session.

## Vendors named by the couple themselves

In her first post, Fani credited the vendors openly. Photo and video were by Kairos Works, with Davy Reinaldo as photographer, while planning and organising were handled by Riviera Event Organizer. Riviera posted a video of the wedding on Wednesday, 7 October 2026, and Kairos Works followed on Saturday, 10 October 2026.

A list like this is useful for your own research. The captions of newly married couples are often the most honest vendor guide, because the names they credit are usually the people who actually worked on the day. When you contact a photographer, ask for the full-day coverage of one wedding, not just a set of best shots.

## Ideas you can borrow

- **Choose a chapel with character.** A chapel in a prayer garden or retreat house can feel quite different from a large church. Ask the managers about capacity, blessing schedules and photography rules, then get your paperwork started at your parish early.
- **Plan a visit to a wedding fair about four months before the day.** Bring a list of the loose ends still open, and take your partner along so decisions can be made on the spot.
- **Have the groom's suit made by a tailor.** Leave time for fittings, and make sure the suit is ready before the photo session.
- **Separate the couple's proposal from the family lamaran.** The romantic moment can happen anywhere, while the formal lamaran stays in the bride's family home town.
- **Write a short vow caption.** One sentence from the marriage vow is enough to stand for the day.
- **Credit your vendors in your post.** It helps other couples and is a thank-you to the team that worked for you.
