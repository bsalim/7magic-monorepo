As soon as two Chinese families start talking about an engagement, someone will ask, "What is their sign?" For some parents the answer is just conversation. For others it shapes the date of the sangjit, the betrothal gift ceremony, and of the wedding itself.

This article is a quick reference for all twelve signs of the Chinese zodiac, known in Indonesia as shio: their birth years, the sign each is considered most compatible with, the sign each is considered to clash with, and what that means for couples marrying in the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat. The signs repeat every twelve years, and the element follows the last digit of the birth year: 0 and 1 are Metal, 2 and 3 Water, 4 and 5 Wood, 6 and 7 Fire, 8 and 9 Earth.

**Born in January or early February? Take care.** The zodiac year does not change on 1 January. The popular zodiac calendar changes at Chinese New Year, which falls between late January and mid-February. Ba Zi changes at the solar term Lichun, around 4 February. So someone born on 20 January 1996, for example, is a Pig, not a Rat. We explain the difference between the two ways of counting in our [introduction to Ba Zi](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/mengenal-ba-zi-empat-pilar-kelahiran). ## The four rules used

**Six Combinations (六合, Liu He).** Six pairs of signs considered to complement each other. This is what is usually meant by "best match".

**Three Harmonies (三合, San He).** Four groups of three signs considered to be of one mind: Monkey, Rat and Dragon; Pig, Rabbit and Goat; Tiger, Horse and Dog; and Snake, Rooster and Ox. Our calculator counts a pair only when it includes the middle sign of its group (Rat, Rabbit, Horse or Rooster), as many practitioners hold.

**Clash (冲, chong, often written ciong in Indonesia).** Six pairs of signs that sit opposite each other in the circle of twelve.

**Harm (害, hai).** Six pairs read as minor friction, lighter than a clash.

A full explanation of these rules, along with the day master and the spouse palace, is in our article on [couple compatibility in Ba Zi](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/tradisi-wedding/kecocokan-pasangan-menurut-ba-zi).

## The signs that clash with the 2027 Year of the Fire Goat

2027 is the Year of the Fire Goat (丁未), beginning at Chinese New Year on 6 February 2027. In the Tai Sui tradition, four signs are said to "fan tai sui", or be out of step with the year. The Hong Kong and Indonesian sources we read agree on the same four:

| Sign | Relation to 2027 |
|---|---|
| Goat | Its own year (ben ming nian) |
| Ox | Direct clash, plus punishment (xing) |
| Dog | Punishment (xing) and break (po) |
| Rat | Harm (hai) |

What does this mean for a wedding? There is no rule that these signs must not marry in 2027. What families who keep the tradition usually do is choose the date more carefully, for example avoiding days whose earthly branch clashes with the couple's signs, or consulting a master. Some also pray to Tai Sui at a temple early in the year.

Beyond these four signs, 2027 is also called a "widow year" because it contains no Lichun. ## If your signs clash

First, the zodiac sign is only one of the eight characters in Ba Zi. Ba Zi practitioners themselves rarely conclude anything from a single clash in the year pillar, because the spouse palace and the day master are read too. A couple whose signs clash may still have very harmonious day pillars.

Second, the wedding date can be chosen to balance things. Our calculator checks up to three candidate dates against both your signs and both your day pillars, then marks which are safe, which need consideration and which clash.

Third, the decision is still yours and your families'. We found no research showing that zodiac compatibility affects how long a marriage lasts, and research on Western star signs has found no such effect either.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which signs are most compatible for marriage?

By tradition, the Liu He pairs: Rat and Ox, Tiger and Pig, Rabbit and Dog, Dragon and Rooster, Snake and Monkey, and Horse and Goat. After those, pairs from the same Three Harmony group.

### Which signs clash with each other?

Rat and Horse, Ox and Goat, Tiger and Monkey, Rabbit and Rooster, Dragon and Dog, and Snake and Pig.

### Are two people with the same sign compatible?

Under the classical rules, two identical signs fall under none of Liu He, Three Harmony, clash or harm. The result is neutral, and the reading depends more on the other pillars.

### Which signs clash in 2027?

The Goat, Ox, Dog and Rat, with the Ox in direct clash with the Year of the Goat.

### My sign is the Rat, but I was born on 25 January 1996. Is that right?

No. Chinese New Year 1996 fell on 19 February and Lichun on 4 February, so by either way of counting you are still a Pig.
