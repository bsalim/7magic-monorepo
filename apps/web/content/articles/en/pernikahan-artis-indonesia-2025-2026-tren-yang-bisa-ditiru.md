If you've followed Indonesian celebrity wedding news over the past two years, one habit is hard to miss. Time and again, when the mahar (the Islamic dowry) is read out at the akad, the figures sound like a date. Febby Rastanty's wedding in November 2024 used this pattern, and by mid-2026 at least five more couples had done the same.

The mahar is only one pattern. Across the twelve weddings we've gathered here, several other habits recur: adat split by event, a small akad with a big reception, receptions without a pelaminan, and vendor names that turn up again and again. This article is organised by theme rather than by couple, so you can see straight away what fits your own plans. The full list of dates and venues is at the end.

## A dowry that spells the date

The pattern is simple: grams of gold for the day and month, a cash amount for the year. Febby is the exception, with the month held in the US$11.

- Febby Rastanty and Drajad Djumantara: 9 grams of logam mulia (gold bullion), US$11 and Rp2,024, for 9 November 2024.
- [Angga Yunanda and Shenina Cinnamon](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-angga-yunanda-shenina-cinnamon-bvlgari-bali): 10 grams and 2 grams of logam mulia plus US$2,025, for 10 February 2025.
- [Luna Maya and Maxime Bouttier](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-luna-maya-maxime-bouttier-bali-jakarta): 7.5 grams of logam mulia and US$2,025, for 7 May 2025.
- [Al Ghazali and Alyssa Daguise](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-al-ghazali-alyssa-daguise-st-regis-jcc): 16.6 grams of logam mulia and €2,025, for 16 June 2025.
- [El Rumi and Syifa Hadju](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-el-rumi-syifa-hadju-raffles-jakarta): 26 grams and 4 grams of logam mulia plus £2,026, for 26 April 2026.
- [Jennifer Coppen and Justin Hubner](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-jennifer-coppen-justin-hubner-bali): 18 grams of gold, made up of 12 grams and 6 grams, plus €2,026, for 12 June 2026.

Look at the variations. Luna and Al used a decimal, so a single piece of gold holds both day and month. Angga, El and Jennifer split it into two pieces. The currency changes too, between dollars, euros and pounds. For your own wedding, the amount can be scaled to what you can afford. What makes a mahar like this memorable is the number, not the size.

Ranty Maria and Rayn Wijaya took the numbers idea further. Both were born on the 26th, started dating on the 26th, got engaged on 26 April 2024 at Tokyo DisneySea, and married on Monday, 26 January 2026. If you and your partner share a number like this, marrying on a weekday for the right date can be a sensible choice.

## Different adat for different events

Couples from two adat backgrounds often face the same question: whose adat do we follow? Some answered it by splitting.

Febby Rastanty used Sundanese adat for the morning akad, from her husband's side, then Palembang adat for the evening reception, from her own family's side. Al Ghazali and Alyssa Daguise married in Sundanese adat, since Alyssa's mother is from Garut, then held a ngunduh mantu (the groom's family's reception) themed "Javanese Royal Wedding" three days later. Teuku Rassya and Cleantha Islan used Acehnese adat for the akad, complete with the Linto Baro or Meukasah and Meukeutop for the groom and a gold Acehnese crown for the bride, followed by a modern reception. Rafi Pangestu and his bride used Acehnese adat for the akad and Solo Putri style for the reception, in black velvet with paes, the Javanese bridal forehead make-up.

This avoids an argument over who gives way. Each family gets one event that feels like theirs. One thing to remember: two adat means two sets of outfits, often two bridal stylists, and two running orders. Count that in from the moment you draw up the budget.

## A small akad, a big reception

Several couples deliberately kept the akad intimate, then threw the doors open for the reception.

Al and Alyssa closed their akad day with a dinner for 100 to 200 guests dressed in black, with a sweetheart table for the couple. Three days later, the ngunduh mantu at the JCC Assembly Hall was planned for about 3,000 guests. Luna Maya and Maxime Bouttier married in Bali, then held a second reception in Jakarta almost three months later. Jaz Hayat and Wavi Zihan went the other way and kept their whole wedding intimate, in all-white Malay adat.

Splitting things like this helps keep the akad solemn. The akad is for the people who are truly close, while the duty to invite colleagues, your parents' acquaintances and business contacts can be met at the reception.

## Guest lists that swell

Luna and Maxime's reception at the Four Seasons Hotel Jakarta invited 500 people. Nearly 800 came.

That figure is a useful reminder. Invitations get passed on, guests bring partners or children, and your parents' colleagues turn up even without a direct invitation. When you work out catering and room capacity, don't go by the number of invitations alone. Talk to your caterer about a sensible number of spare portions, and pick a room that is still comfortable if guests exceed the plan.

## Receptions without a dais

The traditional pelaminan, the raised dais where the couple sits, is being dropped at some receptions. [Brisia Jodie and Jonathan Alden](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-brisia-jodie-jonathan-alden-katedral-jakarta) chose the theme "Dreamy of Aurora" at the Pondok Indah Golf Ballroom, a winter scene in blue, white and crystal, with no pelaminan. Luna and Maxime held an internationally themed reception in Bali the day after the akad, both dressed in white. [Amanda Manopo and Kenny Austin](https://7magicwedding.com/artikel/pernikahan-selebriti/pernikahan-amanda-manopo-kenny-austin-langham-jakarta) received their church blessing in the garden at The Langham, Jakarta, then moved on to a reception in the same hotel's ballroom that same day.

If this format appeals to you, talk to your parents first. To some families, a pelaminan is still a must.

## Souvenirs people actually use

Two couples chose souvenirs for everyday use. Luna and Maxime gave an engraved coffee machine, ZEN Tableware and skincare. Amanda and Kenny let each guest pick one of three items: a 32-inch Android TV, a microwave or a juicer, plus a goodie bag that included a small piece of logam mulia.

The scale is obviously far beyond most budgets. What you can copy is the principle. Choose something guests will use, and if you can, let them pick between two simple items so no souvenir ends up in a drawer.

## Bali as the destination

Four couples chose Bali. Angga and Shenina married privately at Bvlgari Resort Bali, and the news only broke on the day itself. Luna and Maxime held their akad at COMO Shambhala Estate, Payangan. Ranty and Rayn married at The Royal Santrian, Nusa Dua, and the ceremony was livestreamed on YouTube. Jennifer and Justin used two venues, Tirtha Bali in Uluwatu for the akad and Puri Bhagawan in Jimbaran for sunset vows and the reception.

A wedding away from home puts a natural cap on the guest list. Not everyone can fly, and often that's exactly the point. If you take this route, give the relatives who stay behind a way to watch, as Ranty and Rayn did.

## Vendor names that keep recurring

Following celebrity weddings for two years also reveals the same circle of vendors.

- Hian Tjen designed gowns for Shenina Cinnamon, Luna Maya, Amanda Manopo, Ranty Maria and Jennifer Coppen.
- Hilda by Bridestory handled the weddings of Febby Rastanty, Amanda Manopo and El Rumi.
- Marlene Hariman did the make-up for Febby Rastanty and Shenina Cinnamon.
- Andy Chun did Luna Maya's akad make-up and Ranty Maria's.
- Raffles Hotel Jakarta hosted Teuku Rassya's akad on 12 April 2026 and El Rumi's on 26 April 2026, just two weeks apart.

For you, this list is more useful as a way to read the market than as a shopping list. Vendors in demand are usually booked far ahead. If you have your eye on a designer, make-up artist or venue that many people want, start asking as soon as your date is fixed.

## What you can borrow

- Build the mahar from the numbers in your wedding date. The amount is up to you; the meaning still reads.
- If the two families have different adat, split it by event so each family has its part.
- Keep the akad small and solemn, then meet your social obligations at the reception.
- Plan a buffer for guests beyond the invitation count, especially in the catering.
- Choose souvenirs people use every day.
- For a wedding away from home, provide a livestream or video for relatives who can't come.
- Book in-demand vendors as soon as the date is set.
