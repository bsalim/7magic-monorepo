The message lands in the family group chat one evening, forwarded by an auntie: "Next year is a widow year. If you're getting married, do it before Chinese New Year." No source, no explanation. Your mum replies with a thumbs-up sticker. You and your partner, who have only just started looking at venues for mid-2027, look at each other.

The phrase "widow year" has indeed been doing the rounds again ahead of 2027, including in Chinese-language media. This article explains what it means, why 2027 qualifies, and how families with different views usually handle it. We won't tell you whether to believe it. That's for you and your family to decide.

## Which zodiac year is 2027?

2027 is the Year of the Fire Goat, 丁未 (ding wei) in the Chinese calendar. Chinese New Year 2578 falls on Saturday, 6 February 2027, and the Goat year runs until 25 January 2028. Anyone born or married up to 5 February 2027 is still in the Year of the Fire Horse by the lunar calendar.

In the zodiac tradition, the Goat clashes (chong) with the Ox. For some families, that means a bride or groom born in an Ox year should be more careful choosing a date this year. ## What is a widow year?

The Chinese calendar is really two calendars running side by side. One is lunar, and it sets Chinese New Year. The other is solar, and it divides the year into 24 solar terms. The first of those is **lichun** (立春), the start of spring, which almost always falls around 4 February.

Because a lunar year is shorter than a solar one, Chinese New Year moves back and forth between late January and mid-February. As a result, some lunar years contain two lichun, some contain one, and some contain none at all. A year without lichun is called 无春年 (a year without spring) or 寡年. In some regions it is also called 盲年, a blind year. The character 寡 is where the pun 寡妇年, widow year, comes from.

The belief goes roughly like this: lichun is thought to bring yang (masculine) energy and fertility, so a woman who marries in a year without it is feared to end up a widow, or to struggle to have children. The Malaysian newspaper Kwong Wah Yit Poh notes that the name "widow year" itself is a later play on words.

## Why 2027 counts

We checked the dates against the Gregorian-Lunar calendar conversion tables from the Hong Kong Observatory, the official body that publishes them every year.

| Event | Date | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Lichun 2027 | 4 February 2027 | Thursday |
| Chinese New Year, start of the Goat year | 6 February 2027 | Saturday |
| Next Chinese New Year, start of the Monkey year | 26 January 2028 | Wednesday |
| Lichun 2028 | 4 February 2028 | Friday |

Lichun 2027 arrives two days before the Goat year begins, so it still belongs to the Horse year. The next lichun arrives after the Goat year has ended. Between 6 February 2027 and 25 January 2028 there is no lichun at all.

This isn't only our arithmetic. Kwong Wah Yit Poh, in a January 2024 article on the widow year, already listed 2027, the Year of the Goat, as one of the 无春年. Two articles by Chinese metaphysics practitioners published on Sohu in October 2026 also name 2027 as a 无春年, with the same dates.

## How often does a year without lichun happen?

Far more often than people expect. Guo Hu, a Beijing meteorology official quoted by China Daily, said there are on average seven such years in every 19-year cycle. Kwong Wah puts it at roughly once every two to three years.

By our own calculation from solar and lunar positions, the lunar years without lichun around now are 2019, 2021, 2024, 2027, 2029 and 2032. The first four match Kwong Wah's list. If your older sibling or a cousin married in the 2024 Year of the Dragon, they already married in a "widow year".

## The sceptics, and their reasons

The pushback comes from two quite different directions.

**From scientists.** Guo Hu called the idea that a year without lichun is unlucky "totally groundless". The experts quoted by Kwong Wah call it a superstition with no scientific basis, simply a coincidence of the gap between the lunar and solar calendars.

**From metaphysics practitioners themselves.** This is the interesting part, because they are the very people families ask for auspicious dates. Writing on Sohu, Cai Mingdao says no classical text uses whether a year contains lichun as a measure of good or bad fortune. He also points out that in Ba Zi the new year begins exactly at lichun, not at Chinese New Year, so by that reckoning no year ever loses its spring. What decides a wedding date, in his view, is the day pillar of the wedding and the charts of the couple. Jiang Qun, another practitioner, writes that couples planning to marry in 2027 can go ahead with peace of mind.

## For those who hold to it: the usual choices

The belief is still very much alive, especially among parents and grandparents. Ahead of 2024, China Daily reported couples in China racing to marry before the widow year began, to the point that hotels and function venues in Gansu province were fully booked. Others went ahead as planned because it felt unfair to postpone over an unproven belief.

The options we found most often in those sources:

- **Marry before 6 February 2027.** It's the most direct choice, but families taking it need to start looking for a date in December 2026 or January 2027 now.
- **Postpone to the 2028 Year of the Monkey**, which begins on 26 January 2028.
- **Choose the date from the couple's Ba Zi charts**, not from the year's label. This is what Cai Mingdao recommends, and our [Couple Ba Zi Calculator](https://7magicwedding.com/wedding-tools/kalkulator-bazi-pasangan) can help you check whether a candidate date clashes with your zodiac signs.
- **Talk it through with your parents.** Jiang Qun suggests explaining where the calendar quirk comes from to worried parents, while still respecting their feelings. If the family still wants certainty, ask the family's usual master for a date.

## If you choose to marry before Chinese New Year, the window is narrow

In Jakarta, the window before Chinese New Year 2027 overlaps with another one. According to the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 AH is expected on Monday, 8 February 2027, just two days after Chinese New Year. The official date awaits the sidang isbat, the government's moon-sighting session. That means Muslim families who want to marry before the fasting month are chasing the same weekends as Chinese families who want to marry before the Goat year. The last of those weekends, 5 to 7 February, is also the Chinese New Year weekend, with a collective leave day on Friday 5 February.

Don't forget the order of events. The seventh month of the Chinese calendar, known as the Hungry Ghost Month, runs from 2 to 31 August 2027 according to the Hong Kong Observatory table. Customs vary. ## Frequently asked questions

### When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Saturday, 6 February 2027. In Indonesia, the government has also set Friday, 5 February 2027 as a collective leave day for Chinese New Year.

### Which years are widow years?

By our calculation, the nearest lunar years without lichun are 2024, 2027, 2029 and 2032. The year 2028, by contrast, contains two lichun, on 4 February 2028 and 3 February 2029.

### What zodiac sign is a baby born in January 2027?

By the lunar calendar, Horse, because the Goat year only begins on 6 February. In Ba Zi, which uses lichun, a baby born before 4 February 2027 is also a Horse, while one born after lichun on the morning of 4 February, or on 5 February, is already a Goat.

### Does marrying in a widow year really bring bad luck?

There's no evidence that it does. Scientists call it a superstition, and some Chinese metaphysics practitioners reject it too. But if this belief matters to your parents, respecting it is a perfectly valid choice as well.
